You don't need to watch the Inbox all day to know when a customer writes. The Inbox plays a sound or shows a browser notification for new messages, and the bell at the top of every page collects the events that concern you, such as conversations assigned to you and mentions in notes.

## While the Inbox is open

When a customer sends a new message and the Inbox is open in your browser:

- If you're looking at the page, a short chime plays.
- If the tab is in the background or another window has focus, your browser shows a notification with the contact's name and message instead. Click it to jump straight to the conversation.

The first time, your browser asks whether the site may show notifications. Allow it to get notifications in the background; if you block it, you only get the chime.

### Mute the sound

Click the bell in the Inbox header (**Mute new-message sound**). Click it again (**Unmute new-message sound**) to turn the sound back on. Muting also stops the background browser notifications. The setting is saved in this browser only, so each teammate and each device chooses for itself.

<Note>
Alerts only work while the Inbox is open in a tab. To hear about conversations when it's closed, use the bell, email or push notifications below.
</Note>

## The notifications bell

The bell at the top right of every page shows notifications for your workspace. Click it to see the latest; click a notification to open what it's about. **Mark all read** clears the unread count, and **View all activity** shows older ones.

The Inbox events in the bell are:

| Event | When you get it |
| --- | --- |
| **New conversation** | A contact starts a new chat. Sent to the team. |
| **Conversation assigned to you** | A teammate, or a rule, routes a conversation your way. |
| **You were mentioned** | A teammate @mentions you in a reply or an internal note. |
| **Daily digest** | A once-a-day summary of what is waiting for you, if you turn it on. |

The bell can also show events from other areas, such as failed automations, posts and billing.

## Choose what you're told about

Open the bell and click **Settings** to open **Notification settings**. These settings are yours alone and apply to the current workspace.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **In-app notifications** | The master switch for your bell in this workspace. Turning it off mutes every event without forgetting which ones you picked. |
| **Bell**, **Email**, **Push** per event | Choose where each event reaches you. For email, **Default** keeps the emails you get today; **Each time** and **Daily summary** are extra emails; **Off** stops them. |
| **Daily email summary** | One email a day with the notifications you set to **Daily summary**. Choose the time under **Send at**; it uses your time zone, detected from your browser. |
| **When a conversation is assigned to me** | **Email me** and, where offered, **Send me a WhatsApp message**. |
| **Daily digest** | One notification a day summarising what is waiting for you. |

Click **Save**.

### WhatsApp assignment alerts

WhatsApp alerts are sent from your workspace's default WhatsApp number using an approved assignment template, so they only arrive when both are available. You also need your own WhatsApp number in your profile; if it's missing, the settings tell you to add it in **Edit Profile**.

## Troubleshooting

**No sound plays.** Check that the Inbox isn't muted, that your computer's sound is on, and that you've clicked somewhere on the page since opening it: browsers only allow sound after you interact with a page.

**No browser notifications.** Check your browser's site settings and allow notifications for the dashboard. Also check your operating system's focus or do-not-disturb mode.

**I don't get assignment emails.** Open the bell's **Settings** and check **When a conversation is assigned to me** and the **Email** column for **Conversation assigned to you**.

**A teammate gets alerts for conversations I handle.** **New conversation** goes to everyone on the team who can see that channel. Each person can turn it off in their own settings.
