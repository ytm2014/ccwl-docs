The Inbox puts the messages from every connected channel in one list, so your team can answer them without switching apps. In the sidebar, open **Inbox**. A red count next to it shows how many conversations are unread. The page has three columns: the conversation list on the left, the open thread in the middle and the contact's details on the right. On a phone you see one column at a time; **Back to conversations** returns to the list.

## The header

Next to the **Inbox** title, a badge shows how many conversations are open. On the right of the header:

- The bell turns the new-message sound on or off (**Mute new-message sound** / **Unmute new-message sound**). See [New-message alerts](/guide/inbox/alerts).
- **AI settings**, where your platform offers it.
- The gear (**Inbox settings**), with **Conversation rules** and **Saved replies**.

## DMs and Engagements

Where your platform has both, two tabs sit at the top of the list, each with its unread count:

- **DMs**: private conversations with your contacts on every messaging channel.
- **Engagements**: public comments, mentions and reviews on your connected accounts. See [Comments, mentions and reviews](/guide/inbox/engagements).

Without the **Engagements** tab, a switch between **DMs** and **Comments** above the list lets you see comment threads in the same list.

## The conversation list

Each row shows the contact's name and channel, the last message and its time. It can also show:

| Marker | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Unread** | New messages nobody has opened yet. |
| **Draft:** | You started a reply here and haven't sent it. Drafts are kept in your browser. |
| **AI handling** | The bot sent the last message and isn't paused. |
| **Done**, **Spam**, **Hate speech** | The conversation's status or an AI flag. |
| **Assigned to** a name, or **Unassigned** | Who owns the conversation. |
| **Reachable on** a number of channels | The same person can be answered on more than one channel. |

When the list is long, more conversations load as you scroll.

### Status

The status menu above the list chooses which conversations you see: **Open**, **Done**, **Archived**, **Spam** or **All**. Open conversations need attention; done ones are handled. See [Conversation status](/guide/inbox/status).

### Quick filters

The chips under the status menu narrow the list in one click:

- **Mine**: conversations assigned to you.
- **Unassigned**: conversations nobody owns.
- **AI handling**: the bot isn't paused and sent the last message.
- **Waiting on customer**: open conversations where your side sent the last message.

### Search

Click the search icon (**Search Conversations**) and type what you are looking for. The search works together with the status, quick filters and filters you have set.

### Filters

**Filter** opens more filters. Combine as many as you like; **Clear filter** resets them.

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Channel** | **All channels** or one connected channel. |
| **Platform** | **All platforms** or one network, such as WhatsApp or Instagram. |
| **Assigned to** | **Anyone**, **Unassigned** or a teammate. |
| **Label** | **All labels** or one of your contact tags. |
| **Sentiment** | **Any mood** or one mood. Needs AI insights. |
| **AI label** | **Any AI label** or one label the AI gave the message. Needs AI insights. |
| **Unread only** | Only conversations with unread messages. |

If nothing matches, the list says *No conversations match these filters.* with a **Clear filters** button.

## AI insights

A banner above the list offers **Turn on AI insights**. With insights on, every DM and comment gets a mood and labels, so you can filter by them and spot unhappy customers or ready buyers. Insights use your workspace's AI credits; the banner shows how many are left this month. When credits run out, new messages aren't labelled until your credits renew on the 1st or you add more (**Get more AI credits**). The banner can also point you to **Set up your Brand Voice**.

## When the Inbox is empty

With no channel connected, the Inbox says *No messages yet* and offers **Connect a channel**. See [Messaging channels](/guide/channels/overview).

## Common questions

**Why don't I see a conversation a teammate can see?** Your role may limit you to some channels, or to the conversations assigned to you. Ask an admin.

**Why is there no reply box?** Your role lets you read but not reply. The thread says *Your role lets you read conversations but not reply to them.*

**Do filters stay when I come back?** Your filters and the open conversation are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view with a teammate who has the same access in <AppName />.
