Several people can work in the same Inbox without stepping on each other. Internal notes let you leave context that only your team sees, mentions pull a teammate in, and the typing line shows when someone else is already answering.

## Add an internal note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch the reply box to Note">
    Open the conversation. Above the reply box, switch from **Reply** to **Note**. The box changes colour and reads *Only your team sees notes.*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the note">
    Type the note. To bring in a teammate, type `@` and pick their name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Click **Add note**. Switch back to **Reply** before you answer the customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Notes are never sent to the customer. They're stored on the contact, so they stay with the person across all their conversations and channels.

You can also add a note from the **Notes** section of the contact panel: type in *Add a note*, then click **Add note**.

<Note>
Adding, editing and deleting notes needs the permission to edit contacts, which admins and members have unless a custom role removes it. Without it, the **Note** switch doesn't appear.
</Note>

## Edit or delete a note

In the contact panel, under **Notes**, hover a note and use **Edit note** or **Delete note**. Deleting asks you to confirm; the note is removed for everyone.

## Mention a teammate

Typing `@` in a note, or in the **Notes** section of the contact panel, opens a list of your teammates. A mentioned teammate gets a *You were mentioned* notification in the bell at the top of the page, which opens the conversation. Whether they also get an email or a push notification depends on their own notification settings. See [New-message alerts](/guide/inbox/alerts).

## See who is replying

While a teammate types in the same conversation, a line under the thread shows *Sam is typing…*, or the number of teammates typing. Use it to avoid two people answering the same customer. On your website chat, the visitor can see a typing indicator too, if you turned it on for the widget.

## Share the work

- **Assign** the conversation so it's clear who owns it. See [Assign conversations](/guide/inbox/assign).
- **Mark it done** when it's handled, so it leaves everyone's **Open** list. See [Conversation status](/guide/inbox/status).
- **Use saved replies** so the whole team answers common questions the same way. See [Saved replies](/guide/inbox/saved-replies).
- **Pause the bot** while a person handles a tricky conversation. See [Take over from the bot](/guide/inbox/bot-handover).

## Common questions

**Can the customer ever see a note?** No. Notes are never sent on any channel.

**I wrote my answer as a note by mistake.** Copy the text, delete the note from the contact panel, switch to **Reply** and send it.

**A teammate didn't get my mention.** They may have turned mentions off in their notification settings, or they may not have access to this conversation's channel.
