Saved replies are answers your team types again and again: opening hours, delivery times, how to return an order. Save them once and anyone on the team can drop them into a reply. They're shared by the whole workspace and work on every channel, in direct messages and in comment replies.

<Note>
Saved replies are not WhatsApp templates. They're ordinary text you insert while the reply window is open. To write to a WhatsApp customer after the 24-hour window, you need an approved template. See [Reply windows](/guide/inbox/reply-windows).
</Note>

## Create a saved reply

You can create saved replies in two places.

**From the Inbox settings**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Saved replies">
    In the sidebar, open **Inbox**. Click the gear (**Inbox settings**) in the header and choose **Saved replies**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reply">
    Click **New saved reply** and fill in the fields below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save reply**. Click **Done** to close the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**From the reply box**

While answering a conversation, click **Saved replies** in the toolbar and choose **New saved reply** at the bottom of the list. Enter a **Title** and the reply text, and click **Save reply**.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | A short name your team recognises in the list, for example *Shipping times*. Required. |
| **Message** | The text that goes into the reply. Required. |
| **Shortcut (optional)** | A short code shown next to the reply in the list, to help your team find it. Only in the Inbox settings list. |

## Insert a saved reply

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list">
    In the reply box under a conversation, click **Saved replies**. Each entry shows its title and the start of its text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick one">
    Click the reply. Its text goes into the reply box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and send">
    Adjust the text for this customer if needed, then send it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On the **Engagements** tab, the reply box under a comment has the same **Saved replies** button.

While a conversation's reply window is closed, the saved replies button is greyed out. See [Reply windows](/guide/inbox/reply-windows).

## Edit or delete a saved reply

- **In the Inbox settings list**, use **Edit** or **Delete** on the reply's row.
- **In the reply box list**, hover a reply and click the pencil (**Edit**) or the bin (**Delete**).

Deleting asks you to confirm: the reply is removed for everyone in the workspace.

## Who can manage saved replies

Anyone who can reply can insert saved replies. Creating, editing and deleting them needs the permission *Manage saved replies*, which admins and members have unless a custom role removes it. Without it, **New saved reply**, **Edit** and **Delete** don't appear.

## Tips for useful saved replies

- Keep titles short and specific so the right reply is easy to spot in the list.
- Write the text so it works on every channel: avoid formatting that only one network shows.
- Leave a gap where a name or order number goes, and fill it in before sending.
- Review the list now and then and delete replies that are out of date.
