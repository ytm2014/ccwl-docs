Every reply you send from the Inbox goes out on the customer's own channel: they get it in WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, as an SMS or in your website chat, not as an email from <AppName />. This page covers the reply box under the thread and everything its toolbar can add.

## Send a reply

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    In the sidebar, open **Inbox** and click the conversation. The thread shows every message, the replies to specific messages and, while a teammate is writing, a line such as *Sam is typing…*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check where the reply goes">
    If the person can be reached on more than one channel, chips above the reply box let you pick one under **Reply on**. If the same channel has more than one of your numbers or profiles, **Send from** picks the one that sends it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and send">
    Type your message. Press Enter to send, or Shift+Enter for a new line. You can also click **Send**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To answer one message in particular, hover it and click **Reply**. A *Replying to* bar appears above the reply box; **Cancel reply** removes it.

Your unsent text is kept as a draft for that conversation and channel, so you can switch conversations and come back to it. Drafts are saved in your browser, not shared with your team.

## The toolbar

The buttons under the reply box depend on the channel you're replying on and on what your workspace has set up. A button that the channel can't use doesn't appear.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Attach image / video** | Pick an image or video from your media library or upload one. |
| **Attach file** | Send a document. |
| **Record a voice note** | Record in the browser and send it as audio. Click the microphone again (**Stop recording**) to finish. Your browser asks for microphone access the first time. |
| **WhatsApp text formatting** | Wrap the selected text for WhatsApp: **Bold** (`*text*`), **Italic** (`_text_`), **Strikethrough** (`~text~`) or **Monospace** (` ```text``` `). |
| **WhatsApp template** | Pick an approved template and fill in its values. |
| **Send a form** | Attach one of your WhatsApp forms. **Remove form** takes it off again. |
| **Saved replies** | Insert an answer your team saved. See [Saved replies](/guide/inbox/saved-replies). |
| **Insert booking link** | Add the booking link of one of your appointment types. The list shows each type's length and location. **Manage meeting types** opens your appointment types. |
| **Suggest a reply with AI** | Get AI drafts written from the conversation, your Brand Voice and your AI Knowledge. |
| **Request a payment** | Send a payment link through a connected payment gateway. |

### Send a WhatsApp template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the template list">
    Click **WhatsApp template**. Use **Search templates** to find one. Only templates WhatsApp has approved, for the number you're sending from, are listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the values">
    For each variable, a **Value for** field appears above the reply box. Use the variables button in a field to insert contact details such as first name, phone or a custom field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    The reply box reads *Sending a template — fill the values above, then Send.* Click **Send**. **Remove template** cancels it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the list says there are no approved templates yet, create one first. See [WhatsApp templates](/guide/broadcasts/message-templates).

### Get an AI draft

Click **Suggest a reply with AI**. One or more drafts appear above the reply box, marked *AI draft: uses your Brand Voice and AI Knowledge*. Click a draft to put it in the reply box, then edit it before sending. If AI isn't set up for your workspace, you see *AI is not configured yet*; an admin connects an AI provider under **Integrations**. The contact panel offers the same help as **Suggested reply** (see [The contact panel](/guide/inbox/contact-panel)).

### Request a payment

Click **Request a payment**, choose the gateway, enter the amount and currency, and optionally add *What's this for?*. Click **Send payment link**. The button only appears when a payment gateway is connected under **Integrations**.

## Reply windows

Some channels only let you write freely for a while after the customer's last message. A chip above the reply box shows the time left, such as *3h 20m left*, and turns to *Window closed* when it runs out. On WhatsApp you can then only send an approved template; on other channels you wait for the customer to write again. See [Reply windows](/guide/inbox/reply-windows).

## When a reply can't be sent

If sending fails, the reason appears under the reply box and your text stays where it was: *The message could not be sent. Your draft has been kept.* A failed message in the thread is marked *· failed*. Common reasons:

- **Your role is read-only.** The reply box says *Your role lets you read conversations but not reply to them.*
- **The reply window has closed.** Send a template on WhatsApp, or wait for the customer.
- **The contact is blocked.** The thread says so and offers **Unblock contact**.
- **The channel needs reconnecting.** See [Channel troubleshooting](/guide/channels/troubleshooting).
- **The conversation has no message from the customer** on a channel that only allows replies, such as WhatsApp. The reply box explains this.

Other refusals show the platform's own reason, or *This conversation cannot be replied to right now.*
