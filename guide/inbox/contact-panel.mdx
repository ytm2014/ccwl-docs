When you open a conversation, the panel on the right shows who you're talking to and lets you update their record without leaving the Inbox. On smaller screens the panel is hidden; widen the window to see it, or use **Open contact** to go to the full profile.

## What the panel shows

The top of the panel shows the contact's name and picture, plus **Spam** or **Hate speech** badges if AI insights flagged the conversation. Below that:

| Field | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Stage** | The contact's pipeline stage, or **No stage**. You can change it here. |
| **Owner** | The teammate the conversation is assigned to, or **Unassigned**. Change it with **Assign** in the thread header. |
| **E-mail** and **Phone** | The contact's details. Depending on your role, parts may be masked. |
| **Channels** | An icon for every network the contact has written to you on. |
| **Orders** | How many orders they've placed and, where known, their total. |
| **First seen** | When they first contacted you. |

## Change the stage

Pick a stage from the **Stage** menu. The change is saved straight away and shows on the contact's profile and in the pipeline. Stages are set up in Contacts. See [Contacts](/guide/contacts/overview).

## Add and remove tags

Under **Tags**, click **Add tag** (the plus) and choose a tag. To remove one, click the cross on it (**Remove tag**). **Edit tags** opens the tag editor. Tags work as labels in the Inbox: you can filter the conversation list by them with **Filter** > **Label**.

Changing the stage and tags needs the permission to edit contacts, which admins and members have unless a custom role removes it.

## Get a suggested reply

The **Suggested reply** section writes a reply from the conversation, your Brand Voice and your AI Knowledge.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for a suggestion">
    Click **Suggest a reply**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to do with it">
    Click **Use** to put the text into the reply box, **Another** for a different suggestion, or **Try again** if it failed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and send">
    Review the text in the reply box and send it as usual.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If AI isn't set up for your workspace, the section says *AI is not set up for this workspace yet.*

## Keep notes

The **Notes** section lists your team's notes on this contact. Type in *Add a note* and click **Add note**; type `@` to mention a teammate. Hover a note to **Edit note** or **Delete note**. Notes are seen by your team only. See [Notes and teamwork](/guide/inbox/notes).

## Open the full profile

Click **Open contact** at the bottom of the panel to see the contact's full profile in Contacts, with every field, their history and their conversations on all channels. See [Contact profile](/guide/contacts/contact-profile).

## On the Engagements tab

For a comment, mention or review, the panel shows **Author details** instead: the author, their **Mood**, when they were **First seen** and the **AI insight** for the thread. If the author is already one of your contacts, a linked contact card appears with **Send a DM** and **Open contact**, and the panel also shows their stage, tags, notes and a suggested reply. If they aren't, **Add to contacts** adds them, except for X authors, who can't be added. See [Comments, mentions and reviews](/guide/inbox/engagements).
