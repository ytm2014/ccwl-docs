The **Engagements** tab of the Inbox collects the public side of your social accounts: comments on your posts, mentions of your accounts and reviews of your business. You answer them from the same place as your direct messages, and you can move a commenter into a private conversation where the platform allows it.

<Note>
Don't see the **Engagements** tab? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet, or your role doesn't let you see engagements. Without the tab, comment threads may still appear in the **DMs** list when you switch it to **Comments**.
</Note>

## Open the Engagements tab

In the sidebar, open **Inbox** and click **Engagements** at the top of the list. The tab shows its own unread count. If nothing has arrived yet, the tab says it is listening for new activity on your connected accounts.

## Narrow the list

| Control | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Status menu | **Open**, **Unread**, **Resolved** or **All**. |
| **Type** chips | Show only some kinds of thread, such as comments, mentions or reviews. |
| **Search Conversations** | Search the threads. |
| **Filter** | **Sentiment** and **AI label** (with AI insights on), **Account** (one connected account or **All accounts**), and the switches **Show spam**, **Show hidden** and **Show archived**. |

Spam is hidden by default; the list says **Spam hidden** until you turn on **Show spam**. **Clear filter** resets the filters. **Load more** fetches older threads.

## Reply to a comment, mention or review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the thread">
    Click it in the list. The thread shows the post it belongs to (**View post** opens it on the network) and the comments in order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what you're answering">
    By default you reply to the latest comment. Click **Reply** on another comment to answer that one; **Cancel reply** goes back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and send">
    Type in *Write a reply…*. Use **Saved replies** to insert a saved answer, or **Attach an image or video** where the network allows it. Then choose how to send it (below).
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Send** | Posts your reply publicly under the comment. |
| **Send & resolve** | Posts the reply and marks the thread **Resolved** in one step. |
| **Send as DM** | Sends your text privately to the commenter instead of replying in public. Greyed out when the platform doesn't allow a private reply to that comment. |

If a reply fails, it shows in the thread as *Not sent:* with your text, so you can try again.

### Reviews

A review takes one public reply. Sending a new reply replaces the one already there.

### X

Replies and mentions from X are only imported, and replies only sent, when your X account has a paid X API tier (Basic or higher). X authors can't be added to your contacts.

## Manage the thread

The thread header has:

- **Resolve** or **Reopen**.
- **Assign to**: pick a teammate or **Unassigned**.
- **Pause the bot** or **Resume the bot**, with the **AI on** / **AI off** badge, where the thread belongs to a contact.
- **Open contact**, when the author is one of your contacts.
- **More actions**: **Mark as unread**, **Hide** / **Unhide**, **Archive conversation** / **Unarchive conversation**, **Block contact** / **Unblock contact** and **Delete permanently**.

Hiding a thread takes it out of the list in your workspace. It shows again with the **Show hidden** filter. Where a comment can be liked from the dashboard, a **Like** button appears under it.

If AI insights flagged a thread as hate speech, a notice says so. Nothing is removed on the platform.

## The author panel

The panel on the right shows the author, their **Mood**, when they were **First seen** and the **AI insight** for the thread, including whether it looks like spam or hate speech. If the author is already one of your contacts, you can **Send a DM** or **Open contact**, and add notes, tags and a stage as in the [contact panel](/guide/inbox/contact-panel). If not, **Add to contacts** creates a contact for them.

## When a flow is paused on a comment

If a flow was running on a comment thread and you replied, the thread shows the same *Flow paused* card as a direct message, with **Resume**, **Skip step** and **End flow**. See [Take over from the bot](/guide/inbox/bot-handover).

## Which networks appear

Comments arrive from the networks you've connected that support them, such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Threads, YouTube, LinkedIn, Mastodon, Bluesky and Reddit. Reviews come from Google Business Profile. See [Messaging channels](/guide/channels/overview).
