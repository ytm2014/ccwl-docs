Some messaging platforms limit how long a business may write to a customer after the customer's last message. The platform enforces this, not your workspace: a message sent after the window has closed is refused. The Inbox shows you the window so you don't have to work it out yourself.

## The windows per channel

| Channel | How long you can reply freely | After the window closes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp | 24 hours after the customer's last message | Only an approved WhatsApp template can be sent. |
| Messenger and Instagram | 24 hours. Where your platform has Meta's approval, a person replying by hand gets up to 7 days. | Wait for the customer to write again. |
| TikTok | 48 hours | Wait for the customer to write again. |
| Telegram, SMS, website live chat, Mastodon, Bluesky | No window | You can always reply. |
| Comments and reviews | No window | A public reply goes to the post or review, not to a person. |

Each new message from the customer starts the window again.

## What you see in the thread

Above the reply box, a chip shows how long is left, for example *5h 12m left*, or *2d 4h left* for longer windows. Hover it to see which window applies. The chip changes colour as the deadline nears: in the last 4 hours of a 24-hour window, or the last 24 hours of a longer one.

When the window closes, the chip reads *Window closed*, and:

- **On WhatsApp**, a banner explains that the 24-hour customer-care window is closed and that only an approved template can be sent until the customer writes back. Click **Choose template** to pick one, or **Create a template** if you have none. The text and media buttons are greyed out until then. See [Reply to a conversation](/guide/inbox/reply#send-a-whatsapp-template).
- **On other channels with a window**, the reply box says the window has closed and that you can message again once the contact sends a new message.

## Conversations the customer never started

On WhatsApp, you can't send a normal reply to someone who has never written to you on that number. The reply box explains that WhatsApp only allows a reply after the customer has messaged you. Start these conversations with an approved template instead, from the Inbox or with a [broadcast](/guide/broadcasts/overview).

## Reaching customers after the window

- **WhatsApp templates** are the only way to restart a WhatsApp conversation. Meta must approve each template first. Manage them in **Salesbot** > **More** > **Message templates**.
- **Ask the customer to write back.** A booking link, a payment link or a question sent while the window is open gives them a reason to reply, which reopens it.
- **Move to a channel without a window.** If you have the customer's phone number and an SMS channel, an SMS has no window.

<Note>
The window applies to the channel you're replying on. If a contact can be reached on more than one channel, pick another one under **Reply on**: its window may still be open.
</Note>

## Common questions

**The chip shows plenty of time, but the reply was refused.** The platform may refuse a reply for another reason, such as a disconnected channel or a blocked contact. The reason appears under the reply box. See [Channel troubleshooting](/guide/channels/troubleshooting).

**Do flows follow the same windows?** Yes. Automated messages are held to the standard window of each platform; the 7-day extension on Messenger and Instagram only applies to a person replying by hand.
