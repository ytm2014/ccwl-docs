Each appointment type decides for itself when it can be booked. You set weekly hours and a few limits in the type's editor, and every place that offers times (the booking page, the **Book Meeting** step in a flow, the reschedule picker and connected AI assistants) uses the same rules.

To change them, open the type: in the sidebar, open **Salesbot** > **More** > **Appointments** > **Appointment types**, then click the type's name.

## Weekly availability

Under **Weekly availability** there is a row for each day from **Mon** to **Sun**.

- Turn a day on with its switch. It starts with 09:00–17:00.
- Change the start and end times, or click **+ Add time** to add another range on the same day, for example 09:00–12:00 and 14:00–17:00.
- Remove a range with its delete button. A day with no ranges shows **Unavailable**.

You can have up to ten ranges a day. Ranges whose end isn't after their start are dropped when you save.

## Time zone

**Time zone** is the zone your weekly hours are in. A new type starts with your browser's zone when it is in the list. Daylight saving changes are followed automatically, so 09:00 stays 09:00 local time all year.

Contacts see times in their own zone where it is known (see [Booking page](/guide/appointments/booking-page)); the hours you set don't move.

## Booking limits

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Slot interval (min)** | The gap between the start times offered. With 09:00–11:00 hours, a 60-minute meeting and a 30-minute interval, the offers are 09:00, 09:30 and 10:00. |
| **Buffer before** | Minutes kept free before each meeting. |
| **Buffer after** | Minutes kept free after each meeting, for notes or travel. |
| **Min notice (min)** | How soon a meeting can start after someone books. 120 means nobody can book a slot starting in the next two hours. |
| **Days ahead** | How far into the future people can book. |

## How free times are worked out

Free times start from your weekly hours over the next **Days ahead** days. Then these are removed:

1. times that break **Min notice (min)** or fall past **Days ahead**;
2. busy time in the connected Google Calendar for this type, including the buffers around each slot;
3. meetings already booked for this type, with their buffers.

What is left is offered to contacts. A busy check only happens when the type has a usable Google Calendar connection. If Google doesn't answer the busy check, the time is treated as free.

<Note>
Busy time is read from the calendar chosen for the type. Meetings of other appointment types are blocked only if they are on that calendar too; without a calendar, two different types can be booked at the same time.
</Note>

## Two people, one slot

The slot is checked again at the moment someone confirms. If another person took it a second earlier, the second booking is refused: the booking page says the time was just taken and asks for another, the flow step offers fresh times, and the reschedule picker shows "That time is no longer available".

## Common questions

**The booking page says no times are available.** Check that at least one day is switched on, that **Days ahead** is long enough, that **Min notice (min)** isn't longer than the booking window, and that your calendar isn't fully busy. Also check the type is **Active**.

**A time I'm free shows as unavailable.** Something in the type's calendar overlaps the slot or its buffers, or another booking of the same type does.

**I changed my hours; what about existing bookings?** They stay as booked. New hours apply to times offered from now on.
