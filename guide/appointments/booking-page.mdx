Every active appointment type has its own public booking page. Visitors don't need an account: they pick a date and time, enter their details if needed, and confirm. After booking they get a personal page where they can see, reschedule or cancel the meeting.

## Get the link

- On the **Appointment types** tab (in the sidebar, open **Salesbot** > **More** > **Appointments** > **Appointment types**), open a type's **...** menu and click **Copy booking link** or **Open booking page**.
- In the [inbox](/guide/inbox/overview), click the calendar button **Insert booking link** in the reply box. The **Booking links** list shows each active type with its length, location and address; clicking one adds the link to your reply.
- In a [flow](/guide/automation/overview), the **Book Meeting** step can send the link for you instead of offering times in the chat.

The address is made from the type's name when you create it and never changes, so links you've shared keep working after a rename. The page only works while the type is **Active**; otherwise visitors get a "not found" page.

## What visitors see

The page header shows your workspace name, the appointment type's name, its length and the kind of location: **Google Meet**, **Zoom**, **Phone call**, **In person** or **Custom location**. Your location details (phone number, address, private link) are not shown here; the contact gets them after booking.

Below the header, under **Choose a time**:

1. **Select a date**: a month calendar where days with free times can be clicked. Arrows move between months within the booking window.
2. **Select a time**: the free start times on that day.
3. A line saying **All times shown in** a time zone. This is the visitor's own time zone when their browser reports one; otherwise the contact's saved zone or the appointment type's zone.
4. **Your details**: **Your name**, **Email** and **Phone (optional)**. The visitor clicks **Confirm booking**.

If there are no free times in the booking window, the page says no times are available right now and asks the visitor to check back later.

The page appears in your platform's branding. It is hidden from search engines.

## Personal links from a flow

When the **Book Meeting** step sends the link, it is personal to that contact. The page then shows **Booking as** and the contact's name instead of the details form, and the booking is attached to the conversation. The flow continues down its booked path once the contact books.

## Plain links and new contacts

A plain link (copied, inserted from the inbox, or shared on a website) doesn't know who the visitor is, so the page asks for name and email. The booking then goes to a web contact:

- an earlier web contact with the same email that has no conversation, if there is one;
- otherwise a new contact with the source **Booking Page**.

For safety, a plain link never attaches the booking to an existing contact you chat with, even when the email matches, so a stranger typing someone's email can't make you message that person. Such a web contact has no chat channel, so confirmations and reminders can't reach them in a conversation; the booking still stands and the failed message is noted in [Activity](/guide/analytics/activity-log). Use a personal link from a flow when you want the booking tied to the chat.

If your plan's contact limit is reached, the visitor can still book. The contact is created and your team gets an alert.

## After booking: the contact's booking page

Confirming takes the visitor to their personal booking page, which says **Booking confirmed.** and lists:

- **When**, in their time zone,
- **Duration**,
- **Where**: the location, with **Join meeting** for an online link and **Join Google Meet** when there is a Meet link.

While the meeting is still upcoming, the page offers:

- **Reschedule**: shows **Pick a new time** with the free times. The contact picks one and clicks **Confirm new time**, or **Keep current time** to go back.
- **Cancel meeting**: asks for confirmation, then cancels and shows that the meeting has been cancelled.

Once the meeting has started, the page only says it has passed; contacts can't move or cancel it then. A cancelled meeting shows **This meeting was cancelled.**

The same page is linked from the `{{meeting.reschedule_url}}` and `{{meeting.cancel_url}}` variables you can put in your messages. Anyone with the link can manage the booking, so treat it like a private link. See [Confirmations and reminders](/guide/appointments/messages-and-reminders).

## Messages visitors may see

| Message | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Sorry, that time was just taken — please choose another. | Someone else booked the slot first. |
| We couldn't complete the booking. Please try another time. | The booking couldn't be created, for example the calendar or Zoom refused it. |
| Please enter your name, a valid email and pick a time. | A field was missing or the email wasn't valid. |
| That time was just taken — please pick another. | The new time chosen when rescheduling was taken. |
| Could not reschedule. Please try again. | The reschedule failed or the meeting had already started. |
