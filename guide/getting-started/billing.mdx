Each workspace has its own subscription. Admins manage it from workspace settings: open **Settings → Subscription** in the sidebar, or **Subscription** in the workspace menu.

<Note>
Your platform may manage billing for you. In that case these pages, or some of them, are not shown, and you should contact your platform about your plan.
</Note>

## Subscription

The **Subscription** page shows your **Current plan**, its status and, during a trial or after cancelling, the date it changes. From here you can:

- **Subscribe** or **Upgrade plan** if you have no paid plan yet.
- **Change subscription plan** to move to another plan.
- **Resume** a cancelled subscription before it ends.
- **Downgrade to free plan**, where your platform has a free plan.
- **Update payment method**, under **Payment Method**.
- **Cancel subscription**.

If a payment fails, the page says **Your last payment failed**. Publishing is paused until the payment goes through; update your payment method to resume it.

### Choose or change a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plans">
    On **Subscription**, click **Subscribe**, **Upgrade plan** or **Change subscription plan**. The **Upgrade** button in the top bar opens the same page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Choose monthly or yearly billing and pick a plan. Use **Compare plans** to see them side by side. If you have a coupon, click **Have a coupon?** and enter it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Subscribe**, or **Start trial** where the plan has a free trial. When you switch plans, the page explains how the change is charged before you confirm with **Switch plan**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your platform may also offer **Whitelabel plans**, which make you a reseller agency with your own branded console, sub-accounts for your customers and your own billing.

### Cancel your subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    On **Subscription**, under **Cancel subscription**, click **Cancel subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us why">
    Choose a reason and, if you like, add a comment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Cancel subscription**, or **Keep subscription** to change your mind.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period. After that, the workspace either moves to the free plan or, without one, stops publishing and is locked. Your accounts, posts and media are kept.

## Usage

**Usage** shows how much of each plan resource the workspace is using, such as posts, accounts, members and AI credits, against your plan's limits.

## Receipts

**Receipts** lists your invoices with the **Invoice Number**, **Billing date** and **Amount**. Click **Download** to save one.

## Add-ons

Where your plan offers them, **Add-ons** lets you add more to your plan without changing it, such as more sales inbox profiles, the Flow Builder, Broadcasts, Team roles and integrations.

- Switch between **Monthly** and **Yearly** prices.
- Click **Buy** on an add-on, or **Switch to this** to change to another tier.
- Your current add-ons are listed under **Your add-ons**. Click **Cancel add-on** to stop one at the end of the paid period, or **Keep add-on** to undo that.

Every plan includes the inbox on all your connected profiles; add-ons add the sales inbox's automations and tools. A feature your plan doesn't include yet may show a lock with a button that shows you how to get it. Some workspaces also have an **Inbox storage** page here, showing storage used by the inbox.

## Trials, locked workspaces and notices

- During a free trial, the top bar shows the days left, for example **Trial · 5 days left**, and a notice may remind you to choose a plan to keep your scheduled posts publishing.
- When a trial ends, the workspace shows **Your free trial has ended**. Click **Choose a plan** or **Continue with** your plan to pick up where you left off. Your accounts, posts and media are kept.
- A workspace is **Locked** when its subscription has ended or is paused, or when the administrator locked it. Click **Reactivate subscription** to start again, or contact support if the administrator locked it.
