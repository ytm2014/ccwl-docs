Your profile belongs to you, not to a workspace: the same name, password and preferences apply in every workspace you belong to. Open it from your user menu (your avatar in the top-right corner) by clicking **My Settings**.

The profile pages have their own sidebar: **My Settings**, **Workspaces**, and, where your platform offers them, **API**, **Integrations** and **Connected apps**. Your platform may also add **Theme**.

## Profile Information

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Avatar** | Your picture, shown in the user menu and to your team. |
| **Name** | Your name as your team sees it. |
| **Email** | The address you sign in with. |

Click **Save**.

If your workspace uses assignment alerts in the sales inbox, an **Assignment alerts** panel lets you enter your **WhatsApp number**, with its country code, for alerts about conversations assigned to you. Turn the alerts on in each workspace's notification settings.

## My Preferences

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Language** | The language of the dashboard. |
| **Timezone** | The time zone used for scheduling and dates. |
| **Time format** | 12-hour or 24-hour clock. |
| **First day of week** | **Sunday** or **Monday**, for the calendar. |
| **Appearance** | **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your device's setting. |

Click **Save**. You can also switch dark mode on and off from your user menu.

<Tip>
Check your **Timezone** before you schedule posts, so they go out at the time you expect.
</Tip>

## Two Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication adds a code from your phone to your password when you sign in. This panel only appears if your platform offers it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install an authentication app">
    Install a TOTP app, such as Google Authenticator, on your phone or computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Click **Enable**. Scan the QR code with your app, or enter **Your secret key** in it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Type the **Code** your app shows and click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your recovery codes">
    Store the recovery codes in a secure password manager. They let you back into your account if you lose your device. You can **Show recovery codes** or **Regenerate recovery codes** later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To turn it off, click **Disable**.

## Update Password

Enter your **Current password**, then your **New password** twice (**Confirm new password**), and click **Save**. Use a long, random password.

## Delete Account

Where your platform allows it, **Delete Account** removes your account and all its data permanently, including the data of any workspace you own. You are asked to confirm first.

<Warning>
Deleting your account cannot be undone. If you own workspaces other people use, think about whether they still need them first.
</Warning>

## API keys and connected apps

These are mainly for developers and AI assistants:

- **API** lists your personal keys, which call the API and the AI assistant (MCP) server as you. Click **Create API key** to make one. See [Authentication](/authentication).
- **Integrations** connects AI tools and apps to your account. See [Connect your app](/mcp/connect).
- **Connected apps** lists apps you allowed to sign in to your account, such as ChatGPT or Claude. Click **Disconnect** to remove one's access straight away.
