Each person in a workspace has a role that decides what they can do there. Admins invite people and change roles on the **Team** page. Where the sales inbox is part of your workspace, admins can also create **team roles**: named sets of permissions that sit between a member and an admin.

## The three workspace roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Can access and edit everything, including the workspace's configuration: social accounts, team, subscription and settings. |
| **Member** | Can access and edit everything except the configuration. Members create and schedule posts and use the inbox if they have inbox access. |
| **Viewer** | Can access everything except the configuration, but cannot edit or schedule posts. |

The person who created the workspace is its **Owner**. The owner is always an admin, cannot be removed, and is the only one who can delete the workspace.

Some screens also depend on extra access an admin gives each member:

| Option | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| **Can Approve** | Approve posts that need approval before they are published. |
| **Inbox Access** | Open the inbox and work on conversations. |
| **Bulk Posts Access** | Use **Bulk Posts**. |
| **RSS Feeds Access** | Use **RSS Campaigns**. |

Admins always have all of these.

## Open the Team page

The Team page is for admins. Open **Settings → Team Members** in the sidebar, or go to workspace settings and click **Team**. It lists everyone in the workspace with their **Name**, **Email**, **Role**, whether they **Can approve**, and the date they **Joined At**. People who have not accepted their invitation yet show as **Pending**.

## Invite a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the invitation">
    On the Team page, click **Invite member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email address">
    Type the address the invitation should go to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Pick **Admin**, **Member** or **Viewer**, then tick the access options they need, such as **Can Approve** or **Inbox Access**. If your workspace uses team roles, you can pick a role here instead; a role replaces the individual options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Click **Invite**. The person appears as **Pending** until they accept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To withdraw an invitation, open the menu at the end of its row and click **Cancel**, then confirm.

## Change a role or remove someone

Open the menu at the end of a person's row:

- **Edit role** opens a dialog with the same choices as the invitation. Make your changes and click **Update**.
- **Remove** takes them out of the workspace after you confirm. Their account is not deleted; they just lose access to this workspace.

You cannot change your own role or the owner's.

## Team roles for finer permissions

Team roles let you decide exactly what someone can do in the sales inbox and related areas, for example a "Receptionist" who can reply to conversations but not delete contacts. A role also decides post approval, inbox, bulk posts, RSS and which channels the person works on. Workspace admins always have every permission, whatever role they hold.

Open team roles from the Team page: click the **Roles** tab, or the **Team Roles** button beside **Invite member**. Members and viewers who are allowed to manage roles find **Team Roles** in their user menu.

Don't see team roles? Your plan or your platform doesn't include them yet.

### Create a role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new role">
    Click **New role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and describe it">
    Enter a **Name** and, if you like, a **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose its permissions">
    Under **Permissions**, permissions are grouped by area, such as **Inbox**, **Contacts**, **Automations**, **Broadcasts**, **Reports**, **Channels**, **Settings**, **Posting** and **Team**. Tick an area's name to select all its permissions, or tick them one by one. Areas your workspace doesn't have are not listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Limit its channels (optional)">
    Under **Channel access**, choose **All channels (including ones connected later)** or **Only these channels**, then tick the channels. Members with the role only see and manage posts, analytics and conversations for those channels. Only a workspace admin can change a role's channels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The roles list shows each role's **Name**, how many **Permissions** it has, its channel limit, and how many **Members** hold it. Use **Edit** or **Delete** on a row.

### Give someone a role

On the Team page, open the person's menu, click **Edit role**, and pick the role in the team role list. Without a role, a member keeps their own **Inbox Access**, **Bulk Posts Access** and **RSS Feeds Access** settings.

<Note>
When you delete a role, its members go back to the access stored for them before.
</Note>
