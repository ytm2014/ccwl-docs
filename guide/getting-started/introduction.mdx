This guide explains how to use each part of the dashboard: publishing to your social networks, answering customers in one inbox, automating conversations, and building link-in-bio pages. It is written for everyone who works in a workspace, from the owner who sets it up to the teammate who only reviews posts. If you build integrations, the developer pages start at [Quickstart](/quickstart).

## What you can do

| Area | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Content** | Write a post once and publish or schedule it to several social accounts. Plan it on the **Calendar**, keep images and videos in the **Media Library**, reuse **Templates**, and create many posts at once with **Bulk Posts** or from a feed with **RSS Campaigns**. |
| **Inbox** | Read and answer messages from your connected channels in one place, such as WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Telegram, SMS and the live chat on your website. Conversations show an unread count in the sidebar. |
| **Salesbot** | The sales side of the inbox: **Contacts**, automated **Flows**, **Broadcasts** to a list of contacts, and more tools under **More**. |
| **Bio Links** | Build link-in-bio **Pages**, short **Links** and **QR Codes**, and see how they perform. |
| **Analytics** | See how your accounts and posts perform. |
| **Integrations** | Connect other tools and AI assistants to your workspace. |

Everything happens inside a **workspace**: a shared space with its own social accounts, posts, inbox and team. You can belong to several workspaces and switch between them at any time.

## Who it is for

- **Workspace owners and admins** connect social accounts, invite the team, manage the subscription and change workspace settings.
- **Members** create and schedule posts, work in the inbox and build pages, without access to the configuration.
- **Viewers** can look around and follow what is planned, but cannot edit or schedule posts.

See [Team members and roles](/guide/getting-started/team-and-roles) for exactly what each role can do.

<Note>
Some areas depend on your plan and on what your platform offers, so your sidebar may not show everything on this page. If an area is missing, your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet.
</Note>

## A first session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and check your workspace">
    Sign in to <AppName />. The workspace you are in is named at the top of the sidebar, next to the logo. See [Workspaces](/guide/getting-started/workspaces).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your accounts">
    If you are an admin, open **Accounts** in the sidebar and click **Connect account**. See [Social accounts](/guide/getting-started/social-accounts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Open **Settings → Team Members** and click **Invite member**. See [Team members and roles](/guide/getting-started/team-and-roles).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first post">
    Click **New post** at the top of the sidebar. See [Create a post](/guide/publishing/create-a-post).
  </Step>
</Steps>
