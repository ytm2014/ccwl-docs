The **Accounts** page holds every social account and messaging channel connected to your workspace. Posts are published to these accounts, and messages from them arrive in your inbox. Only workspace **Admins** can open this page; in the sidebar, click **Accounts**.

## Connect an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    On **Social Accounts**, click **Connect account**. If no account is connected yet, the page shows the same button in the middle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the network">
    Click the tile of the network. Each tile says what you connect, such as a page or a profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and allow access">
    For most networks you are sent to the network to sign in and allow access. Some networks, such as Bluesky or the Telegram bot, ask for details in a form instead; Mastodon and Pixelfed ask you to choose your server, or **Custom** to enter its address, then click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to connect">
    If your login manages several pages or profiles, **Account Entities** lists them. Under **Choose entity**, select the ones you want and click **Finish Connection**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The account appears as a card on the page and is ready for posts, the calendar and analytics.

### Networks you can connect

The dialog only shows the networks your platform has set up, in this order:

- Facebook Page
- Instagram (with Facebook) and Instagram
- WhatsApp (a business number)
- Threads and TikTok
- LinkedIn Page and LinkedIn Profile
- YouTube and Google Business Profile
- X and Pinterest
- Bluesky, Mastodon and Pixelfed
- Reddit and Tumblr
- Telegram bot (Salesbot) and Telegram channel (posting)
- VKontakte
- SMS and Live chat

WhatsApp, the Telegram bot, SMS and Live chat are messaging channels for your inbox. Some of them appear only if your workspace includes the sales inbox and your role lets you manage channels.

## Find an account

Use **Search accounts** to find an account by name. The filter next to it narrows the list by **Account groups**, **Platforms**, or accounts that **Needs re-authentication**.

## Account actions

Open the menu in the corner of an account's card:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **New post** | Opens the post editor with this account selected. |
| **Queue** | Shows the account's queued posts. |
| **Posting Schedule** | Opens the posting schedule for this account. |
| **Analytics** | Opens the account's figures, where the network supports it. |
| **View profile** | Opens the account on the network, in a new tab. |
| **Update** | Refreshes the account's details from the network. |
| **Re-authenticate** | Signs in to the network again to renew the connection. |
| **Edit suffix** | Adds a suffix to the account's name. |
| **Manage Number** | For a WhatsApp number, opens the page where you manage it. |
| **Copy ID** / **Copy UUID** | Copies the account's IDs for developers. Shown where your platform offers API access. |
| **Delete** | Removes the account from the workspace, after you confirm. |

Channels without a social account, such as Live chat or SMS, have their own cards at the bottom of the page with **Edit name**, **Customize widget** (for Live chat) and **Disconnect**. Disconnecting removes the connection; past conversations stay in your inbox.

## Reconnect an account

When a network stops accepting the connection, for example after a password change, the card is marked **Unauthorized** and a notice says posts cannot be published to it until you re-authenticate. Click **Re-authenticate** on the card or in its menu, and sign in to the network again.

Some cards show a **Reconnect** button instead, for example to publish with TikTok Business, or a **Move now** button for an Instagram account that uses the old connection. Follow the button to keep publishing.

## Group accounts

Account groups let you select several accounts in one click when you create a post. Click **Manage groups** to see your **Account groups**, and **New group** to add one. Deleting a group keeps the accounts themselves. A card shows the groups its account belongs to.

## Invite a client to connect their accounts

If you manage accounts for clients, they can connect their own accounts to your workspace without getting access to it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open client invites">
    On **Social Accounts**, click **Invite Client to Add Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the link">
    Click **Generate Invite**. Switch on **Activate Link**, choose the **Link expiration** in days, and optionally **Set a password**, which the client must enter to open the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Click **Copy Link** under **Link to share**, then **Save**. Send the link to your client.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Client Invite** page lists your links and whether each is **Active**. Use **Copy Link** or **Delete Link** on a row. Accounts your client connects appear on your **Social Accounts** page.
