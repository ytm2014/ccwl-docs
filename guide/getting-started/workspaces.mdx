A workspace holds everything for one brand, client or team: its social accounts, posts, media, inbox, bio pages and team members. Each workspace has its own subscription and its own members, and nothing is shared between workspaces. You can belong to several, with a different role in each.

## Switch workspaces

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar, next to the logo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a workspace">
    Under **Switch workspaces**, click the one you want. If you belong to more than eight, type part of its name in the search box first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The workspace opens on its start page. You can also see every workspace you belong to on **My Settings → Workspaces** (from your user menu), with your **Role** in each and the date you joined. Click a name to open it.

## Create a workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new workspace">
    Open the workspace menu and click **Create workspace**, or go to **My Settings → Workspaces** and click **Create Workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Name** and click **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You become the workspace's owner. Then connect its accounts, invite your team and, if needed, choose a plan under **Subscription** (see [Billing and subscription](/guide/getting-started/billing)).

<Note>
Don't see **Create workspace**? Your platform may limit how many workspaces you can own, or create workspaces for you.
</Note>

## Workspace settings

Workspace settings are for admins. Open them from **Settings → Workspace** in the sidebar, or **Settings** in the workspace menu. A menu on the left of the settings pages groups them:

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| **Workspace** | **Settings**, **Team**, and **Services** where offered |
| **Billing** | **Subscription**, **Usage**, **Receipts**, and **Add-ons** where offered |
| **AI** | **Brand Voice** and **AI Knowledge**, where offered |
| **Security** | **Delete workspace**, for the owner only |

### Settings

Under **Details**:

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Avatar** | The workspace's picture, shown in the workspace menu and lists. Upload or remove it. |
| **Name** | The workspace's name. |

Click **Save**. Where your platform offers API access, **Usage in API** shows the workspace's ID, which developers need; click it to copy it. Some workspaces also show **Custom fields** here, the extra fields you can store on contacts in the sales inbox.

### Team

Invite people and set their roles. See [Team members and roles](/guide/getting-started/team-and-roles).

### Services

**Third Party Services** stores the workspace's own credentials for an outside service, such as X. It only appears when your platform lets workspaces use their own credentials. If X is not set up, **Accounts** shows a warning with a **Configure services** button that brings you here.

### Billing and AI

See [Billing and subscription](/guide/getting-started/billing) for **Subscription**, **Usage**, **Receipts** and **Add-ons**. **Brand Voice** and **AI Knowledge** shape how AI writes for this workspace.

## Delete a workspace

Only the workspace's owner can delete it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security">
    In workspace settings, under **Security**, click **Delete workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete workspace**, type the workspace's name in the box, and click **Delete workspace** again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Deleting a workspace deletes all its data. If the workspace has a subscription, it is cancelled immediately and you won't be charged again.
</Warning>

## Leave a workspace

If you no longer need a workspace someone else owns, go to **My Settings → Workspaces** and click the leave icon on its row, then confirm. You lose access to everything in it. The owner cannot leave their own workspace.
