Every page of a workspace has the same frame: the sidebar on the left, a bar across the top with the page title, and your user menu in the top-right corner. This page walks through each part. What you see depends on your role, your plan and what your platform offers, so some items below may be missing for you.

## The sidebar

At the top of the sidebar are the logo and the name of the workspace you are in. Click the workspace name to open the workspace menu (see [Switch and manage workspaces](#the-workspace-menu)).

Below it, the **New post** button opens the post editor. It is shown to admins and members, not to viewers.

The sidebar then lists the areas of the workspace:

| Item | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your workspace's start page. |
| **Analytics** | Figures for your accounts and posts. |
| **Inbox** | Your conversations, with a count of unread ones beside the label. |
| **Content** | **Posts**, which folds open to **All Posts**, **Queue**, **Templates**, **Bulk Posts** and **RSS Campaigns**; then **Calendar** and **Media Library**. |
| **Engagement & Automation** | **Salesbot**, which folds open to **Overview**, **Contacts**, **Flows**, **Broadcasts** and **More**; and **Bio Links**, which folds open to **Pages**, **Links**, **QR Codes** and **More**. |
| **Settings** | Workspace settings, for admins: **Workspace**, **Team Members**, **Subscription**, and under AI, **Brand Voice** and **AI Knowledge** where offered. |
| **Accounts** | Your connected social accounts and channels. Admins only. |
| **Integrations** | Apps and tools connected to the workspace. |
| **Help Center** | Your platform's help site, in a new tab. Your platform may give it another name, or not show it. |

Groups that fold open, such as **Posts**, **Salesbot** and **Bio Links**, work like an accordion: one is open at a time, and the group that holds the page you are on opens by itself. The sidebar remembers which group you left open.

A few items depend on your role:

- **Queue** is shown to admins.
- **Templates** is shown to admins and members.
- **Bulk Posts** and **RSS Campaigns** need the matching access, which an admin gives on the Team page or through a team role.
- Items in **Salesbot** follow your team role's permissions. See [Team members and roles](/guide/account/team-members).

<Note>
If you arrived from a partner site, you may also see a link back to it at the top of the sidebar.
</Note>

Don't see **Salesbot**, **Bio Links** or **Inbox**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet.

## The workspace menu

Click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar. The menu shows:

- The workspace you are in. Admins also see **Settings** and **Subscription** for it here.
- **Switch workspaces**: the other workspaces you belong to. Click one to open it. With more than eight, a search box appears so you can type part of the name.
- **Create workspace**, if you are allowed to create one.

See [Workspaces](/guide/getting-started/workspaces) for more.

## The top bar

The bar across the top shows the title of the page you are on, and on some pages tabs or buttons for that page. On the right, depending on your workspace, you may see:

- How many days are left in a free trial, for example **Trial · 5 days left**.
- An **Upgrade** button, which takes you to the plans.
- The notifications bell.
- Your avatar, which opens your user menu.

### Notifications

Click the bell to open **Notifications**, a list of recent activity in the workspace, newest first. From the panel you can:

- Click an item to open what it is about.
- Click **Mark all read** to clear the unread count.
- Click **View all activity** to open the full activity list, where your role allows it.
- Click **Settings** to choose which notifications you receive.

When there is nothing new, the panel says **Nothing new. You are all caught up.**

## Your user menu

Click your avatar in the top-right corner. The menu shows your name and email address, then:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **My Settings** | Your name, email address, password, language, time zone and two-factor authentication. See [Your profile and security](/guide/account/profile). |
| **API** | Your personal API keys, for developers. Shown when your platform offers API access. |
| **Integrations** | Connect AI tools and apps to your account. Shown with **API**. |
| **Team Members** | The workspace's Team page. Shown to admins in some workspaces. |
| **Team Roles** | The custom roles page, for members and viewers whose role lets them manage roles. |
| **Dark mode** | Switches between light and dark. The choice is saved to your profile. |
| **Sign Out** | Signs you out. |

If you also run a reseller agency on the platform, you may see **Billing** and **Agency** here too. Platform administrators see links to their consoles at the top of the menu.

<Tip>
For the full choice of appearance, including following your device's setting, use **Appearance** on **My Settings**.
</Tip>
