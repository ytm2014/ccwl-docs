The **Meta Ads** card on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page connects your business ad account. Your workspace already knows which conversations started from an ad; with Meta Ads connected, it also brings in what those ads cost and how they were delivered, so the Ads report can show spend, cost per conversation and return on ad spend next to the conversations and revenue.

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission. The card is under the **Productivity** tab.

<Note>
Does the card say **Not available yet** (the page reads "Meta Ads is not available yet.") or **Not included in your plan**? Your platform or your plan doesn't include it yet.
</Note>

## Connect Meta Ads

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect with Meta">
    In the sidebar, open **Integrations** > **Meta Ads** and click **Connect with Meta**. Sign in to Meta and allow access to your business ad account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the ad account">
    Back on the page, pick your account under **Ad account**. Each option shows the account's name, ID and currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Click **Save ad account**. The button reads **Verifying account…** while Meta confirms you can read that account; the page then says "Ad account selected."
  </Step>
</Steps>

Selecting an account starts reporting. Paid messaging (sending paid marketing messages through Meta) needs separate setup and isn't turned on by this card.

## What the page shows

| Item | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Account currency** | The currency Meta reports spend in, or **Not provided**. |
| **Last complete refresh** | When delivery and spend were last fetched in full, or **Not synced yet**. |
| A warning | "The last refresh was incomplete. Check the connection and retry." when the last fetch failed part-way. |

Use **Test connection** at the top to check the grant and the selected account still work; it answers "Meta verified the ad account grant." To change accounts or renew access, click **Reconnect with Meta**.

## What it adds to the Ads report

The Ads report (in the sales inbox reports) lists conversations, leads, recorded revenue and first-ad contacts per ad, whether or not Meta Ads is connected. Connecting adds Meta's measurements:

- **Spend**, **Impressions** and **Clicks** per ad, with when each was measured.
- **Cost / conversation** and **Cost per lead**.
- **ROAS** (return on ad spend), withheld when currencies are mixed, measurements are missing or refunds are unresolved. Currencies are never converted.

You can export the report as CSV or PDF. See [Ad attribution report](/guide/analytics/ad-attribution).

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** at the top of the page. The grant and the selected account are removed and the page confirms "Meta Ads disconnected." Spend and delivery stop refreshing; your conversation and revenue history stays in the report.

If the page says "Meta Ads is not enabled for this workspace. Your saved referral history remains available in reports.", your plan or platform has turned the feature off; your past data is kept.

## Troubleshooting

- **"That account could not be verified on the current Meta grant. Reconnect or refresh the account list."** Your Meta sign-in can't read that ad account. Click **Reconnect with Meta** and make sure you allow access to it.
- **"The current grant or selected ad account could not be verified. Reconnect Meta Ads."** Meta no longer accepts the saved access. Click **Reconnect with Meta**.
- **The report says the account is awaiting its first synchronization.** Wait for the first refresh after you saved the account.
- **The card shows Error.** Meta rejected the saved grant. Click **Reconnect with Meta**.
