The **DeepSeek** card on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page connects your own DeepSeek account. Your flows' **AI Reply** steps can then run on DeepSeek models, billed to your DeepSeek account. DeepSeek supports **Functions** and **CRM contact tools**. It doesn't offer **File search**, **Web search** or **Reasoning effort**, so the page has no **Vector stores** tab. For how your own key fits with the built-in AI, see [AI providers](/guide/integrations/ai-providers).

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission. The card is under the **AI** tab.

<Note>
Don't see **DeepSeek**, or does connecting say your plan uses the built-in AI? Your plan or your platform doesn't include your own AI keys.
</Note>

## Connect DeepSeek

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a key at DeepSeek">
    Sign in to the DeepSeek platform at platform.deepseek.com. Under **API keys**, create a key and copy it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it">
    In the sidebar, open **Integrations** > **DeepSeek**. Under **API credentials**, paste the key into **API key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a proxy (optional)">
    Fill in **Base URL (optional)** only if you reach DeepSeek through your own proxy. It must be a public `https://` address. Leave it empty to use DeepSeek directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. The key is checked with DeepSeek first; the page then confirms "DeepSeek connected."
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change the key later, paste the new one and click **Save & reconnect**.

## Models

The **Capabilities** panel lists the models under **Models:** and the default under **Default:**. Today these are `deepseek-flash` (the default) and `deepseek-v4-pro`. The list on the page is always the current one.

## What it unlocks

- DeepSeek models in the **AI Reply** step of your [flows](/guide/automation/overview).
- Function calling, including the CRM contact tools, inside AI replies.
- With your own key connected, AI replies run on it first and don't use your plan's monthly AI allowance.

DeepSeek can't read images, search the web or search your documents. For those, connect [OpenAI](/guide/integrations/openai), [Claude](/guide/integrations/claude) or [Google Gemini](/guide/integrations/gemini) as well and pick that model in the steps that need it.

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** at the top of the page. The key is removed, and **AI Reply** steps fall back to another connected provider or the built-in AI, where your plan has it.

## Troubleshooting

- **"That base URL is not reachable publicly (…)."** The proxy address must start with `https://` and be reachable from the internet. Fix it or leave the field empty.
- **"Could not reach the DeepSeek API: …"** DeepSeek refused the key, or your proxy didn't answer. Check the key and your DeepSeek balance.
- **The card shows Error.** DeepSeek rejected the saved key during use. Create a new key and click **Save & reconnect**.
