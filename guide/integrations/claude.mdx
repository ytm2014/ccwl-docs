The **Claude** card on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page connects your own Anthropic account. Your flows' **AI Reply** steps can then run on Claude models, billed to your Anthropic account. Claude supports **Web search**, **Functions** and **CRM contact tools**. It doesn't offer **File search** or **Reasoning effort**, so the page has no **Vector stores** tab. For how your own key fits with the built-in AI, see [AI providers](/guide/integrations/ai-providers).

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission. The card is under the **AI** tab.

<Note>
Don't see **Claude**, or does connecting say your plan uses the built-in AI? Your plan or your platform doesn't include your own AI keys.
</Note>

## Connect Claude

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a key at Anthropic">
    Open the Anthropic Console at console.anthropic.com. Under **Settings → API keys**, create a key and copy it. It starts with `sk-ant-`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it">
    In the sidebar, open **Integrations** > **Claude**. Under **API credentials**, paste the key into **API key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. The key is checked with Anthropic first; the page then confirms the connection and the card shows **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change the key later, paste the new one and click **Save & reconnect**.

## What it unlocks

- Claude models in the **AI Reply** step of your [flows](/guide/automation/overview).
- Web search and function calling (including the CRM contact tools) inside AI replies.
- With your own key connected, AI replies run on it first and don't use your plan's monthly AI allowance.

If you want the AI to answer from your own documents, connect [OpenAI](/guide/integrations/openai) or [Google Gemini](/guide/integrations/gemini) as well: only they can search uploaded files.

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** at the top of the page. The key is removed, and **AI Reply** steps fall back to another connected provider or the built-in AI, where your plan has it.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Could not reach the Claude (Anthropic) API: …"** Anthropic refused the key or couldn't be reached. Check the key is active and your Anthropic account has credit, then try again.
- **The card shows Error.** Anthropic rejected the saved key during use. Create a new key and click **Save & reconnect**.
- **"This plan uses the built-in AI."** Your plan doesn't allow own keys.
