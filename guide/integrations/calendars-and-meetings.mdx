Two cards on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page help you take bookings in conversations: **Google Calendar** creates calendar events with a Google Meet link and checks your availability, and **Zoom** creates Zoom meetings with join links. Both are under the **Productivity** tab.

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission.

<Note>
If a card says **Not available yet**, your platform hasn't set up sign-in for that service. If it says **Not included in your plan**, your plan doesn't include it.
</Note>

## Google Calendar

With Google Calendar connected, the **Book Meeting** step in your flows can create calendar events with a Google Meet link, read your availability, and send confirmations and reminders.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Open **Integrations** > **Google Calendar** and click **Sign in with Google**. Allow calendar access. The page then shows **Connected as** with your Google account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the booking calendar">
    Under **Booking calendar**, pick the calendar to use. New meetings are created there, and its free/busy time is checked when slots are offered. Click **Refresh** if a calendar you just created isn't listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start taking bookings">
    Add a **Book Meeting** step to a flow, or set up a meeting type under **Meetings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A meeting type can use a different calendar from the booking calendar you choose here.

## Zoom

With Zoom connected, new bookings get a Zoom meeting and its join link automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Zoom">
    Open **Integrations** > **Zoom** and click **Sign in with Zoom**. Approve access for the Zoom account that should host the meetings. The page then shows **Connected as** with that account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Zoom for a meeting type">
    Set a meeting type's location to **Zoom**, so new bookings of that type get a join link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Rescheduling a booking moves its Zoom meeting, and cancelling a booking deletes it.

Clicking **Disconnect** revokes access at Zoom and stops new links being created.
