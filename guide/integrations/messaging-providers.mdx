These cards on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page connect messaging services: an SMS gateway (**Twilio**, **Plivo** or **Custom HTTP gateway**, under the **SMS** tab) and **Slack** for notifications (under **Productivity**).

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission.

<Note>
Don't see a card? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet.
</Note>

## SMS gateways

Connecting a gateway sets up a workspace-wide SMS channel for broadcasts, the inbox and the **Send SMS** flow step.

| Gateway | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Twilio** | **Account SID**, **Auth token**, **From number / Messaging Service SID** |
| **Plivo** | **Auth ID**, **Auth token**, **Sender number** |
| **Custom HTTP gateway** | **Send URL** (with `{to}`, `{text}` and `{from}` placeholders), **HTTP method** (GET or POST), **Authorization header value** (optional), **Sender ID** (optional) |

### Connect a gateway

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your credentials">
    For Twilio, copy the Account SID and Auth Token from **Account → API keys & tokens** in the Twilio Console. For Plivo, copy the Auth ID and Auth Token from **Account → API authentication**. For a custom gateway, build the send address your provider documents, using the placeholders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste them and connect">
    Open the gateway's card, fill in the fields and click **Connect**. Keys are encrypted and never sent back to your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the inbound webhook">
    For Twilio and Plivo, the **Inbound & delivery webhook** panel shows a URL. Click **Copy** and set it for your number in the gateway's dashboard (in Twilio, **Phone Numbers → Active numbers**; in Plivo, **Phone Numbers → Manage numbers**). Replies then appear in the inbox and delivery reports update each message's status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The panel also says whether this gateway supports inbound SMS and delivery reports. A custom gateway only sends: it has no inbound replies or delivery reports.

Once connected, the **SMS** panel shows the **Sender** and how many messages were **Sent** and **Received**.

### Delivery settings

Where your platform offers them, **Delivery settings** has two options. Click **Save settings** after changing them.

- **Add an opt-out line to SMS broadcasts** adds a line (which you can edit) on a new line of each person's first SMS broadcast from this number, then at most once every 30 days, unless the message already mentions STOP. It is added to broadcasts only, never to replies or **Send SMS** steps.
- **Check failed numbers with a line type lookup** appears for gateways that offer it. When the gateway reports an invalid number, one lookup per number (billed by the gateway, remembered for 30 days) tells a landline from a typo, and landlines are then skipped.

## Slack

Slack posts a message in a channel of your choice whenever selected events happen in your workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an incoming webhook in Slack">
    At api.slack.com/apps, create or pick an app for your Slack workspace and turn on **Incoming Webhooks**. Click **Add New Webhook to Workspace** and choose the channel to post in. Copy the webhook URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the URL">
    Open **Integrations** > **Slack** and paste it into **Incoming webhook URL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the channel scope">
    Under **Channel scope**, keep all channels or pick one messaging channel, so only events from that channel notify Slack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick events">
    Under **Events to notify**, tick the events you want. Events a channel can't report are greyed out, and some are marked **Limited**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect** (later, **Save Slack**). Failed deliveries are retried up to 3 times.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To check it works, pick an **Event** under **Send a test notification** and click **Send test**. A sample message is posted to your Slack channel and appears in the **Recent delivery log**.

The saved webhook URL is never shown again; leave the field blank to keep it.
