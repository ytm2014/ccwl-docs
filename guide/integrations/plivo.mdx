The **Plivo** card on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page connects your Plivo account as your workspace's SMS gateway. You can send and receive SMS, Plivo's delivery reports update each message's status, and failed numbers can be checked with a line type lookup. For what the SMS channel unlocks (inbox, broadcasts, the **Send SMS** step, delivery settings), see [SMS gateways](/guide/integrations/sms-gateways).

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission. The card is under the **SMS** tab.

<Note>
Don't see **Plivo**, or does it say **Not included in your plan**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include SMS yet.
</Note>

## Fields

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Auth ID** | Your Plivo Auth ID. |
| **Auth token** | Your Plivo auth token. |
| **Sender number** | Your Plivo number, in international format (for example `+1500…`). |

All three are required.

## Connect Plivo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your credentials">
    In the Plivo Console, open **Account → API authentication** and copy your **Auth ID** and **Auth Token**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    In the sidebar, open **Integrations** > **Plivo**. Paste both, enter your **Sender number**, and click **Connect**. The credentials are checked with Plivo first, then the SMS channel is created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the webhook on your number">
    Click **Copy** in the **Inbound & delivery webhook** panel. In Plivo, under **Phone Numbers → Manage numbers**, point the inbound-message URL and the delivery-report URL at that address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Replies to your number now appear in the inbox, and delivery reports update message status.

## Line type lookup

Plivo offers a Number Lookup, so the **Delivery settings** panel shows **Check failed numbers with a line type lookup**. Tick it and click **Save settings**. When Plivo reports an invalid destination, one lookup is made for that number (billed by Plivo and remembered for 30 days) to tell a landline from a typo. Landlines are then skipped in future sends.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Could not reach the Plivo API: …"** Plivo refused the Auth ID and token. Copy them again from **Account → API authentication**.
- **Replies don't reach the inbox.** The inbound-message URL on your number isn't set to the URL from the page.
- **Messages have no delivery status.** The delivery-report URL isn't set to the URL from the page.
- **The card shows Error.** Plivo rejected the saved token. Paste the new one and click **Save & reconnect**.
