These cards on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page send your sales inbox contacts to other tools: **HubSpot** and **Mailchimp** (under **Sales & CRM**) and **Google Sheets** (under **Productivity**).

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission.

<Note>
Don't see a card, or does it say **Not included in your plan**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet.
</Note>

## HubSpot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a private app in HubSpot">
    In HubSpot, open **Settings → Integrations → Private Apps** and create a private app. Grant it the `crm.objects.contacts.write` scope and copy its access token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the token">
    Open **Integrations** > **HubSpot** and paste the token into **Private app access token**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose auto-sync">
    Optionally tick **Auto-sync every new & updated contact**. When it's on, contacts with an email are pushed to HubSpot automatically, with no flow step needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mailchimp

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get an API key">
    In Mailchimp, open **Account → Extras → API keys** and create or copy a key. The datacenter part of the key (for example `-us21`) is read from it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the key">
    Open **Integrations** > **Mailchimp** and paste it into **API key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. Your audiences load and appear under **Audiences**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose auto-sync">
    Optionally tick **Auto-sync every new & updated contact** and pick the audience under **Auto-sync into audience**, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After connecting

- **Synced** shows how many contacts have been sent to the CRM so far.
- A contact needs an email address to sync.
- Without auto-sync, you can push contacts on demand with the **Sync to HubSpot** or **Add to Mailchimp** step in a flow.
- To change the key, paste the new one and click **Save**. A saved key shows as dots; leave the field blank to keep it.

## Google Sheets

Google Sheets lets your flows add a row to a spreadsheet for each contact or event, using the **Send to Google Sheets** step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Open **Integrations** > **Google Sheets** and click **Sign in with Google**. Allow access to your spreadsheets and Drive files. The page then shows **Connected as** with your Google account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a spreadsheet">
    Under **Spreadsheets**, click **New spreadsheet**, enter a **Name** and, optionally, **Headers (optional)** as a comma-separated list (written as the first row), then click **Create**. Or paste the address of an existing Google Sheet and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in a flow">
    Add the **Send to Google Sheets** step to a flow to append a row each time it runs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each spreadsheet in the list shows its number of tabs and its headers; click the name to open it in Google Sheets. The bin button next to a spreadsheet takes it off this list.

If the card says **Not available yet** and the page reads "Google is not available yet.", your platform hasn't set up Google sign-in.
