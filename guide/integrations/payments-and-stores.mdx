The **Payments** cards on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page let you send checkout links in conversations and track whether they were paid. The **Commerce** cards connect your online store so orders can trigger flows and customers are synced.

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission.

<Note>
Don't see a card, or does it say **Not included in your plan**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet.
</Note>

## Payment gateways

Every gateway page has an **API credentials** panel. The fields depend on the gateway:

| Gateway | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Stripe** | **Secret key**, **Webhook signing secret** |
| **PayPal** | **Client ID**, **Client secret**, **Environment** (sandbox or live), **Webhook ID** |
| **Paystack** | **Secret key** |
| **Razorpay** | **Key ID**, **Key secret**, **Webhook secret** |
| **MyFatoorah** | **API token**, **API base URL**, **Webhook secret key** |
| **Mercado Pago** | **Access token**, **Webhook secret** |
| **Flutterwave** | **Secret key**, **Secret hash** |

Below the fields, a short note lists the currencies the gateway supports.

### Connect a gateway

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your credentials">
    Follow the page's **Setup guide**, which names the exact screen in your gateway's dashboard (for example **Developers → API keys** in Stripe, or **Apps & Credentials** in PayPal).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste them and connect">
    Fill in the fields and click **Connect**. Keys are encrypted and never sent back to your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the webhook">
    Once connected, a **Webhook endpoint** panel shows a URL. Click **Copy** and add it as a webhook in your gateway's dashboard, so payments that succeed, fail or are refunded are reported back. The setup guide lists the events to subscribe to, where the gateway asks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the webhook settings">
    Some gateways give you a value only after you add the webhook (Stripe's signing secret, PayPal's webhook ID, Razorpay's and MyFatoorah's webhook secret). Paste it into the form and click **Save & reconnect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
For Flutterwave, make up a long secret hash yourself, enter it in **Secret hash** and click **Connect**. Then, in Flutterwave's webhook settings, paste the webhook URL and type the same hash. Each payment is re-checked with Flutterwave before it is marked paid.
</Tip>

Test or live mode follows the keys you use: use test keys while you set things up. The **Payments** panel counts payments that **Succeeded** and are **Pending**.

## Online stores

**Shopify** and **WooCommerce** sync your store's products, customers and orders, and let orders trigger flows.

| Store | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Shopify** | **Store URL** (your-store.myshopify.com), **Admin API access token**, **API secret key** |
| **WooCommerce** | **Store URL**, **Consumer key**, **Consumer secret** |

### Connect a store

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create credentials in your store">
    **Shopify:** in Shopify admin, open **Settings → Apps and sales channels → Develop apps** and create a custom app. Grant the Admin API scopes listed in the setup guide and enable **Protected customer data access**, so order webhooks include names and phone numbers.

    **WooCommerce:** in WordPress admin, open **WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → REST API** and add a key with Read/Write permissions. Your store must use HTTPS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste them and connect">
    Open the store's card, fill in the fields and click **Connect store**. Webhooks are registered in your store automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once connected:

- **Synced data** shows the number of **Products**, **Customers** and **Orders**, with the last sync time. Click **Sync now** to sync again.
- Where available, **Webhook health** checks whether your store is sending events. **Check webhooks** lists any missing or disabled subscriptions, and **Repair subscriptions** sets them up again.
- If the store can't be read, a message at the top of the page says so.

### Messaging store customers

- **WhatsApp:** store customers are usually outside the 24-hour window, so automations should send an approved template first. Only customers who already have a WhatsApp conversation with your number are messaged; an order never adds a new contact.
- **Messenger and Instagram:** Meta only allows messaging people who have messaged your page before, so store events for unknown customers are skipped on those channels.
