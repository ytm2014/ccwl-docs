The cards under the **AI** tab of the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page connect your own account with an AI provider: **OpenAI**, **Claude**, **Google Gemini** or **DeepSeek**. Once connected, your flows' **AI Reply** steps can run on that provider's models and your own key. This page explains how that fits with the built-in AI and helps you choose; each provider then has its own page.

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission.

## Built-in AI or your own key

Your workspace can run AI replies in two ways:

- **Built-in AI.** Your platform provides the AI. Replies count towards your plan's monthly AI allowance. When it runs out, AI replies pause until the start of next month, or until you upgrade.
- **Your own key.** You connect a provider here and pay that provider directly. When your own provider is connected, AI replies run on it first, and they don't count towards the monthly allowance.

Some plans only use the built-in AI. On those, connecting a provider shows "This plan uses the built-in AI. Upgrade to connect your own AI provider keys." See [Plans and add-ons](/guide/billing/add-ons).

## Compare the providers

Each provider page has a **Capabilities** panel that shows what the provider can do inside the **AI Reply** step. Supported features are highlighted; unsupported ones are struck through.

| Capability | OpenAI | Claude | Google Gemini | DeepSeek |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **File search** (search your documents) | Yes | No | Yes | No |
| **Web search** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Reasoning effort** | Yes | No | No | No |
| **Functions** (call tools) | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **CRM contact tools** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

- **File search** lets the AI look things up in documents you upload. Only OpenAI and Google Gemini have it, so only their pages show a **Vector stores** tab, and only they can hold your [AI knowledge](/guide/automation/overview) when you use your own key.
- **Reasoning effort** lets the step tell the model how hard to think before replying. Only OpenAI's models offer it.
- **CRM contact tools** let the AI read and update the contact it is talking to. Every provider has them.

## Choose a provider

- Want the AI to answer from your own documents? Choose **OpenAI** or **Google Gemini**.
- Need web search but not documents? **Claude**, OpenAI and Google Gemini all offer it.
- Only need plain replies and tools? **DeepSeek** offers function calling, without web or file search.

You can connect more than one provider. Each **AI Reply** step uses the provider and model you pick in it.

## Connect any provider

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a key from the provider">
    The provider page's **Setup guide** tells you where to create it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it">
    In the sidebar, open **Integrations**, click the provider's card and paste the key into **API key** under **API credentials**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. The key is checked with the provider before it is saved, and the page confirms "… connected."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in a flow">
    In the flow builder, add an **AI Reply** step and pick this provider's model. See [Flows](/guide/automation/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change the key later, paste the new one and click **Save & reconnect**. **Disconnect** removes the key; vector stores you created stay listed.

## Troubleshooting

| Message or symptom | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "This plan uses the built-in AI. Upgrade to connect your own AI provider keys." | Your plan doesn't allow own keys. Use the built-in AI or upgrade. |
| "Enter the API key." | Paste a key before clicking **Connect**. |
| "Could not reach the … API: …" | The provider refused the key or couldn't be reached. Check the key is active and has credit at the provider, then try again. The message includes the provider's own reason. |
| "AI reply limit reached for this month" | The built-in allowance ran out. Connect your own key, or upgrade. |
| "No AI provider is connected." | Connect a provider, or ask your platform to set up the built-in AI. |
| The card shows **Error** | The provider rejected the saved key (for example it was deleted). Paste a new key and click **Save & reconnect**. |
