The **Google Calendar** card on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page connects a Google calendar to your bookings. Once it's connected, a booking made in a conversation (by a **Book Meeting** flow step or a booking link) becomes an event in your calendar, can get a Google Meet link automatically, and the times offered to contacts skip the slots your calendar shows as busy.

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission. The card is under the **Productivity** tab.

<Note>
If the card says **Not available yet** and the page reads "Google is not available yet.", your platform hasn't set up Google sign-in. If it says **Not included in your plan**, your plan doesn't include it.
</Note>

## Connect Google Calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    In the sidebar, open **Integrations** > **Google Calendar** and click **Sign in with Google**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow calendar access">
    Choose your Google account and allow access to your calendars. You're brought back to the page, which shows **Connected as** with your Google address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the booking calendar">
    Under **Booking calendar**, pick the calendar to use; your main calendar is marked **primary**. Click **Refresh** if a calendar you just created in Google isn't listed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the booking calendar does

- New meetings are created as events in this calendar.
- When times are offered to a contact, this calendar's free/busy time is checked, so slots that clash with an existing event aren't offered.
- A meeting type can use a different calendar, which then takes over for that type.

## Use it for bookings

Set up a meeting type in your appointments area and choose its location. With Google Calendar connected, the location **Google Meet (auto link)** creates a Meet link on the calendar event for every booking. The other locations (**Phone call**, **In person**, **Custom (your own link/notes)**) still create a calendar event, without a Meet link. See [Appointment types](/guide/appointments/appointment-types).

Then let contacts book:

- Add a **Book Meeting** step to a flow and pick the meeting type. It offers times in the chat (or sends a booking link) and has three exits: booked, **NO SLOTS** when no time is free, and **FAILED**. See [Flows](/guide/automation/overview).
- Or share the meeting type's booking link.

## What it unlocks

| Where | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting types | The **Google Meet (auto link)** location, and a calendar per type. |
| **Book Meeting** step | Calendar events, Meet links and availability checks for in-chat bookings. |
| [Webhooks](/guide/integrations/webhooks), [Slack](/guide/integrations/slack), [Zapier](/guide/integrations/zapier) | The **Meeting booked**, **Meeting rescheduled** and **Meeting cancelled** events. |

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** at the top of the page. The Google sign-in is removed. New bookings no longer create calendar events or Meet links, and availability is no longer checked against your calendar. Events already in your calendar stay there.

## Troubleshooting

- **No times are offered (the step takes NO SLOTS).** Your booking calendar may be busy for the whole period the meeting type offers, or the meeting type has no open hours. Check both.
- **A calendar is missing from the list.** Click **Refresh** to reload the list from Google. Check the calendar belongs to, or is shared with, the Google account shown under **Connected as**.
- **Bookings stop creating events.** The card may show **Error** because Google withdrew access (for example after a password change). Click **Sign in with Google** again.
- **You want Zoom links instead.** Connect [Zoom](/guide/integrations/zoom) and set the meeting type's location to **Zoom (auto link)**.
