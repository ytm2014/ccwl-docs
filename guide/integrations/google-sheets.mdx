The **Google Sheets** card on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page connects a Google account so your flows can write to and read from spreadsheets. Use it to log every new lead in a sheet, keep an order list your team already works from, or look up an order number a customer sends and reply with its status.

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission. The card is under the **Productivity** tab.

<Note>
If the card says **Not available yet** and the page reads "Google is not available yet.", your platform hasn't set up Google sign-in. If it says **Not included in your plan**, your plan doesn't include it.
</Note>

## Connect Google Sheets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    In the sidebar, open **Integrations** > **Google Sheets** and click **Sign in with Google**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow access">
    Choose your Google account and allow access to your spreadsheets and Drive files. You're brought back to the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the account">
    The **Google account** panel now shows **Connected as** with your Google address, and the card shows **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add spreadsheets

Your flows can only use spreadsheets listed under **Spreadsheets** on this page. Add them in either of two ways:

- **Create a new one.** Click **New spreadsheet**, enter a **Name** and, optionally, **Headers (optional)** as a comma-separated list (for example `Name, Phone, Email, Order`), then click **Create**. The headers are written as the first row.
- **Add an existing one.** Paste the address of a Google Sheet into the field next to **New spreadsheet** and click **Add**. The Google account you connected must be able to open it.

Each spreadsheet in the list shows its number of tabs and its headers. Click its name to open it in Google Sheets. The bin button takes it off this list; the spreadsheet itself stays in your Google Drive.

## Use it in a flow

Connecting Google Sheets unlocks two steps in the flow builder, under **Actions**:

### Send to Google Sheets

Appends one row each time the flow runs.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Spreadsheet** | One of the spreadsheets on this page. |
| **Tab** | The tab to write to. |
| **Columns (one row is appended per run)** | For each column, a header and a value. Values can use variables: contact fields, answers collected with a **User Input** step, and order data. |

### Get Row from Google Sheets

Reads a row back from a sheet and saves its values for later steps.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Spreadsheet** and **Tab** | Where to look. |
| **Search column (header)** | The header of the column to search, matched against the first row without regard to case. |
| **Match value** | The value to find, often a variable such as the customer's last message. The first matching row wins. |
| **Save columns to variables** | Pairs of a header and a variable name, so later steps can use the value. |
| **Or save every column with a prefix** | Saves every column as a variable named with your prefix plus the header. |

The step has two exits: **FOUND** and **NOT FOUND**. A miss, a step that isn't set up, and a read error all take **NOT FOUND**, so the conversation never stops there. Connect both exits.

See [Flows](/guide/automation/overview) for building flows.

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** at the top of the page. The Google sign-in is removed and the Sheets steps stop writing or reading until you connect again. Your spreadsheets stay in Google Drive.

## Troubleshooting

- **The step says "No spreadsheets yet — connect Google Sheets under Integrations first."** Connect Google Sheets and add at least one spreadsheet on this page.
- **Rows land in the wrong columns.** The values are written in the order of the step's columns, starting at the first column of the tab; the headers in the step are only labels. Put the columns in the same order as the sheet.
- **Nothing is written, but the flow carries on.** The step skips the row (and the flow continues) when Google Sheets isn't connected, or when the spreadsheet was removed from this page. Check both.
- **Get Row always takes NOT FOUND.** Check the **Search column (header)** spelling, that the value really exists in that column, and that the step's tab is the right one.
- **The card shows Error.** Google rejected the saved sign-in, for example because access was removed in your Google account. Click **Sign in with Google** again.
