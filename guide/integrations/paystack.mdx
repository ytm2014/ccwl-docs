The **Paystack** card on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page connects your Paystack account, so you can collect card and bank payments in conversations. Paystack supports NGN, GHS, ZAR, KES and USD; amounts are charged in the currency's smallest unit. For how payment links, triggers and the inbox button work, see [Payment gateways](/guide/integrations/payment-gateways).

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission. The card is under the **Payments** tab.

<Note>
Don't see **Paystack**, or does it say **Not included in your plan**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include payments yet.
</Note>

## Fields

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Secret key** | Your Paystack secret key, starting with `sk_test_` or `sk_live_`. |

Paystack needs no separate webhook secret: it signs each event with your secret key, and every event is checked against it.

## Connect Paystack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your secret key">
    In the Paystack Dashboard, open **Settings → API Keys & Webhooks** and copy your **Secret key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    In the sidebar, open **Integrations** > **Paystack**, paste it into **Secret key** and click **Connect**. The **Webhook endpoint** panel appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the webhook URL">
    Click **Copy** next to the webhook URL. Back on the same Paystack page, paste it into **Webhook URL** and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test and live mode

Test or live mode follows the key: an `sk_test_` key creates test payments, an `sk_live_` key real ones. Paystack keeps a separate webhook URL for each mode, so set it for live mode too when you switch keys.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Could not reach the Paystack API: …"** Paystack refused the key. Check you copied the secret key, not the public key.
- **Payments stay Pending.** The webhook URL isn't set for the mode your key is in.
- **A currency is refused.** Paystack only supports NGN, GHS, ZAR, KES and USD, and only those your account has enabled.
- **The card shows Error.** Paystack rejected the saved key. Paste the new key and click **Save & reconnect**.
