The **Zapier** card on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page connects your sales inbox events (a new contact, an inbound message, a broadcast sent and more) to the apps you use through Zapier. You generate a key in the dashboard, use it in a Zap, and every event you pick as a trigger reaches Zapier as it happens. Developers can find the technical details on [Zapier](/zapier).

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission.

<Note>
Don't see **Zapier**, or does it say **Not included in your plan**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet.
</Note>

## Connect Zapier

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate a key">
    Open **Integrations** > **Zapier** and click **Generate API key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the key and base URL">
    Under **Zapier credentials**, click **Reveal** or **Copy** next to **API key**. Copy it now: it is shown only once. Copy the **Base URL** too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a Zap">
    In Zapier, create a Zap and choose the app named in the page's **Setup guide** as the trigger. When Zapier asks you to authenticate, paste the API key and the base URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a trigger event">
    Choose a trigger event, for example "New contact". Zapier subscribes to it automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the Zap on">
    The Zap appears under **Connected Zaps** on the page, and events flow through as they happen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connected Zaps

**Connected Zaps** lists every event a Zap is subscribed to, with its name and event code. Until you build one, it reads "No active Zaps yet."

## Send a test event

Under **Send a test event**, pick an **Event** and click **Send test**. A sample payload goes to every Zap subscribed to that event, which helps when you map fields in the Zap editor.

## Replace the key

After the first time, the **API key** field only shows the start of the key. Click **Generate a new key** to get a new one. The new key replaces the old one, which stops working at once, but your Zaps stay subscribed: update the key in Zapier's connection settings.

To disconnect Zapier from the workspace, click **Disconnect** at the top of the page.

<Tip>
For your own software, use [webhooks](/guide/integrations/api-and-webhooks#webhooks) instead. They are signed, retried and have a delivery history.
</Tip>
