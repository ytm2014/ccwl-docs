The **Webhooks** card on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page sends events from your workspace to an HTTPS address on your own server as they happen: a post published, a message received, a contact created, a payment succeeded and more. This page covers the dashboard side. Developers can find the event list and payloads in [Webhooks overview](/webhooks/overview) and [Webhook events](/webhooks/events).

You need the workspace **Admin** role. In the sidebar, open **Integrations**, then click the **Webhooks** card. On some platforms the card first shows a short list of options; pick the one you want to manage.

## Create an endpoint

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Create endpoint**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and address">
    Enter a **Name** (for example "CRM notifications") and the **HTTPS endpoint URL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick events">
    Under **Events to send**, tick the events you want. Use **Search events** to filter the list and **Select all** to tick a whole group. For sales inbox events you can limit each event to one channel, or keep **All channels and workspace events**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable it">
    Tick **Enable this endpoint** to start deliveries now. Left unticked, the endpoint is saved as **Paused**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and copy the secret">
    Click **Save endpoint**. Copy the signing secret with **Copy secret**: it is not shown again after you close the dialog. Your developer uses it to [verify signatures](/webhooks/verify-signatures).
  </Step>
</Steps>

A workspace can have up to 25 endpoints.

## Read the list

Each endpoint shows its name and status (**Enabled** or **Paused**), its URL, the number of events it receives and the result of the **Last delivery**.

## Test, history and resend

- **Send test** queues a sample of the endpoint's first subscribed event. It only works while the endpoint is enabled.
- **Delivery history** lists each delivery with its status, number of attempts and HTTP status. Click **Details** to see every attempt with its outcome and duration, and **Resend** to send a finished delivery again. Use **Previous**, **Next** and **Refresh** to move through the list.

Failed deliveries are retried automatically, so a receiver that was briefly down usually catches up on its own. Check **Delivery history** when events seem to be missing.

## Edit, rotate and delete

- **Edit** reopens the form. Saving cancels deliveries still queued for the old settings. If the Admin responsible for the endpoint has left the workspace, tick **Make me the responsible administrator for this endpoint**.
- **Rotate secret** creates a new signing secret. For 24 hours, deliveries are signed with both the old and the new secret while you update your receiver. Tick **Revoke the old secret immediately instead** to skip that overlap.
- **Delete** stops future and queued deliveries to that endpoint.

## Older endpoints

Endpoints created with the earlier webhook settings appear under **Older endpoints** and keep working. When your receiver is ready, click **Move to workspace webhooks**: you get a new secret, then **Send test and move** moves the endpoint once your receiver answers the test. You can **Move back** for a limited time afterwards.

## Webhooks, Zapier or Slack?

| Use | When |
| --- | --- |
| Webhooks | Your own software needs every event, signed and with a delivery history. |
| [Zapier](/guide/integrations/zapier) | You want to connect events to other apps without writing code. |
| [Slack](/guide/integrations/slack) | Your team wants a message in a Slack channel for selected events. |

## Troubleshooting

- **Send test does nothing.** The endpoint is **Paused**. Edit it and tick **Enable this endpoint**.
- **Deliveries fail with an HTTP error.** Open **Details** in **Delivery history** to see the status your server answered, fix the receiver, then **Resend**.
- **Signatures stopped matching after a rotation.** Update your receiver with the new secret within 24 hours, or rotate again.
- **You can't create another endpoint.** You've reached 25 endpoints. Delete one you no longer use.
