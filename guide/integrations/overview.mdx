The **Integrations** page is where you connect your workspace to other tools: AI providers, CRMs, calendars, payment gateways, online stores, SMS gateways and more, plus the developer tools (**REST API**, **Webhooks** and **MCP**). In the sidebar, open **Integrations**.

## Who can open it

- Workspace **Admins** can open the page and every developer card.
- Other team members can open it when their team role includes the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission. They see the provider cards and can connect them, but the **REST API** card and the workspace **Webhooks** page need the **Admin** role.
- Every member can open the **MCP** page to connect their own AI assistant, also from the **Connect an agent** card in **Profile** > **API**. See [AI assistants](/guide/integrations/ai-assistants).

If you can't open **Integrations**, ask a workspace Admin to give you the permission.

## Browse the cards

The page shows one card per integration. Tabs along the top filter them by category, each with a count:

| Tab | What you find there |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every card. |
| **Developer** | **REST API**, **Webhooks** and **MCP**. |
| **AI** | OpenAI, Claude, Google Gemini and DeepSeek. |
| **Productivity** | Zapier, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, Zoom, Slack and the Meta cards. |
| **Sales & CRM** | HubSpot and Mailchimp. |
| **Commerce** | Shopify and WooCommerce. |
| **Payments** | Stripe, PayPal, Paystack, Razorpay, MyFatoorah, Mercado Pago and Flutterwave. |
| **SMS** | Twilio, Plivo and **Custom HTTP gateway**. |

A tab only appears when it has at least one card. Which cards you see depends on your plan and on what your platform offers, so your list may be shorter.

Each provider card shows a status badge: **Connected**, **Not connected**, **Error**, or **Not available yet** for a Google, Zoom or Meta tool your platform hasn't set up. The bottom of the card says **Connect** or **Manage**; click the card to open it.

<Note>
Don't see the provider cards, only the developer ones? Your plan or your platform doesn't include the sales inbox integrations yet.
</Note>

## Cards your plan doesn't include

A tool that isn't part of your workspace's plan still has a card, so you can see what's available. Its badge reads **Not included in your plan**, and a notice above the cards explains that these integrations can be added with an add-on. Use the button in that notice (**See add-ons**, or **Upgrade your plan** when no add-on fits your plan) to add it. You can't connect a locked tool until your plan includes it.

## On an integration's page

Every provider page has the same frame around the provider's own settings:

- **Back to integrations** at the top returns you to the cards.
- Once the tool is connected, **Test connection** and **Disconnect** appear next to its name.
- **Connection status** shows the **State**, **Last sync**, **Events (24h)** and a **Quota** bar.
- **Recent delivery log** lists the latest events sent through the integration, or "No deliveries yet."
- A **Setup guide** panel walks you through getting the credentials from the other service.

Credentials you save are encrypted and never shown again. When a field already holds a saved secret, leave it blank to keep it.

## Request an integration

If the tool you need isn't listed:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Request an integration** at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the tool">
    Fill in **Which integration?**, for example the CRM you use. Optionally add what you'd use it for under **Anything else? (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Click **Send request**. Your platform's team receives it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The button only shows for people who can connect integrations, and you can send a few requests an hour.
