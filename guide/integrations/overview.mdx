The **Integrations** page connects your workspace to other tools: AI providers, Google, Zoom, Slack, CRMs, online stores, payment gateways, SMS gateways and Meta advertising, plus the developer tools (**REST API**, **Webhooks** and **MCP**). In the sidebar, open **Integrations**, at the bottom below **Settings** and **Accounts**.

This page covers what every integration shares. Each integration then has its own page, listed at the end.

## Who can open it

| Who | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Workspace **Admins** | Open every card, connect and disconnect tools, and open the **REST API** and **Webhooks** pages. |
| Members whose team role includes **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** | Open, connect and disconnect the provider cards. The **REST API** and **Webhooks** pages still need the **Admin** role. |
| Every member | Open the **MCP** page to connect their own AI assistant. On some platforms they also see the provider cards, but can't open them. |

If you can't open **Integrations** or a card, ask a workspace Admin to add the permission to your team role. See [Team and roles](/guide/account/roles-and-permissions).

## Browse the cards

The page shows one card per integration. Tabs along the top filter the cards by category, each with a count:

| Tab | Cards |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every card. |
| **Developer** | **REST API**, **Webhooks** and **MCP**. |
| **AI** | OpenAI, Claude, Google Gemini and DeepSeek. |
| **Productivity** | Zapier, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, Zoom, Slack, Meta Ads and Meta Conversion Leads. |
| **Sales & CRM** | HubSpot and Mailchimp. |
| **Commerce** | Shopify and WooCommerce. |
| **Payments** | Stripe, PayPal, Paystack, Razorpay, MyFatoorah, Mercado Pago and Flutterwave. |
| **SMS** | Twilio, Plivo and **Custom HTTP gateway**. |

A tab only appears when it has at least one card. Which cards you see depends on your plan and on what your platform offers, so your list may be shorter. If nothing is available, the page says "No integrations are available for this workspace."

<Note>
Only see the **Developer** cards? Your plan or your platform doesn't include the sales inbox integrations yet.
</Note>

Each provider card shows its category, a short description and a status badge. The bottom of the card shows the connected account (for example your store's name) or **Not connected**, and **Connect** or **Manage**. Click the card to open its page.

## What the status badges mean

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The tool is connected. |
| **Not connected** | Nothing is saved yet, or the tool was disconnected. |
| **Error** | The other service rejected the saved credentials, for example because a key was deleted or a sign-in expired. Open the card and connect again. |
| **Not available yet** | A Google, Zoom or Meta Ads card your platform hasn't set up sign-in for. You can't connect it yet. |
| **Not included in your plan** | Your plan doesn't include this tool. See below. |

When a tool moves to **Error**, anything that depends on it (a flow step, a payment link, an SMS) may fail until you reconnect it. Where your platform has these alerts switched on, workspace Admins also get an email titled "Reconnect your … integration" with a **Reconnect integration** button that opens the card.

## Cards your plan doesn't include

A tool your plan doesn't include still has a card, so you can see what's available. Its badge reads **Not included in your plan**, and a notice above the cards says these integrations can be added with an add-on, with a button to see the add-ons. You can still open the card and disconnect a tool you connected earlier, but you can't connect, test or use it until your plan includes it. See [Plans and add-ons](/guide/billing/add-ons).

## On an integration's page

Every provider page has the same frame around the provider's own settings:

- **Back to integrations** at the top returns you to the cards. The status badge sits next to the name.
- Once the tool is connected, **Test connection** and **Disconnect** appear at the top right.
- **Connection status** shows the **State**, **Last sync**, **Events (24h)** and a **Quota** bar.
- **Recent delivery log** lists the latest events sent through the integration, or "No deliveries yet."
- A **Setup guide** panel lists the steps to get the credentials from the other service.

Keys and tokens you save are encrypted and never shown again. A field that already holds a saved secret shows dots and "saved — leave blank to keep": leave it empty to keep the saved value, or type a new one to replace it.

**Test connection** checks the saved credentials with the other service. You can run a few tests a minute; after that, the page asks you to wait.

**Disconnect** removes the saved credentials for that tool. Flow steps that use it stay in your flows, but can't run until you connect again.

## Request an integration

If the tool you need isn't listed:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Request an integration** at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the tool">
    Fill in **Which integration?**. Optionally say what you'd use it for under **Anything else? (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Click **Send request**. Your platform's team receives it, and the page confirms it was sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The button only shows for people who can connect integrations. You can send a few requests an hour; after that, the page asks you to try again later.

## Troubleshooting

- **"This integration connects via its sign-in button, not a credential form."** Google Sheets, Google Calendar, Zoom and Meta Ads connect with their sign-in button, not a key.
- **"Too many tests. Wait a minute, then try again."** Wait a minute before the next **Test connection**.
- **"… is not reachable publicly"** after you enter an address (a store, a base URL, a send URL): it must be a public HTTPS address. Local or private network addresses are refused.
- **A card disappeared.** Your plan or your platform no longer offers it.
