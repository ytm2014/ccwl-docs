The **Mercado Pago** card on the [Integrations](/guide/integrations/overview) page connects your Mercado Pago account, so you can collect payments in conversations with Checkout Pro links. Mercado Pago serves Latin America (ARS, BRL, MXN, COP, PEN, UYU and more); each account charges in its own country's currency. For how payment links, triggers and the inbox button work, see [Payment gateways](/guide/integrations/payment-gateways).

You need the **Admin** role or the **Connect integrations and manage webhooks** permission. The card is under the **Payments** tab.

<Note>
Don't see **Mercado Pago**, or does it say **Not included in your plan**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include payments yet.
</Note>

## Fields

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Access token** | Your Mercado Pago access token: `APP_USR-…` for live, `TEST-…` for test. |
| **Webhook secret** | The secret Mercado Pago shows for your webhook, under **Your integrations → Webhooks**. |

## Connect Mercado Pago

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your access token">
    In your Mercado Pago developer account, open your application's credentials and copy the **Access token** for the mode you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    In the sidebar, open **Integrations** > **Mercado Pago** and paste it into **Access token**. Every field must be filled in to connect: paste the **Webhook secret** if Mercado Pago already shows one, or type a temporary value for now. Click **Connect**. The **Webhook endpoint** panel appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the webhook">
    Click **Copy** next to the webhook URL. In Mercado Pago, under **Your integrations → Webhooks**, add that URL for payment events and copy the secret it shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the webhook secret">
    Paste it into **Webhook secret** and click **Save & reconnect**. Until the real secret is saved, payments can't be confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each event is checked against the webhook secret, so events that don't come from your account are ignored.

## Test and live mode

Test or live mode follows the token: a `TEST-` token creates test payments, an `APP_USR-` token real ones.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Could not reach the Mercado Pago API: …"** Mercado Pago refused the token. Copy it again from your application's credentials.
- **Payments stay Pending.** The webhook isn't set up, or its secret differs from the one saved here.
- **The amount is charged in another currency.** Each Mercado Pago account charges in its own country's currency. Use that currency in your payment requests.
- **The card shows Error.** Mercado Pago rejected the saved token. Paste a new one and click **Save & reconnect**.
