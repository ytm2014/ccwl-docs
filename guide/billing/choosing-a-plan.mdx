Each workspace has its own subscription, and the plan decides how many social accounts, scheduled posts, team members, storage and AI credits the workspace gets, and which features are included. Workspace admins choose and change the plan.

<Note>
Your platform may manage billing for you. In that case some of these pages aren't shown; contact your platform about your plan.
</Note>

## Open your subscription

In the sidebar, open **Settings** > **Subscription**. You can also click **Upgrade** in the top bar, or a **Choose a plan** button on a trial or plan-limit notice.

The **Subscription** page shows your **Current plan** and its status (**Active**, **Trialing**, **Past due** or **Canceled**), and from here you reach every billing action. The settings menu beside it also has **Usage**, **Receipts** and, where offered, **Add-ons**.

If you have no paid plan, the page says so (or that you're on the free plan) and offers **Subscribe** or **Upgrade plan**.

## The plan picker

Click **Subscribe**, **Upgrade plan** or **Change subscription plan** to open the plans.

- **Monthly** or **Yearly**: switch the billing cycle. Yearly prices show what you save, such as **Save 20%**, and the monthly equivalent (**Billed … yearly**). Your platform may sell only one cycle.
- Each plan card lists what it includes: **Social accounts**, **Accounts per network**, **Scheduled posts at a time**, **Team members**, **Media storage**, **AI credits per month**, and features such as **REST API**, **AI agents (MCP)**, **Webhooks**, **Inbox & engagement** and **X (Twitter)**. One plan may be marked **Best value**; yours is marked **Current plan**.
- **Compare plans** shows them side by side.
- **Have a coupon?** opens a box to enter a coupon, with **Use coupon** and **Remove coupon**. Shown only where your platform accepts coupons.

Where your platform also sells reseller plans, a **Standard plans** / **Whitelabel plans** switch appears above the cycle. A whitelabel plan makes you a reseller agency, with your own branded console, sub-accounts for your customers and your own billing. See [Agency console](/guide/account/agency-console).

## Start a free trial

If your platform offers a trial and you haven't had one yet, the page opens with **Start your …-day free trial**: free for your first days, no card needed where your platform allows it, and you can choose a plan whenever you're ready.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a plan and cycle">
    Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** and the plan you want after the trial.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the trial">
    Click **Start trial (… days)**. The note under the button says what happens next, for example "Free for 14 days, then … a month."
  </Step>
</Steps>

During the trial the top bar shows the days left (**Trial · 5 days left**, or **Trial ends today**). See [Trials and locked workspaces](/guide/billing/trials-and-locked-workspaces).

Where offered, **or subscribe now** next to the trial button lets you skip the trial and pay straight away.

You get one free trial. A further workspace you create may start on a paid plan right away; the page then says **Your new workspace starts on a paid plan right away**.

## Subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a plan and cycle">
    Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then the plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the charge">
    The note says what you'll pay, for example "You'll pay … today, then every month. Cancel anytime."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Click **Subscribe** and complete the payment in the checkout that opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After payment you see **Setting up your workspace** for a few seconds while the payment is confirmed, then **You're all set**. If it takes longer, the payment is still being confirmed; follow it on the **Subscription** page.

If you already pay for another workspace, the plan picker may show a discount for this one, such as "20% off because you already pay for another workspace."

## If you can't subscribe

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **There are no plans** | No plans are on sale yet. Contact your platform. |
| **No payment platform is enabled, so nobody can subscribe to a plan yet.** | Your platform hasn't set up payments. Contact your platform. |
| **Something went wrong.** | The checkout couldn't start. Try again in a moment. |
