You can cancel a workspace's subscription at any time. It stays active until the end of the billing period you paid for, and you can change your mind until then. Cancelling is for workspace admins.

## Cancel the subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subscription page">
    In the sidebar, open **Settings** > **Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the cancellation">
    In the **Cancel subscription** panel, click **Cancel subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read what happens">
    The dialog explains what happens at the end of the period (see below). If a yearly price is cheaper, it also offers **Switch to yearly billing and save …%** under **Before you go**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us why">
    Under **Why are you cancelling?**, choose a reason: **It's too expensive**, **It's missing features I need**, **I'm not using it enough**, **I'm switching to another tool**, **Technical issues or bugs**, **I only need a break** or **Other reason**. Add a comment in **Anything else we should know? (optional)**; with **Other reason**, the comment box is where you explain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Cancel subscription**. Click **Keep subscription** instead to close the dialog without cancelling.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after you cancel

Until the end of the current billing period, nothing changes: you keep your plan and publishing continues. A notice tells you the date: **Your subscription ends on …**.

After that date:

| Your platform... | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Has a free plan | Your workspace moves to the free plan. Your accounts, posts and media are kept. |
| Has no free plan | Publishing stops and the workspace is locked. Your accounts, posts and media are kept, so you can come back any time. |

Admins can get a **Subscription ended** notification when this happens.

Add-ons are separate. Cancel any you no longer need on the **Add-ons** page. See [Add-ons](/guide/billing/add-ons).

## Change your mind before it ends

While a cancelled subscription is still running, the **Subscription** page and the dashboard notice offer to keep it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subscription page">
    In the sidebar, open **Settings** > **Subscription**, or click **Resume subscription** on the notice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep it">
    The dialog **Keep your subscription?** says your plan continues as before and renews on its date. Click **Keep subscription**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Nothing is charged until the normal renewal date.

## Resume a paused or ended subscription

If the subscription has ended or is paused, opening the workspace shows the **Locked** page. Click **Reactivate subscription** (or **Reactivate** with your plan's name) to start again. Where you resume a paused subscription from the **Subscription** page, **Resume your subscription?** says your plan starts again today and billing continues from now; click **Resume**.

Your data comes back as it was as soon as the workspace is active again. See [Trials and locked workspaces](/guide/billing/trials-and-locked-workspaces).

## Downgrade instead

If you want to keep using the workspace on a smaller budget, consider a cheaper plan or the free plan instead of cancelling. See [Change your plan](/guide/billing/changing-your-plan).

## Deleting the workspace

Cancelling doesn't delete anything. To remove the workspace and all its data for good, the owner deletes it from workspace settings; any active subscription is then cancelled immediately and not charged again. Deleting your own account does the same for every workspace you own. See [Workspace settings](/guide/account/workspace-settings#delete-the-workspace).
