A workspace can be on a free trial, active, cancelled and running out, or locked. The dashboard always tells you which, and what to do next. Your accounts, posts and media are kept in every case.

## During a free trial

- The top bar shows the time left, for example **Trial · 5 days left**, or **Trial ends today** on the last day.
- A notice may say **Your free trial ends in 5 days.** with **Choose a plan to keep your scheduled posts publishing.** and a **Choose a plan** button.
- If you picked a plan when you started the trial, the **Subscription** page says when the trial ends and what you'll pay from then.
- Admins can get **Trial ending soon** in the notifications bell a few days before the end.

To keep publishing without a break, choose a plan before the trial ends. See [Choosing a plan](/guide/billing/choosing-a-plan).

## When the trial ends

If no plan is chosen, opening the workspace shows **Your free trial has ended** with **Choose a plan to pick up where you left off.** The page reminds you how many social accounts are connected and how many scheduled posts are waiting to be published.

- Click **Continue with …** to subscribe to the plan you were trying (it shows the starting price), or **Choose a plan** to see them all.
- Your accounts, posts and media are kept. Publishing resumes as soon as you subscribe.

Admins can get a **Trial ended** notification.

## Notices on the dashboard

| Notice | What it means | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your last payment failed, so publishing is paused.** | A payment didn't go through. | **Update payment method** |
| **Your subscription ends on …, then your workspace moves to the free plan.** | You cancelled and your platform has a free plan. | **Resume subscription** |
| **Your subscription ends on …. Resume it to keep publishing after that.** | You cancelled and there's no free plan. | **Resume subscription** |
| **Your free trial ends in … days.** | Your trial is running out. | **Choose a plan** |

Some notices offer **Manage billing**, which opens the **Subscription** page.

## Locked workspaces

A locked workspace opens on the **Locked** page: **Sorry, this workspace has been locked.**, with the reason:

| Reason | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **The subscription for this workspace has ended, so publishing has stopped.** | Click **Reactivate subscription** (or **Reactivate** with your plan's name) and subscribe again. |
| **The subscription for this workspace is paused, so publishing has stopped.** | Click **Reactivate** to resume it. |
| **The administrator locked this workspace. Contact support to unlock it.** | Your platform locked it. Contact support. |

Your accounts, posts and media are kept and come back as soon as the workspace is active again.

<Note>
Members who aren't admins can't change the subscription. If your workspace is locked or its trial has ended, ask a workspace admin.
</Note>

## Common questions

**Do my scheduled posts still go out while a payment is failing?**
No. Publishing is paused until the payment goes through.

**Can I have a second free trial on a new workspace?**
You get one trial. A further workspace may start on a paid plan straight away.

**I subscribed, but the workspace still says the trial ended.**
Payment confirmation can take a few seconds. If it's still locked after that, check the **Subscription** page; the payment may still be being confirmed.
