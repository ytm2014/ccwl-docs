Your plan sets limits on what the workspace can hold or do at once, and which features it includes. The **Usage** page shows where you stand, notifications warn you before a limit is reached, and the app tells you clearly when something isn't included in your plan.

## The Usage page

In the sidebar, open **Settings** > **Subscription**, then click **Usage** in the settings menu. It lists how much of each plan resource the workspace is currently using, with a bar for each resource that has a limit. Resources without a limit show **Unlimited**; features that are simply on or off show **Enabled** or **Disabled**. The AI credits line also shows when your credits renew and how this period's credits were spent.

## What the limits count

| Limit | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Social accounts** | How many social accounts you can connect in total. |
| **Accounts per network** | How many accounts of the same network you can connect, for example Instagram accounts. |
| **Scheduled posts at a time** | How many posts can wait to be published at the same time. Published posts don't count. |
| **Team members** | How many people can work in the workspace. |
| **Media storage** | Space for the images and videos you upload. On some platforms it also covers inbox files; see [Storage](/guide/account/storage). |
| **AI credits per month** | Credits for the AI assistant. They renew every month on your billing day, on monthly and yearly plans alike. |
| **REST API**, **AI agents (MCP)**, **Webhooks** | Whether you can connect your own tools, AI assistants or services. |
| **Inbox & engagement** | Whether you can reply to comments and messages from one inbox. |
| **X (Twitter)** | Whether you can publish to X. Some plans also count X posts per billing period. |

The sales inbox has its own limits, such as how many profiles per channel can run automations and how many contacts you can hold. See [Contact limits](/guide/contacts/contact-limits) and [Add-ons](/guide/billing/add-ons).

## When you reach a limit

When an action would go over a limit, it's refused and a **Plan limit reached** dialog says which limit, for example "Account limit reached. Max limit: 5", "Maximum limit of scheduled posts reached.", "Members limit reached." or "Your current plan's AI credits limit has been reached." Click **Upgrade plan** to see the plans, or **Dismiss**.

Nothing you already have is removed. To carry on, free something up (delete a scheduled post, disconnect an account, remove a member) or raise the limit with a bigger plan or an add-on.

### X posts

If your plan counts X posts, the composer shows **X posts: used / limit this period** and how much the current post counts; a post with a link counts as more than one. When they're used up, the composer says **Your X posts for this period are used up. Add X posts or connect your own X app.**

### Features not in your plan

A feature your plan doesn't include shows **Not included in your plan**: as a lock badge on its menu item or button, or as a card on its page explaining what's missing, for example "Broadcasts are not included in your plan. You can add them with an add-on." The card's button is **See add-ons**, or **Upgrade your plan** when no add-on is offered for your plan.

## Warnings before you hit a limit

The notifications bell can tell admins when the workspace has used most of a plan limit (**Plan limit almost reached**) and when it has used all of it (**Plan limit reached**), and everyone in the sales inbox when contacts pass 90% or reach the limit. See [Notifications](/guide/account/notifications).

## Get more

- **Change your plan** to one with higher limits. See [Change your plan](/guide/billing/changing-your-plan).
- **Buy an add-on** for more profiles, contacts, AI credits, X posts, storage or features, without changing plan. See [Add-ons](/guide/billing/add-ons).

## Common questions

**Do published posts count toward the scheduled posts limit?**
No. Only posts waiting to be published count.

**When do AI credits come back?**
On your billing day each month. Credits from an AI credits add-on renew with them.
