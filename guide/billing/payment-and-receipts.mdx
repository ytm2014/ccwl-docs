Each workspace pays for its own subscription and add-ons. Workspace admins manage the payment method and download receipts from workspace settings.

## See your payment method

In the sidebar, open **Settings** > **Subscription**. The **Payment Method** panel shows the method on file:

| Field | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | The kind of payment method, such as a card. |
| **Brand** | The card brand. |
| **Ending in** | The last digits of the card. |
| **Expires on** | When the card expires. |

## Update your payment method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payment form">
    Under **Payment Method**, click **Update payment method**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new details">
    You are taken to the payment provider's billing portal. Enter the new card or payment details there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save them in the portal, then come back to the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your card details are entered with the payment provider, not in the dashboard.

## When a payment fails

If a subscription payment doesn't go through:

- The **Subscription** page shows **Your last payment failed**: publishing is paused until the payment goes through. Update your payment method to resume it.
- A notice across the dashboard says the same, with an **Update payment method** button.
- The status shows **Past due**.
- Admins can get a **Payment failed** notification in the bell. See [Notifications](/guide/account/notifications).

Scheduled posts don't go out while the payment is outstanding. Update the payment method; once the payment succeeds, publishing resumes. If the subscription ends without payment, the workspace is locked; see [Trials and locked workspaces](/guide/billing/trials-and-locked-workspaces).

An add-on with a failed payment shows **Payment past due** on the **Add-ons** page. It's fixed the same way.

## Your first payment after a trial

During a trial, the **Subscription** page tells you when the trial ends and what you'll pay from then, for example "Your free trial ends on 12 March. Your plan then becomes active and you'll pay … a month." If you started the trial without a card, choose a plan and pay before it ends to keep publishing.

## Receipts

In the settings menu, click **Receipts**. It lists every invoice for the workspace with its **Invoice Number**, **Billing date** and **Amount**. Click **Download** on a row to save it.

Add-on payments are billed separately from the plan.

## Common questions

**I have several workspaces. Do they share a card?**
No. Each workspace has its own subscription and payment method. Update each one separately.

**Can a member download receipts?**
No. Billing pages are for workspace admins.
