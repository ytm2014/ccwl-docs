You can move your workspace to another plan or billing cycle at any time from the **Subscription** page. The page tells you exactly how the change is charged before you confirm it. Changing plans is for workspace admins.

## Switch to another plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plans">
    In the sidebar, open **Settings** > **Subscription** and click **Change subscription plan**. (While you're on a trial or an active plan, this is how you change.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the plan and cycle">
    Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** and the plan you want. Your current plan is marked **Current plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read how it's charged">
    A confirmation asks, for example, **Switch to Pro, billed yearly?** and explains the charge (see below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Switch plan**, or **Dismiss** to keep your current plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The new limits apply as soon as the change goes through.

## How a change is charged

The confirmation shows one of these, depending on your situation and your platform's payment settings:

| Situation | What the confirmation says |
| --- | --- |
| You're on a trial | Your trial continues; the new price starts when it ends. |
| Charged now for the difference | You're charged today for the difference over the rest of your current billing period, then the new price each month or year. |
| Difference added to your next payment | The difference for the rest of the period is added to your next payment, then the new price. |
| New price from the next payment | The new price applies from your next payment; nothing is charged now. |

<Tip>
Moving from monthly to yearly usually costs less over the year: the plan cards show the saving, for example **Save 20%**.
</Tip>

## Move to the free plan

Where your platform has a free plan, the **Subscription** page offers **Downgrade to free plan**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **Downgrade to free plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read when it happens">
    The dialog says either that you keep your current plan until the end of the billing period you paid for and then move to the free plan, or that your workspace moves to the free plan now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Downgrade**, or **Dismiss**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until the move happens, the page shows **Your workspace moves to the free plan on …**. Once you're on the free plan, the page says **You're on the free plan** and offers **Upgrade plan**.

## Going over a lower plan's limits

If you move to a plan with lower limits than you use today, nothing is deleted. What you already have stays; you can't add more of anything that is over the new limit until you're back under it. For example, accounts connected beyond the new limit stay connected, but you can't connect another. See [Usage and plan limits](/guide/billing/usage-and-limits).

## Add-ons instead of a bigger plan

If you only need more of one thing, such as profiles, contacts, AI credits, X posts or storage, an add-on may be cheaper than a bigger plan. See [Add-ons](/guide/billing/add-ons).

## Common questions

**Can a member change the plan?**
No. Billing is for workspace admins.
