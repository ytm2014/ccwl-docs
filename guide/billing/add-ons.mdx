Add-ons extend your plan without changing it. Each add-on is billed on its own, monthly or yearly, next to your plan. Every plan already includes the inbox on all your connected profiles; add-ons add the sales inbox's automations and tools, more capacity, and integrations.

Don't see **Add-ons** in the settings menu? Your plan or your platform doesn't offer add-ons. If the page says **No add-ons are available for your plan.**, none are offered to your current plan.

## Open the Add-ons page

In the sidebar, open **Settings** > **Subscription**, then click **Add-ons** in the settings menu. A **See add-ons** button on any **Not included in your plan** card brings you here too. The page is for workspace admins and shows **Your plan:** with your current plan's name.

## What you can add

The tiers on offer depend on your plan and your platform. Each card lists what it includes:

| Group | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| **Salesbot automations** | Profiles that can run automations, broadcasts and sequences, per channel: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, SMS, Live chat, Telegram and other networks. A card says, for example, "3 profiles on each channel" or "Unlimited profiles on each channel". |
| **Automation tools** | **Flow Builder** (with Sequences, Growth tools and WhatsApp Forms), **Broadcasts**, and **Team roles** (custom roles for your team). |
| **Integrations** | Families such as CRM (HubSpot, Mailchimp, Slack), Google Sheets and Calendar, Zoom, stores (Shopify, WooCommerce), Payments, Zapier, Meta Ads, and the sales inbox API, webhooks and MCP tools. **Show the list** expands them. |
| **Link in Bio** | Custom domains for bio pages. |
| **Contacts** | More contacts or active contacts, raising your plan's own contact limits. |
| **AI credits** | AI credits every month, added to your plan's AI credits and renewing with them. |
| **X posts** | X posts every month, added to your plan's X posts. A post with a link counts as more than one. |
| **Storage** | Extra storage for the media library and inbox. |

Some tiers also move your workspace up to another plan; the card then says **Includes an upgrade to …**.

## Buy an add-on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the cycle">
    Switch between **Monthly** and **Yearly**. Yearly prices show the saving (**Save …%**) and the monthly equivalent ("That is … a month, billed yearly").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tier">
    Click **Buy** on the tier you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Complete the payment in the checkout that opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Back on the page you may see **Your payment is being confirmed. Your add-on appears here in a moment.** It then appears under **Your add-ons** and its features are available.

You can hold one add-on of each kind. If you already have one, other tiers of the same kind show **Switch to this** instead of **Buy**; switching applies once the payment is confirmed.

AI credits and X posts add-ons can be bought once your trial has ended.

## Your add-ons

**Your add-ons** lists each add-on with how it's **Billed** (monthly or yearly), or **Added by support** if your platform granted it. It also shows:

- **Ends on …** if you cancelled it.
- **Payment past due** if its last payment failed. Update your payment method on the **Subscription** page.
- For a tier that moved your plan up: **If you cancel, your plan returns to … at the end of the paid period**, or, once cancelled, **Your plan returns to … on …**.

### Cancel an add-on

Click **Cancel add-on** and confirm. The add-on stays until the end of the period you paid for; you see **Your add-on ends on …**. Click **Keep add-on** before then to undo it (**Your add-on will renew.**).

When an add-on that moved your plan up ends, the workspace goes back to its previous plan and you get an email. Everything you set up stays; profiles connected beyond your plan stay connected, but you can't add new ones.

## Sales inbox profiles

Your plan or add-on allows a number of active sales inbox profiles per channel. On the **Accounts** page, each profile has a switch to **Turn Salesbot on** or **Turn Salesbot off**, and a count per channel such as "Instagram: 2 of 3 active". A profile with the sales inbox off still posts, and its inbox still works (**Inbox only**), but automations, broadcasts and sequences don't send there.

If all your profiles for a channel are in use, switching another on says your plan allows that many and asks you to switch one off first or add more with an add-on. After swapping profiles you may have to wait a while before switching on another; the message gives the date.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Add-ons can't be bought here yet. Contact support to add one.** | Your platform hasn't set up payments for add-ons. |
| **The checkout could not be started. Try again in a moment.** | Try again; contact support if it keeps happening. |
| **The payment provider could not make the change. Try again in a moment.** | A switch, cancel or resume failed at the payment provider. Try again. |
| **This add-on isn't available for your plan.** | The tier isn't offered to your current plan. |
