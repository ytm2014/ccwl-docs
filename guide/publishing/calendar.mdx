The **Calendar** shows your posts on the days and times they are scheduled or were published. Use it to spot gaps, move posts around and start a new post on a particular day. Every member of the workspace can view it.

In the sidebar, open **Calendar**.

## Month and week views

- Switch between **Month** and **Week** at the top.
- Use the arrows to move back and forward, and **Today** to return to the current date.
- In the month view, a busy day shows **+... more**; click it to see the rest.
- Use **Filters** to show only certain labels, accounts, platforms and so on. These are the same filters as the [posts list](/guide/publishing/posts#search-filter-and-sort).

## Post cards

Each card shows the post's text, the networks it goes to, its time and its status, for example **Scheduled**, **Needs approval**, **Published** or **Failed**. A partly published post shows its score, such as **Published (2 of 3)**.

- Click a card to open the post in the composer.
- Cards marked **Upcoming repeat** are future dates of a [repeating post](/guide/publishing/repeating-posts). Clicking one opens the original post.

## Add a post on a day

Hover over a day (month view) or a time slot (week view) and click **+**. The composer opens with that date and time filled in. This needs the **Admin** or **Member** role, and past dates aren't available.

## Reschedule by dragging

Drag a card to another day or time slot to move it. In the week view, the post lands on the nearest quarter hour under your pointer. The new time is saved when you drop the card, and a message confirms it.

- You can drag drafts, scheduled posts and posts that need approval.
- Published posts, posts being published and upcoming repeats can't be moved.
- Dragging needs the **Admin** or **Member** role.

If the post's accounts have their own times, you are asked whether to **Keep the gaps between accounts** or **Give every account the new time**. See [Schedule or publish a post](/guide/publishing/schedule-a-post#give-each-account-its-own-time).

## Share the calendar

Click **Shared Calendar** to create a read-only link of selected accounts and dates for a client, who can also approve posts if you allow it. See [Shared calendars](/guide/publishing/shared-calendars).

## Tips

- Use the week view to see exact times and how close posts are to each other.
- Filter by a label to see one campaign or client at a time.
- **Home** shows the next two days in **Today & tomorrow**, with free slots you can fill and **Open the calendar**.
