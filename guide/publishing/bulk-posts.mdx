**Bulk Posts** schedules a batch of posts from a CSV file: one row per post, with its text, date and time, and optional media, post type, repetition, Pinterest and YouTube titles and a first comment. Every post in the file goes to the accounts you choose on the page.

In the sidebar, open **Posts** > **Bulk Posts**.

<Note>
Don't see **Bulk Posts**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet, or your role doesn't give you access. Admins have it; for other members, access comes with the role their admin gives them.
</Note>

## Prepare the CSV file

Click **Click to open CSV template** on the Bulk Posts page to download the template, and fill in one row per post. The first row is the header and is skipped. The columns, in order, are:

| Column | Content |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The post text. |
| 2 | Year, for example `2026`. |
| 3 | Month. |
| 4 | Day of the month. |
| 5 | Hour. |
| 6 | Minute. |
| 7 | Post type: `post`, `reel`, `story` or `livestream`. Empty means `post`. |
| 8 | A link to an image or video to attach (optional). |
| 9 | Number of repetitions (optional). |
| 10 | Interval between repetitions (optional). |
| 11 | Pinterest title (optional). |
| 12 | Pinterest link (optional). |
| 13 | YouTube video or Short title (optional). |
| 14 | First comment (optional). |

Save it as a CSV file. Files made before the first-comment column existed (13 columns) still work.

<Tip>
Keep the media links public and pointing straight at the image or video file, so they can be downloaded when the post is created.
</Tip>

## Upload and schedule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the accounts">
    Use the account picker to select the accounts every post in the file goes to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set network options">
    Options for the selected networks appear under the accounts, as in the composer: for example a Pinterest board, TikTok privacy or a Reddit title. See [Networks: options and limits](/guide/publishing/networks/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the file">
    Drag the CSV file onto the upload area, or click **Browse**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    Click **Schedule Posts**. Each row becomes a post scheduled for its date and time, and you are taken to the posts list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A message confirms "Bulk Posts Created Successfully". The new posts behave like any scheduled post: you can open, edit, reschedule or delete them.

## Check the results

- Open **Posts** > **All Posts** > **Scheduled**, or look at the [calendar](/guide/publishing/calendar).
- Open a few of the new posts to check them for errors, such as text too long for a network. See [Preview and checks](/guide/publishing/preview-and-checks).

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Please select account** | Choose at least one account before scheduling. |
| **Please upload CSV file** | Add the file first. |
| **Invalid CSV format** / **Required columns are missing** | Start again from the template and keep the column order. |
| **Error uploading CSV file** | Check the file is saved as CSV and try again. |
| A row's post type is refused | Use one of `post`, `reel`, `story` or `livestream`, and one the selected networks accept. |
