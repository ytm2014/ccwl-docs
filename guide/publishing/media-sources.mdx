Besides your own uploads, the media library can pull images and videos from other places. The same tabs appear in the **Media Library** page and in the composer when you click **Add Media**. Which tabs you see depends on your plan and your platform.

## The tabs

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Upload** | Your own files, with folders. See [Media library](/guide/publishing/media-library). |
| **Stock Photos** | Search a stock photo service and use a photo. |
| **GIFs** | Search for GIFs. |
| **New design** | Create a design in Adobe Express from a preset size. |
| **Canva** | Connect your Canva account and import your designs. |
| **Google Drive** | Pick files from your Google Drive. |
| **Dropbox** | Pick files from your Dropbox. |

Media you take from a stock, GIF, Canva or cloud source is saved to your library when you use it, so you can reuse it later.

## Stock photos and GIFs

1. Open **Stock Photos** or **GIFs**.
2. Type a search term, or browse the suggestions.
3. Select one or more items and click **Insert** (in the composer) to add them.

Stock photos and GIFs carry a credit to their author and source, such as "Photo by ... on ...". When used in a post, the composer can add this credit to your text. If nothing matches, you see **No images found.** or **No GIFs found.**

## Canva

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Open **Canva** and click **Connect to Canva**. Sign in to Canva and allow access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a design">
    Your designs are listed. Use **Load More** for older ones and **Refresh** to see new ones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Click **Import Design** on a design. Choose the format (**Select Format**) and quality (**Select Quality**, between 1 and 100), or for a video the orientation and resolution (horizontal or vertical, 480p to 4k). Click **Export Design**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Lower quality means a smaller file that may look pixelated; higher quality looks better but is larger. **Edit Design** opens the design in Canva. To unlink your account, click **Disconnect Canva** and **Proceed**.

## Google Drive and Dropbox

1. Open **Google Drive** or **Dropbox** and sign in when asked.
2. Pick the files in the picker.

Larger files download in the background (**Downloading...**), and you are notified when they are ready in your library.

## New design (Adobe Express)

Open **New design** and pick a size, such as **Instagram square post**, **Instagram portrait post**, **Instagram story**, **Facebook post**, **Facebook story**, **LinkedIn post**, **X post** or **Pinterest post**, or a **Custom size** in pixels, inches or millimetres. Click **Create design** to open the editor; the finished design is saved to your library.

You can also start from an existing image with **Create for Adobe Express** in its menu.

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Failed to load designs** | Click **Refresh**, or disconnect and reconnect Canva. |
| **Error downloading media. Try again!** | The cloud file couldn't be fetched. Try again or download and upload it yourself. |
