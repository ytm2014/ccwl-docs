The composer is where every post starts. You choose the accounts the post goes to, write one **Original** version that all of them use, and add media and options. Everything you type is saved as you go, so a post you have not scheduled yet waits in your drafts.

You need the **Admin** or **Member** role to create and edit posts. Viewers can open posts but not change them.

## Open the composer

You can start a post from several places:

- Click **New post** at the top of the sidebar.
- In the sidebar, open **Calendar** and click **+** on a day or time slot. The composer opens with that date and time already set. See [Calendar](/guide/publishing/calendar).
- On the **Accounts** page, choose **New post** from an account's menu. The composer opens with that account selected.
- On **Home**, use **Compose a post**, **Post it again** on a strong post, or a free slot in **Today & tomorrow**. See [Home](/guide/publishing/home).

## Write the post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the accounts">
    Click **Select accounts** (it reads **Add accounts** once some are chosen) and tick the accounts to publish to. Each chosen account shows as a chip; remove one with its **x**, or click **Clear selection** to remove them all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the text">
    Type in the editor. The counter under the editor shows how many characters you have left on the strictest network you selected. See [Preview and checks](/guide/publishing/preview-and-checks).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add media">
    Click **Add Media** under the text to pick images or videos from your library, upload new ones or use another source. See [Add media to a post](/guide/publishing/add-media).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the network options">
    When you select an account from a network with options, such as a post type or a title, a box for that network appears above the editor. See [Networks: options and limits](/guide/publishing/networks/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule, queue or publish">
    Use the buttons at the bottom of the composer. See [Schedule or publish a post](/guide/publishing/schedule-a-post).
  </Step>
</Steps>

If no account is connected yet, the account list says **No accounts connected yet**. You can keep writing; an **Admin** sees **Connect account** to add one. See [Social accounts](/guide/getting-started/social-accounts).

<Note>
Some networks, such as X and LinkedIn, do not accept identical content on several accounts. When one of their accounts is already selected, the others show the warning "does not allow simultaneous posting of identical content to multiple accounts" and can't be added. Publish to the other account in a separate post.
</Note>

## Select accounts faster with groups

If you often post to the same accounts, save them as a group:

1. Select two or more accounts.
2. Click **Save as group**, enter a **Group name**, check the **Social Accounts** in it, and click **Save**.

Next time, pick the group in the account list to select all its accounts in one click. Groups are shared by your workspace and can also be managed from the **Accounts** page. See [Social accounts](/guide/getting-started/social-accounts#group-accounts).

## The editor toolbar

The row of buttons under the text holds the writing tools. Each has its own page:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Emoji | Inserts an emoji at the cursor. |
| **Hashtag Manager** | Inserts a saved group of hashtags. See [Hashtags](/guide/publishing/hashtags). |
| **Mention Manager** | Inserts a saved mention that becomes the right handle on each network. See [Mentions](/guide/publishing/mentions). |
| **Variable manager** | Inserts a variable such as the account name. See [Variables](/guide/publishing/variables). |
| **Template manager** | Inserts a template or saves the post as one. See [Templates](/guide/publishing/templates). |
| **AI Assist** | Writes or rewrites the text with AI, when AI is available. See [AI Assist](/guide/publishing/ai-assist). |
| Link button | Turns **URL shortening** on or off, or picks a short-link account. See [Links in posts](/guide/publishing/links-in-posts). |
| **Add Location** | Adds a location for Threads. See [Threads](/guide/publishing/networks/threads). |

## Format the text

Select some text to open a small formatting menu above it. It offers:

- **Case**: **UPPER CASE**, **lower case** or **Title Case**.
- **List style**: turns the selected lines into a list, for example an **Emoji number list**.
- **Bold (Unicode)** and **Italic (Unicode)**: styled characters that keep their look on networks that don't support formatting.
- **Separator**: inserts a separator between paragraphs.
- **AI Assist**, when AI is available, to rewrite only the selected text.

<Tip>
Unicode bold and italic are separate characters, so screen readers and search may not read them as normal words. Use them for a few words, not whole paragraphs.
</Tip>

## Saving and working together

- The header shows the post's status and **Saved** after each change, or **Error on saving** if a change could not be saved.
- If a teammate has the same post open, their avatar appears in the header with "also has this post open".
- If someone else saves a change while you are editing, you see **Someone else has changed this post. Your screen is out of date.** Click **Refresh** to load their version.
- Leaving a scheduled post that still has problems opens **This scheduled post will not be published**, with **Fix issues**, **Unschedule and leave** or **Leave anyway**.

Published posts open read-only with **Posts in history cannot be edited.**, and posts in the trash show **Posts in trash cannot be edited.** To reuse a published post, duplicate it from the [posts list](/guide/publishing/posts).

| **Suggestions** | Tips for each network as you write. |
| **Labels** | Labels to organise the post. See [Labels](/guide/publishing/labels). |
| **Activity** | Comments and history. See [Comments and activity](/guide/publishing/comments-and-activity). |
