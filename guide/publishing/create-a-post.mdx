The composer is where you write a post, pick the accounts it goes to and decide when it goes out. You write one **Original** version that every account uses, and you can give any account its own version when it needs different text, media or options. Your work is saved as you type, so a post you have not scheduled stays in your drafts.

You need the **Admin** or **Member** role to create and edit posts. Viewers can open posts but not change them.

## Start a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the composer">
    In the sidebar, click **New post**. You can also click the **+** on a day or time slot in the **Calendar**, which opens the composer with that date and time already set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the accounts">
    Click **Select accounts** (or **Add accounts** once some are chosen) and tick the accounts to publish to. Remove one with its **x**, or use **Clear selection** to start again. If you often post to the same set, choose **Save as group**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the post">
    Type your text in the editor. The character count shows how much room each selected network allows, and any problems appear above the editor and next to the publishing buttons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add media">
    Click **Add Media** to pick files from your [media library](/guide/publishing/media-library), upload new ones, or use the other sources your workspace offers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule or publish">
    Use the buttons at the bottom of the composer. See [Schedule, queue or publish](#schedule-queue-or-publish).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Some networks do not allow the same content on several accounts at once. The account picker then shows a warning and won't let you select the extra account.
</Note>

## Editor tools

The toolbar under the editor has:

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Emoji | Inserts an emoji. |
| **Hashtag Manager** | Saves groups of hashtags and inserts one with **Insert**. |
| Mentions | Inserts a saved mention, so each network gets the right handle. You can also type `@` in the text. |
| **Variable manager** | Inserts a variable such as the platform or account name, which is filled in for each account when the post goes out. |
| **Template manager** | Inserts one of your [templates](/guide/publishing/templates), or saves the current post with **Save As Template**. |
| **AI Assist** | Writes or rewrites text with AI. Only shown when AI is available in your workspace. |
| **URL shortening** | Turns link shortening **On** or **Off** for this post, when your platform has a shortener. |
| Short-link account | Picks one of your own short-link accounts (**Choose a short-link account**), when you have added one. |
| **Add Location** | Adds a location. Only Threads uses it, and the post must be saved first. |

On each media item you can open **More actions** to **Edit alt text**, add or change a **Video thumbnail**, or remove it. On images, **Tag people** lets you add up to 10 Instagram usernames (save the post first).

## Versions for different accounts

When you choose more than one account, tabs appear above the editor. **Original** is the shared version. To change the post for one account:

1. Click **+** (**Create version**) and choose the account under **Create version for**.
2. Edit the text, media and options on that account's tab. Other accounts keep using the original.

The version menu (**Version actions**) also offers **Copy from another version**, **Apply to other accounts**, **Sync with** another version, **Make this the original** and **Reset to original**, which drops the account's own version so it uses the original again.

## Threads and first comments

Under the editor, a button lets you add more content items when a selected network supports them:

- **Add Post** adds another post to a thread, for networks that publish threads (such as X, Bluesky, Mastodon and Threads). Each item has a menu to **Split at cursor**, **Merge with previous**, **Duplicate**, **Move up**, **Move down** or **Delete** it, and **Number the posts** adds numbering.
- **Add first comment** adds one comment that is posted under the post, for networks that publish first comments. It appears with a **First Comment** header above it.

When an account from these networks is selected, its options appear above the editor:

| Network | Options |
| --- | --- |

| Instagram | **Post**, **Reel** or **Story**; **Collaborators**; audio for Reels; **Publish as a trial Reel** with a **Graduation Strategy**; share to Story. |
| YouTube | **Title**, **Privacy configuration** (**Public**, **Private**, **Unlisted**), **Made for kids**, **Video Tags**, and a **Publishing Mode** of **Video** or **Pre-recorded Livestream**. |
| TikTok | **Who can view this post**, **Allow users to** comment, duet or stitch, **AI-generated content**, **Auto-add music**, **Content Disclosure Setting**, and trending sounds. |
| Pinterest | **Title**, **Link** and a board for each account, with **Create Pinterest Board**. |
| LinkedIn | **Visibility** (**Public** or **Connections**), and **Publish as** a post, a PDF or PowerPoint document, or a pre-recorded livestream. |
| Google Business Profile | **Post**, **Offer** or **Event**, with titles, dates, a coupon code, offer link, terms, and a call-to-action **Button**. |

| X | **X Premium or Pro account**, so long posts are published in full. |
| Reddit | **Title**, **Link**, subreddits and flair, or **Publish post to profile**. |

## Schedule, queue or publish

- **Schedule**: click **Schedule**, choose a date and time, then **Done**. With several accounts you can **Set a time per account**. Then click **Schedule** again to confirm. To undo, click **Clear time**; the post goes back to draft.
- **Add to Queue**: puts the post in the next free time of your [posting schedule](/guide/publishing/posting-schedule). Only shown when the schedule has times.
- **Post now**: publishes straight away after you confirm the list of accounts.

The schedule window also has **Repeat this post**, with **Repeat Frequency (Days)** and **Repeat Until**, so the post goes out again at that interval.

## Approval

Each team member either has **Can approve** or not.

- Without it, you can only **Schedule**. The post is saved as **Needs approval** and waits for someone who can approve.
- With it, you can **Post now** or **Schedule**, and you see an **Approve** button on posts that need approval. To ask for a review before publishing, tick **Post must be approved before it can be published** in the schedule window.

Shared calendars can also let clients approve or reject posts. See [Calendar](/guide/publishing/calendar).

## The side panel

The panel on the right of the composer has these tabs:

- **Preview**: how the post looks on each network (**This version** or **All versions**). Previews are approximate.
- **Suggestions**: tips for each platform as you write.
- **Labels**: add labels to organise posts.
- **Activity**: comments from your team on this post.
- **Delivery** and **Analytics**: after publishing, the result for each account (with **Retry** and **View on platform**) and the post's performance.
