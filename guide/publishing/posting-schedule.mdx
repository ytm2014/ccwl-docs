Your posting schedule is a set of times in the week, for example "Monday to Friday at 9:00 and 17:00". When you click **Add to Queue** in the composer, the post takes the next free time in the schedule, so you don't have to pick a time for every post. The **Queue** page shows those times and what is going out in them.

Only workspace **Admins** can open the **Queue** page. In the sidebar, open **Queue**.

## The Queue page

The page has two tabs:

- **Queue**: the posting times ahead of you and the post booked into each. An empty time shows **Free slot**. A post you scheduled by hand at another time shows as **Scheduled by hand, outside the posting schedule.**
- **Posting Schedule**: where you set the times.

On the left, **Accounts** lists **Shared** (the workspace schedule) and each account. Choose one to see its queue and schedule. An account that has no times of its own shows **Following the shared schedule** and uses the shared one.

## Set your posting times

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the schedule">
    Pick **Shared** for the whole workspace, or an account to give it its own times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Posting Schedule tab">
    Each day of the week has a switch and its list of times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add times">
    Under a day, click **Add Posting Time**, enter the **Time**, and click **Add Posting Time**. Tick **Everyday** to add the time to every day at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the timezone">
    Choose the **Timezone** the times are read in. Everyone sees the same times, whatever their own timezone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Changes save by themselves; a message confirms that the posting times were updated. To remove a time, hover over it and click its **x**. To pause a day without losing its times, turn its switch off.

### Fill the schedule for you

**Generate slots** offers quicker ways to fill a schedule:

- **From the shared schedule**: copy the workspace times into an account's schedule.
- **Recommended times**: times worked out from how your own past posts performed.
- **Copy from account**: copy another account's times.
- **Clear all Posting Times**: remove every time, after you confirm.

Switch on **Tune automatically** to have the schedule rewritten once a week from the times your posts do best at. While it is on, you can't edit the times by hand; turn it off to change them yourself.

## How the queue uses the schedule

When you click **Add to Queue** in the composer:

1. Each selected account gets its next posting time that no other queued post has taken, from its own schedule or the shared one.
2. The post is scheduled for those times and appears in the **Queue** and on the [Calendar](/guide/publishing/calendar).

**Add to Queue** only appears in the composer when the schedule has at least one time. A queued post behaves like any scheduled post: you can open it and change its time, or drag it on the calendar.

<Note>
If you don't have permission to approve posts, a queued post waits as **Needs approval** like any other post you schedule. See [Approval](/guide/publishing/create-a-post#approval).
</Note>

## Best times when scheduling

When you schedule a post by hand, the schedule window shows **Best times to post** for the selected account, worked out from how its past posts performed. Recommendations appear once an account has at least 10 published posts with imported statistics.
