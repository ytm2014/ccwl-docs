Your posting schedule is a set of times in the week, for example "Monday to Friday at 9:00 and 17:00". When you click **Add to Queue** in the composer, the post takes the next free time in the schedule, so you don't have to pick a time for every post. The **Queue** page shows those times and what is going out in each of them.

Only workspace **Admins** can open the **Queue** page. In the sidebar, open **Posts** > **Queue**. You can also open an account's queue or schedule from its menu on the **Accounts** page.

## The Queue page

The page has two tabs:

- **Queue**: the posting times ahead of you and the post booked into each one. An empty time shows **Free slot**. A post you scheduled by hand at another time shows "Scheduled by hand, outside the posting schedule."
- **Posting Schedule**: where you set the times.

On the left, **Accounts** lists **Shared** (the workspace schedule) and each account. Choose one to see its queue and schedule. An account without times of its own shows **Following the shared schedule** and uses the shared times.

If a schedule has no times yet, the **Queue** tab says "This schedule has no posting times yet. Add some on the Posting Schedule tab."

## Set your posting times

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the schedule">
    Pick **Shared** for the whole workspace, or an account to give it its own times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Posting Schedule tab">
    Each day of the week has a switch and its list of times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add times">
    Under a day, click **Add Posting Time**, enter the time, and confirm with **Add Posting Time**. Tick **Everyday** to add the time to every day at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the timezone">
    Choose the timezone the times are read in. The times are read in this timezone, whoever is looking at them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Changes save by themselves, and a message confirms "Posting times has been updated successfully". To remove a time, hover over it and click its **x**. To pause a day without losing its times, turn its switch off.

## Fill the schedule for you

**Generate slots** offers quicker ways to fill a schedule:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **From the shared schedule** | Copies the workspace times into this account's schedule. |
| **Recommended times** | Times worked out from how your own past posts performed. |
| **Copy from account** | Copies another account's times. |
| **Clear all Posting Times** | Removes every time, after you confirm. |

### Tune automatically

Switch on **Tune automatically** to have the schedule rewritten once a week from the times your own posts do best at. While it is on, the times are locked: "These times are rewritten weekly. Turn tuning off to edit them by hand."

## How the queue uses the schedule

When you click **Add to Queue** in the composer:

1. Each selected account gets its next posting time that no other queued post has taken, from its own schedule or the shared one.
2. The post is scheduled for those times and appears in the **Queue** and on the [calendar](/guide/publishing/calendar).

A queued post behaves like any scheduled post: you can open it and change its time, or drag it on the calendar. **Add to Queue** only appears when the schedule has at least one time and you can approve posts.

<Tip>
**Home** shows your **Content runway**: how many of the next 14 days already have a post waiting. It has a **Fill the queue** button. See [Home](/guide/publishing/home).
</Tip>

## RSS campaigns and the queue

An [RSS campaign](/guide/publishing/rss-campaigns) can also be started with **Add to Queue**, so its posts take your free posting times.

## Troubleshooting

- **Add to Queue is missing**: the schedule for the selected accounts has no times, or your role can't approve posts.
- **"The posting schedule has no available times."**: every upcoming time is taken. Add more times or schedule the post by hand.
- **Posts go out at the wrong hour**: check the schedule's timezone on the **Posting Schedule** tab.
