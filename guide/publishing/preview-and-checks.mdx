While you write, the composer checks your post against every network you selected: length, media, required options and more. Errors stop you scheduling until they are fixed; warnings and suggestions are advice. The **Preview** tab shows roughly how the post will look.

## Preview

Open the **Preview** tab in the composer's side panel. It shows a mock-up of the post for each network you selected, with your text, media, link card and options (for example a Reel or Story layout).

- With several versions, switch between **This version** and **All versions**.
- Long text is folded the way the network folds it, with **…see more**. A note explains that the network shows about the first so many characters before readers open the rest.

<Note>
Previews are an approximation and may not match exactly how your post looks on each platform.
</Note>

## Character count

The counter under the editor shows **Characters left** on the strictest network you selected. Hover over it (**Characters left on each platform**) to see the count for every network. Each platform counts characters its own way; for example X counts links and some characters differently, and Mastodon and Pixelfed use their server's limit.

When you go over, the counter turns red and an error appears: "Maximum of ... characters allowed." See each network's limits in [Networks: options and limits](/guide/publishing/networks/overview).

## Errors and warnings

Problems appear in a bar at the top of the composer and as a dot on the version tab they belong to.

- **Errors** must be fixed. The schedule buttons are blocked and their tooltip says "Fix ... issues to schedule or publish this post."
- **Warnings** are information, for example "Only the first post is published on ...". Click **Got it** to dismiss one.

Click **Show** next to a problem to jump to the place to fix it, and **Show all** to list every problem. The buttons next to **Schedule** also show the number of warnings and suggestions.

### Common errors

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| **Add text or media.** | The post is empty for that network. |
| **Add a photo or a video.** / **Add a video.** | The network needs media (for example Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest). |
| **Maximum of ... photos allowed.** | Remove media or give that account its own version. |
| **Combining different media types (videos, photos, GIFs) is not allowed.** | Use one media type for that network. |
| **Maximum of ... hashtags allowed.** | Remove hashtags (Instagram allows 5). |
| **Add a title.** | Fill in the title in the network's options (YouTube, Reddit, Google Business Profile offers and events). |
| **Choose a board.** | Pick a Pinterest board for each Pinterest account. |
| **Choose a start and an end date.** | Google Business Profile offers and events need dates. |
| **It looks like you haven't chosen who can watch your video.** | Choose **Who can view this post** in the TikTok options. |

If you schedule a post and a problem appears later, for example after a video finishes processing, the post shows "This post is scheduled, but it will not be published on ... until you fix ... issue." Open it and fix the issue before its time.

## Suggestions

Open the **Suggestions** tab for tips per network. A dot on the tab means there are new ones. Suggestions never block scheduling. Examples:

- A network works best with fewer hashtags, or a post has none where people search by hashtag (**Suggest hashtags** uses AI).
- A link would reach more people in the first comment (**Move the link to the first comment**).
- Links in Instagram captions can't be clicked.
- Images have no alt text (**Add alt text**).
- A network doesn't allow several accounts to publish the same text (**Create separate versions**).

Click a suggestion's button to act on it, or **Dismiss** it. **Show ... dismissed suggestions** brings dismissed ones back. When you click **Post now**, the confirmation also lists the top suggestions with **Review all ... suggestions**.
