**AI Assist** writes and rewrites post text for you inside the composer. You can ask it to draft a post from a short brief, or use quick commands to improve, expand, shorten or change the tone of what you have written. The result is only a suggestion: you choose whether to insert it.

<Note>
Don't see **AI Assist**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include AI yet. AI requests use AI credits from your workspace.
</Note>

## Open AI Assist

- Click **AI Assist** in the editor toolbar to work on the whole post.
- Or select some text and click **AI Assist** in the formatting menu above it to work on that part only. The panel shows **Selected text** with what you picked.

With **Work with full post context** switched on, AI also reads the rest of your post when it works on a selection, so the result fits around it.

## Ask AI to write a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe what you want">
    In the box (**Write a post about...**), type a brief, for example "A short post announcing our Saturday opening hours, friendly tone".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Press Enter or click **Send**. Click **Stop** to cancel while it is writing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result">
    Read the draft. Use **Retry** for a new attempt, or type a follow-up such as "make it shorter" to refine it. If AI gave several drafts, move between them with **Previous** and **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it">
    Click **Insert** to add the text at the cursor, or **Replace** to replace your text (or only the selection). **Copy** copies it instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click **New session** to start over without the previous conversation.

## Quick commands

The panel offers one-click commands for the text you have:

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Improve Writing** | Rewrites the text to read better. |
| **Fix Spelling & Grammar** | Corrects mistakes without changing the meaning. |
| **Continue writing** | Adds more text after what you wrote. |
| **Expand** | Makes the text longer and more detailed. |
| **Shorten** | Makes the text shorter. |
| **Simplify** | Uses simpler words and sentences. |
| **More Friendly**, **More Formal**, **More Edgy**, **More Engaging** | Changes the tone. |

Type in the box to filter the commands. AI keeps to the character limit of the networks you selected where it can, but check the counter before you schedule.

## Other places AI helps

- **Alt text**: in **Edit alt text**, **Generate with AI** describes the image. See [Add media to a post](/guide/publishing/add-media#alt-text).
- **Hashtags**: in the **Suggestions** tab, **Suggest hashtags** proposes hashtags for your text (1 AI credit). See [Hashtags](/guide/publishing/hashtags#hashtag-suggestions).
- **Images**: in the media library, the **AI** option creates an image from a description. See [Media library](/guide/publishing/media-library#upload-files).

## Brand Voice

Brand Voice tells AI how your brand writes, so every draft starts closer to your style. Workspace **Admins** set it in the sidebar under **Settings** > **Brand Voice**. It has four steps:

- **Identity**: **Business Name**, **Mission**, **Industry** and **Personality**.
- **Audience & Topics**: **Target Audience**, **Preferred Topics** and **Topics to Avoid**.
- **Tone & Style**: **Formality**, **Playfulness** and **Technical Depth**, **Emoji Usage**, **Hashtag Strategy**, **Words to Avoid** and **Preferred CTAs**.
- **Example Posts**: real posts that sound the way you want.

You can also enter a **Website URL** to fill in the fields from your site. Results are suggestions; review them before you save.

## AI credits

Each AI request uses credits from your workspace. Where your workspace shows a balance, the AI panel displays the credits you have left. How many credits you get depends on your plan and add-ons.

## Tips

- Give AI the facts (dates, prices, links) in your brief. It doesn't know them otherwise.
- Always read the result before inserting it. AI can be wrong.
- Use a separate [version](/guide/publishing/post-versions) for each network and run **Shorten** on the ones with tight limits.
