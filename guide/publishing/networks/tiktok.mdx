TikTok posts are a single video or, where your platform allows it, a set of photos. TikTok requires a few choices on every post, so the TikTok options are set separately for each TikTok account you select.

## Limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Text | 2,200 characters. |
| Media | 1 video. Where photo posts are enabled: a video or up to 35 photos, not both. |
| Video length | Up to the maximum TikTok allows for the account. |
| Extra items | None (no threads or first comments). |
| Video cover | A frame of the video. See [Add media to a post](/guide/publishing/add-media#video-covers). |

## TikTok options

For each TikTok account:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Who can view this post** | Required. **Everyone**, **Friends**, **Followers** or **Only you**, as TikTok offers them for the account. |
| **Allow users to** | **Comment**, **Duet** and **Stitch**. Options turned off in the TikTok account's settings are disabled ("Turned off in your TikTok account settings."). Duet and Stitch don't apply to photo posts. |
| **AI-generated content** | "Add this label to tell viewers your content was generated or edited with AI." |
| **Auto-add music** | For photo posts: lets TikTok add background music to the carousel. |
| **Content Disclosure Setting** | Turn on if the video promotes goods or services in exchange for something of value. Then tick **Your brand** (promoting yourself, classified as Brand Organic), **Branded content** (promoting a third party, classified as Branded Content), or both. |

Under the options, TikTok's terms are shown: by posting you agree to TikTok's **Music Usage Confirmation**, and for branded content also its **Branded Content Policy**.

<Note>
Branded content can't be private. If you choose **Only you** with **Branded content**, the composer warns: "Branded content visibility cannot be set to private."
</Note>

## Trending sounds

Where available, the TikTok options show **Trending sound** for each account. Click **Search** to open the sound picker, filter by **Genre**, **Country** and **Trending over**, or search by title, artist or sound ID. Click **Use sound**, then **Change** or **Remove** later.

If an account shows "Reconnect this TikTok account (Accounts, Reconnect) to publish through the TikTok Business API and use trending sounds.", reconnect it on the **Accounts** page.

## Sent to the TikTok inbox

On some platforms, TikTok videos are sent to the account's TikTok inbox instead of being published directly. You then finish and publish them in the TikTok app, and the text is not sent. The composer shows a notice when this applies.

After publishing, it may take a few minutes for a TikTok post to process and appear on the profile.

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **It looks like you haven't chosen who can watch your video.** | Pick **Who can view this post** for each TikTok account. |
| **You need to indicate if your content promotes yourself, a third party, or both.** | With **Content Disclosure Setting** on, tick **Your brand** and/or **Branded content**. |
| **Add a video.** / "Select a video or up to 35 photos for this TikTok account." | Add media; text-only TikTok posts aren't possible. |
| "TikTok does not support mixing photos and videos in the same post." | Use either one video or photos. |
| "You've reached TikTok's posting limit for now." | Wait and publish again later. |
| "TikTok did not confirm this publication in time." | Check the TikTok account before publishing again, to avoid a duplicate. |
