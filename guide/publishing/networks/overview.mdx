Every network has its own rules for a post: how long the text can be, how many photos or videos it takes, and which options it needs. The composer knows these rules and checks your post against every account you selected. This page sums them up; each network has its own page with the details.

## Where network options appear

When you select an account, a box titled after the network (for example **Instagram options** or **TikTok options**) appears above the editor. Options belong to the version you are editing, so an account with its own [version](/guide/publishing/post-versions) can have different options from the original.

## Limits at a glance

| Network | Text | Media per post | Post types | Extra items |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [X](/guide/publishing/networks/x) | 280 characters, or up to 25,000 for Premium | 4 photos, 1 video or 1 GIF | Post | Thread |
| [Facebook Page](/guide/publishing/networks/facebook) | 5,000 | 10 photos, 1 video or 1 GIF | Post, Reel, Story, pre-recorded livestream | First comment |
| [Instagram](/guide/publishing/networks/instagram) | 2,200 | Up to 10, photos and video mixed | Post, Reel, Story | First comment |
| [Threads](/guide/publishing/networks/threads) | See the counter | Up to 20, mixed | Post, carousel | Thread |
| [LinkedIn](/guide/publishing/networks/linkedin) | 3,000 | 9 photos, 1 video or 1 GIF | Post, document, pre-recorded livestream | First comment, where available |
| [TikTok](/guide/publishing/networks/tiktok) | 2,200 | 1 video, or photos where enabled | Video, photo post | None |
| [YouTube](/guide/publishing/networks/youtube) | 5,000 (description) | 1 video | Video, pre-recorded livestream | First comment |
| [Pinterest](/guide/publishing/networks/pinterest) | 500 | 1 photo or 1 video | Pin | None |
| [Google Business Profile](/guide/publishing/networks/google-business-profile) | 1,500 | 1 photo | Post, Offer, Event | None |
| [Bluesky](/guide/publishing/networks/bluesky) | 300 | 4 photos, 1 video or 1 GIF | Post | Thread |
| [Mastodon](/guide/publishing/networks/mastodon-and-pixelfed) | Your server's limit (usually 500) | 4 photos, 1 video or 1 GIF | Post | Thread |
| [Pixelfed](/guide/publishing/networks/mastodon-and-pixelfed) | Your server's limit (usually 2,000) | Up to your server's limit (usually 20), mixed | Post, Story | Thread |
| [Reddit](/guide/publishing/networks/reddit) | 1,500 | 1 photo or 1 video | Post to subreddits or profile | None |
| [Tumblr](/guide/publishing/networks/tumblr) | 1,000 | 1 photo, video or GIF | Text, photo, quote, link, chat, video | None |
| [Telegram](/guide/publishing/networks/telegram-and-vkontakte) | 4,500 | 10 photos, 1 video or 1 GIF | Channel post | None |
| [VKontakte](/guide/publishing/networks/telegram-and-vkontakte) | 1,000 | 1 photo, video or GIF | Post | None |

Unless a network allows mixing, a post can contain only one kind of media: photos, a video or a GIF.

## Posting the same content to several accounts

X and LinkedIn do not accept identical content on several accounts. Once one of their accounts is selected, you can't add another account of the same network to that post; the account list explains: "... does not allow simultaneous posting of identical content to multiple accounts."

## What happens when a rule is broken

- **Errors** (too long, too many photos, a missing title or board) stop you from scheduling until they are fixed.
- **Warnings** tell you about something that will be left out, such as a first comment on a network that doesn't publish one.
- **Suggestions** are tips, such as moving a link to the first comment.

See [Preview and checks](/guide/publishing/preview-and-checks).

## Networks you can connect

The list of networks your workspace can connect depends on your platform. Connecting and reconnecting accounts is covered in [Social accounts](/guide/getting-started/social-accounts).

<Note>
Networks change their rules from time to time. If a post is refused even though the composer showed no error, the reason appears on the post's **Delivery** tab. See [Failed posts](/guide/publishing/failed-posts).
</Note>
