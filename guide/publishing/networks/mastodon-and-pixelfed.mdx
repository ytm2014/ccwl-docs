Mastodon and Pixelfed run on many independent servers. Some limits, such as the maximum length of a post, depend on the server your account is on, and the composer reads them from your account.

## Mastodon

### Limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Text | Your server's limit, usually 500 characters. |
| Media | Up to 4 photos, or 1 video, or 1 GIF. Media types can't be mixed. |
| Content | Text or media is required. |
| Extra items | Thread (**Add Post**). |
| Video cover | A thumbnail image can be set. |

### Mastodon options

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Visibility** | **Public**, **Quiet public**, **Followers only** or **Mentioned people only**. |
| **Who can quote** | **Anyone**, **Followers only** or **Just me**. |
| **Mark media as sensitive** | Hides the media behind a warning until people choose to see it. |

## Pixelfed

### Limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Text | Your server's limit, usually 2,000 characters. |
| Media | A post needs at least one photo or video, up to your server's limit (usually 20). Photos and videos can be mixed. |
| Story | Exactly one photo or video. |
| Extra items | Thread (**Add Post**). |

### Pixelfed options

Choose **Post** or **Story**:

| Type | Settings |
| --- | --- |
| **Post** | **Mark media as sensitive**. |
| **Story** | **Allow replies**, **Allow reactions**, and **Story duration (seconds)**: 3, 5, 10, 15 or 30 seconds. |

## Write a thread

On both networks, click **Add Post** under the editor to add the next post of the thread. See [Threads and first comments](/guide/publishing/threads-and-first-comments).

## Connecting

When you connect Mastodon or Pixelfed, you choose your server, or **Custom** to enter its address. See [Social accounts](/guide/getting-started/social-accounts#connect-an-account).

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Maximum of ... characters allowed.** | Your server allows fewer characters. Shorten the post or use a thread. |
| **Add a photo or a video.** | Pixelfed posts need media. |
| **A story needs exactly one photo or video.** | Keep one item for the Pixelfed Story. |

## Analytics

Mastodon and Pixelfed accounts have overview, engagement, content, hashtags and insights reports. See [Account analytics](/guide/publishing/account-analytics).
