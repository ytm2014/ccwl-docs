X posts are short and can be chained into threads. The composer counts characters the way X does and warns you before a post is too long.

## Limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Text | 280 characters. With **X Premium or Pro account** ticked (or a verified account), up to 25,000. |
| Media | Up to 4 photos, or 1 video, or 1 GIF. Media types can't be mixed. |
| Video length | Up to 140 seconds. |
| Content | Text or media is required. |
| Extra items | Thread (**Add Post**). |
| Several X accounts in one post | Not allowed: X refuses identical content on several accounts. |

X counts characters in its own way, for example links count as a fixed length. Trust the composer's counter rather than a plain character count.

## X options

When an X account is selected, **X options** offers one setting:

- **X Premium or Pro account**: tick it if the account has a paid X subscription. "Long posts are published in full instead of being cut to 280 characters."

## Write a thread

Click **Add Post** under the editor to add the next post of the thread. You can split, merge, reorder and number the posts. See [Threads and first comments](/guide/publishing/threads-and-first-comments).

If you paste a long text into an X post, the composer offers **Split into ... posts** to turn it into a thread automatically.

## X posts allowance

On some platforms, X posts are counted per period. When that applies, the X options show:

- **X posts: used / limit this period**, and how much the current post counts.
- "Posts with a link count as ...": a post with a link may count as more than one.
- When the allowance is used up: "Your X posts for this period are used up. Add X posts or connect your own X app."

Don't see this line? Your workspace doesn't count X posts. Add-ons for extra X posts are covered with your plan's add-ons.

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Maximum of 280 characters allowed.** | Shorten the post, split it into a thread, or tick **X Premium or Pro account** if the account has a subscription. |
| **Maximum of 4 photos allowed.** | Remove photos, or spread them over the thread. |
| **The video is longer than ...** | Trim the video to 140 seconds. |
| **Only ... posts can contain a link.** | Your allowance limits link posts. Remove links from some posts. |
| The account shows as disconnected | Reconnect it on the **Accounts** page. See [Social accounts](/guide/getting-started/social-accounts#reconnect-an-account). |

## Analytics

X accounts have no reports in **Analytics** yet. Publishing works as normal. See [Analytics](/guide/publishing/analytics).
