Instagram accounts can be connected through a Facebook Page or with an Instagram login. Both publish posts, carousels, Reels and Stories; a few options, such as audio for Reels, need the Facebook Page connection.

## Post types

Choose the type in **Instagram options**:

| Type | What it needs |
| --- | --- |
| **Post** | At least one photo or video. Up to 10 items, photos and video can be mixed (a carousel). |
| **Reel** | Exactly one video, 3 seconds to 15 minutes. |
| **Story** | Exactly one photo or video; a Story video can be up to 60 seconds. |

Some types are disabled when the media doesn't fit them, for example **Reel** without a video.

## Limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Text | 2,200 characters. |
| Hashtags | Up to 5 (not checked on Stories). More is an error. |
| First comment | Text only, no media. Not on Stories. |
| Video cover | Reels take a thumbnail image; feed videos and Stories use a frame of the video. See [Add media to a post](/guide/publishing/add-media#video-covers). |

Links in Instagram captions can't be clicked. The **Suggestions** tab reminds you to point people to the link in your bio.

## Instagram options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Collaborators** | "Invite up to 3 accounts. Once they accept, the post also appears on their profiles." Type each username and press Enter. Not on Stories. |
| **Audio** | For Reels: **Add audio** from Instagram's licensed audio (**Music**, **Original sounds**, **Trending audio**), with **Audio volume** and **Video volume**. Every selected Instagram account publishes the Reel with the same track. |

<Note>
Instagram only lets accounts connected through a Facebook Page add audio to a Reel. Its catalogue is smaller than in the Instagram app, because it lists only audio licensed for use outside the app.
</Note>

## Instagram extra options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Also share to Instagram Story** | "Posts and Reels. The post is also shared to the Story when it meets Instagram's Story rules." |
| **Publish as a trial Reel** | For Reels: "A trial Reel is shown to people who do not follow you first, so you can see how it does before your followers see it." Collaborators are removed. |
| **Graduation Strategy** | For trial Reels: **Manual** (you decide in Instagram) or **Auto (Performance-based)** (Instagram shows it to followers if it does well). |

## Tag people

Tag up to 10 Instagram usernames on each image: save the post, open the image's **More actions** and choose **Tag people**. See [Add media to a post](/guide/publishing/add-media#tag-people-on-instagram).

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Add a photo or a video.** | Instagram posts need media. |
| **Maximum of 5 hashtags allowed.** | Remove hashtags, or give the Instagram account its own [version](/guide/publishing/post-versions). |
| **A reel needs exactly one video.** | Keep one video, or choose **Post**. |
| **The first comment cannot include photos or videos.** | Remove media from the first comment. |
| "Use an Instagram username: letters, numbers, full stops and underscores, up to 30 characters." | Fix the collaborator's username. |
| "This account cannot add audio to a Reel." | Connect the account through its Facebook Page to use audio. |
| Analytics say insights aren't approved | Followers, posts and publishing still work. See [Account analytics](/guide/publishing/account-analytics). |
