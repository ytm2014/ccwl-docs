You publish to Facebook Pages you manage. A post can go out as a regular post, a Reel, a Story or a pre-recorded livestream.

## Post types

Choose the type in **Facebook page options**:

| Type | What it needs |
| --- | --- |
| **Post** | Text or media. Up to 10 photos, or 1 video, or 1 GIF (no mixing). |
| **Reel** | Exactly one video, 3 to 90 seconds long. |
| **Story** | Exactly one photo or video. No first comment. |

## Limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Text | 5,000 characters. |
| Hashtags | Up to 30 (not checked on Stories). |
| First comment | Text required; up to 1 photo or GIF. Not on Stories. |
| Video cover | A thumbnail image can be set on videos in a **Post**. |

## Facebook extra options

Under the type, **Facebook extra options** offers:

- **Pre-recorded livestream**: "Publishes one video as a live broadcast on the Page." The post must contain exactly one video. Then set:
  - **Livestream title**, up to 255 characters.
  - **Repeat the video**: **No repeat**, or 1 to 30 times.
- **Also share to Facebook Story**: "Posts only. The post is also shared to the Page's Story when it meets Facebook's Story rules." Not available for Reels, Stories or livestreams.

## Add a first comment

Click **Add first comment** under the editor to add a comment that is posted right under your post. See [Threads and first comments](/guide/publishing/threads-and-first-comments).

## Tips

- Facebook shows the first part of a long post and folds the rest. The **Preview** tab shows roughly where the fold falls.
- Post links in the first comment if you find link posts reach fewer people; the **Suggestions** tab can move them for you.

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **A reel needs exactly one video.** | Keep one video, or switch the type to **Post**. |
| **A story needs exactly one photo or video.** | Keep one item for the Story. |
| **The video is shorter/longer than ...** | Reels must be 3 to 90 seconds. |
| **Pre-recorded livestream requires a video file.** | Add exactly one video. |
| **Maximum of 30 hashtags allowed.** | Remove hashtags. |
| The Page shows as disconnected | Reconnect it on the **Accounts** page. |

## Analytics

Facebook Pages have a full report in **Analytics**: overview, engagement, video, content, hashtags and insights. See [Account analytics](/guide/publishing/account-analytics).
