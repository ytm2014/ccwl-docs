You can publish to LinkedIn personal profiles and to LinkedIn Pages. Besides regular posts, LinkedIn takes document posts (a PDF or PowerPoint shown as a swipeable document) and pre-recorded livestreams.

## Limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Text | 3,000 characters. |
| Media | Up to 9 photos, or 1 video, or 1 GIF. Media types can't be mixed. |
| Video length | 3 seconds to 30 minutes. |
| First comment | Where your platform supports it: text required, up to 1 photo or GIF. |
| Video cover | A thumbnail image can be set on videos in a post. |
| Several LinkedIn accounts in one post | Not allowed: LinkedIn refuses identical content on several accounts. |

## LinkedIn options (Pages)

For LinkedIn Pages, **LinkedIn options** has **Visibility**: **Public** or **Connections**.

## LinkedIn extra options

For both profiles and Pages, **LinkedIn extra options** offers **Publish as**:

### Post

A regular post. You can also attach a document:

1. Enter a **Document Title** (required for a document).
2. Click **Click to Upload Documents** and choose a PDF or PowerPoint file. "Post a PDF or PowerPoint file as a LinkedIn document."
3. To take it off again, click **Remove**.

A document post can't carry images or videos: "LinkedIn publishes the document only. Remove the media or the document."

### Pre-recorded Livestream

Publishes one video as a live broadcast. The post needs exactly one video ("A livestream needs a video in the post."). Choose a **Loop / Repeat Count**: **No repeat**, or 1 to 30 times.

## Add a first comment

Where available, click **Add first comment** under the editor. If the button is missing for LinkedIn, your platform doesn't publish LinkedIn first comments. See [Threads and first comments](/guide/publishing/threads-and-first-comments).

## Mentions

To tag a person or company page, use a saved [mention](/guide/publishing/mentions) and link it to the LinkedIn profile. LinkedIn's search needs at least 3 letters and no digits.

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Document Title is required** | Enter a title before uploading or publishing the document. |
| "Only PDF and PowerPoint files can be posted as a LinkedIn document." | Convert the file. |
| **The document could not be uploaded.** | Try again, or try a smaller file. |
| **Pre-recorded livestream requires a video file.** | Add exactly one video. |
| **The video is shorter/longer than ...** | Keep videos between 3 seconds and 30 minutes. |

## Analytics

LinkedIn Pages have reports for overview, engagement, content, hashtags and insights; profiles have overview and engagement. See [Account analytics](/guide/publishing/account-analytics).
