Each YouTube post uploads one video to your channel. The post text becomes the video description, and the YouTube options hold the title, privacy and tags.

## Limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Description (post text) | 5,000 characters. |
| Media | Exactly 1 video. Photos and GIFs aren't accepted. |
| Title | Required, up to 100 characters. |
| Video tags | Up to 30. |
| First comment | Text only, up to 10,000 characters. Not on **Made for kids** videos or livestreams. |
| Video cover | A thumbnail image can be set. See [Add media to a post](/guide/publishing/add-media#video-covers). |

## YouTube options

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | The video's title. Required. |
| **Privacy configuration** | **Public**, **Private** or **Unlisted**. |
| **Made for kids** | Marks the video as made for children. First comments are then not available. |

## YouTube extra options

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Publishing Mode** | **Video**: "Upload and publish as a standard YouTube video." **Pre-recorded Livestream**: "Broadcast uploaded video via RTMP as a live event." |
| **Loop / Repeat Count** | For livestreams: **No repeat**, or 1 to 30 times. |
| **Video Tags** | Type a tag and press Enter. "Use short, relevant tags. Up to 30 tags are allowed." |

## Add a first comment

Click **Add first comment** under the editor to post a comment under the video as soon as it is published. See [Threads and first comments](/guide/publishing/threads-and-first-comments).

## Tips

- Large videos take time to upload and process. Schedule them a little ahead of when they should appear.
- Use a separate [version](/guide/publishing/post-versions) if the YouTube description should differ from your other networks.

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Add a video.** | YouTube posts need one video. |
| **Add a title.** | Fill in the **Title**. |
| **The title can have at most 100 characters.** | Shorten the title. |
| "First comment is not available for made-for-kids videos." | Remove the first comment or untick **Made for kids**. |
| **A livestream needs a video in the post.** | Add the video to broadcast. |
