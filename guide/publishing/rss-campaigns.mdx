An RSS campaign watches a website's feed and creates posts from its new items, a set number per day. Each post is written from the item's title, description and link, depending on the switches you choose. Use it to share every new blog article or news item automatically.

In the sidebar, open **Posts** > **RSS Campaigns**.

<Note>
Don't see **RSS Campaigns**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet, or your role doesn't give you access. Admins have it; for other members, access comes with the role their admin gives them.
</Note>

## Create a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a campaign">
    Click **Create New Campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the feed">
    Under **Feed**, type the address in **Enter feed URL**. If no feed is found, you see **No feed was found on current URL**; check the address. A feed URL can only be used by one campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the accounts">
    Select the accounts to post to, and set any network options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the post parts">
    You can't type the text yourself. Use **Hide/Show Feed Title**, **Hide/Show Feed Description** and **Hide/Show Feed Link** to choose what goes in. **Items On Provided RSS Link** lets you look through the feed, and the **Preview** shows how the newest item will look. Keep at least one switch on, or posts would be empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the campaign details">
    Under **Campaign**, enter the **Name of Campaign**, the **Number of Posts to be made from this feed Daily** (1 to 24), and the **Order for Feeds Selection**: **All Posts** or **Most Recent Posts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Pick a time and click **Schedule**, click **Post now**, or click **Add to Queue** to use your [posting schedule](/guide/publishing/posting-schedule). You are taken back to the campaign list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Network notes

- **Reddit**: a post without a title of its own takes the feed item's title.
- **Google Business Profile**: an event or offer without start and end dates is posted as a standard update.

## Manage campaigns

The **RSS Campaigns** list shows each campaign's:

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaign Name (link)** | The name and the feed address. |
| **Accounts** | Where it posts. |
| **Last Time Fetched** | When the feed was last read. |
| **Numbers Published** | How many posts it has created. |
| **Posts per Day/Today's Posts** | The daily number and how many went out today. |
| **Posts Fetch Order** | **All Posts** or **Most Recent Posts**. |
| **Status** | **ACTIVE** or **INACTIVE**. |

Use **Search...** to find a campaign. From a row's **Actions** you can:

- **Edit** the campaign.
- **Set Active** or **Deactivate** it, after you confirm. A deactivated campaign stops creating posts.
- **Delete** it, after you confirm.

Click **View Posts** to see the posts your campaigns created in the posts list (filtered by **Source** > **RSS feed**).

## Tips

- Use **Most Recent Posts** to share only new items, and **All Posts** to work through older items too.
- Pair a campaign with the queue so feed posts fill your free posting times instead of clashing with your own posts.
- Check the first few posts after starting a campaign, and edit the campaign if the text or link isn't right.

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No feed was found on current URL** / "Provided URL does not contain feeds, please recheck URL" | Use the feed's own address (often ending in `/feed` or `.xml`), not the home page. |
| "An RSS campaign with this URL exists, campaign cannot be saved" | Edit the existing campaign instead. |
| "The title, description and link are all switched off, so posts would be empty." | Turn on at least one post part. |
| "RSS campaign status is currently inactive" | Click **Set Active**. |
