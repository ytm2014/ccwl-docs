Labels are coloured tags you put on posts, such as "Promotion", "Client A" or "Blog". They help you see at a glance what a post is for, and you can filter the [posts list](/guide/publishing/posts), the [calendar](/guide/publishing/calendar) and [shared calendars](/guide/publishing/shared-calendars) by them.

Labels belong to the workspace, so everyone sees the same set. Adding and changing labels needs the **Admin** or **Member** role.

## Add a label to a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Labels tab">
    In the composer, open the **Labels** tab in the side panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a label">
    Click **Add label**. A list of your labels opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick or create one">
    Type in **Search or Create New** to find a label and click it. If it doesn't exist yet, click **Create "..."** (or press Enter) to create it and add it to the post.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A post can have several labels. They show on the post's row in the posts list and on its card in the calendar.

## Remove a label from a post

On the **Labels** tab, click the **x** on the label (**Remove**). The label itself stays available for other posts.

## Change a label's colour

Click a label's colour on the **Labels** tab (**Change label color**) and pick a new one. The colour changes everywhere the label is used.

## Delete a label

Open the label's menu on the **Labels** tab and choose **Delete label**. You are asked "Are you sure you want to delete the ... label from everywhere?". Confirming removes it from every post that has it.

<Warning>
Deleting a label removes it from every post that has it, and the posts themselves stay as they are.
</Warning>

## Filter by label

- **Posts list**: open **Filters** and pick labels under **Labels**. See [Posts list](/guide/publishing/posts#search-filter-and-sort).
- **Calendar**: use the calendar's **Filters** the same way.
- **Shared calendars**: choose labels under **Filter Posts by Tags** so a client sees only those posts. See [Shared calendars](/guide/publishing/shared-calendars).

## Ideas for labels

- One label per client, campaign or product line.
- A label for each content type, such as "Video", "Blog" or "Offer".
- A "Needs review" label for drafts that a teammate should look at before they are scheduled.
