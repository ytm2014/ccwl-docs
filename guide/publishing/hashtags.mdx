You can type hashtags straight into a post, but if you use the same sets again and again, save them as hashtag groups in the **Hashtag Manager** and insert a whole group with one click.

## Create a hashtag group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Hashtag Manager">
    In the composer, click the **#** button in the editor toolbar (**Open Hashtag Manager**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a group">
    Click **New Hashtag Group**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the group">
    Enter a **Hashtag Group Name**, such as "Product launch", and the **Hashtag Group Content**: the hashtags themselves, for example `#launch #newproduct #smallbusiness`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Hashtag Group**, then **Done** to close the manager.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Hashtag groups are shared by everyone in the workspace.

## Insert a group

1. Put the cursor where the hashtags should go.
2. Open the **Hashtag Manager**.
3. Click **Insert** next to the group.

The hashtags are added to the text of the version you are editing.

## Edit or delete a group

In the **Hashtag Manager**, open the group's **More actions** menu and choose **Edit** or **Delete**. Use **Go Back** to return to the list.

## Hashtag limits by network

| Network | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram | At most 5 hashtags in a post (Stories excepted). More is an error, and the post can't be scheduled. |
| Facebook Page | At most 30 hashtags in a post (Stories excepted). |
| Bluesky | Up to 8 **Hidden tags** in the Bluesky options, in addition to hashtags in the text. See [Bluesky](/guide/publishing/networks/bluesky). |
| YouTube | Up to 30 **Video Tags** in the YouTube options. See [YouTube](/guide/publishing/networks/youtube). |

If a post goes over a limit, the error appears above the editor and on the version's tab: "Maximum of 5 hashtags allowed."

## Hashtag suggestions

The **Suggestions** tab of the composer can give hashtag tips as you write:

- When a network works best with fewer hashtags, it says so, for example "works best with no more than ... hashtags".
- When a post has no hashtags on a network where people search by hashtag, it suggests adding some.
- Where AI is available, **Suggest hashtags** asks AI for hashtags that fit your text. Review them, ask for changes in the box ("Ask for changes, like "more specific""), and click **Add these hashtags**. Each request uses 1 AI credit.

See [Preview and checks](/guide/publishing/preview-and-checks#suggestions).

## Tips

- Keep groups small and focused. You can insert several groups into one post.
- Give an account its own [version](/guide/publishing/post-versions) when one network needs fewer hashtags, for example Instagram.
- A hashtag that sits in a [variable](/guide/publishing/variables) or [template](/guide/publishing/templates) is inserted as text like any other.
