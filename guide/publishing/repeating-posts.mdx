A repeating post goes out on its scheduled date and then again every so many days, until the end date you set. Use it for evergreen content such as opening hours, a standing offer or a reminder.

## Make a post repeat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the schedule window">
    In the composer, click **Schedule** and pick the first date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on repeating">
    Tick **Repeat this post**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how often">
    Under **Repeat Frequency (Days)**, choose every 1 to 60 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it ends">
    Under **Repeat Until**, set the date and time of the last repeat. It must be after the first scheduled time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    Click **Done**, then **Schedule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If something is missing, the window tells you: "Choose how often the post repeats.", "Choose the date and time the repetition ends." or "The repetition must end after the post is scheduled."

## See upcoming repeats

- On the [calendar](/guide/publishing/calendar), future dates show as cards marked **Upcoming repeat**. Clicking one opens the original post. Upcoming repeats can't be dragged.
- In the posts list, filter by **Source** > **Repeating** to find posts created by a repetition.

## Manage repetitions

In the sidebar, open **Posts** > **All Posts** and click the **Repeated Posts** tab. The table lists each repetition with its:

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Frequency** | "Every ... day(s)". |
| **Start Date** and **End Date** | When the repetition runs. |
| **Content** | The post's text and accounts. |
| **View** | The **Dates this post is scheduled for**. |
| **Date Created** | When the repetition was set up. |

From a row's menu you can:

- **Edit Post Repetition**: change the **Repeat Frequency (Days)** and **Repeat Until**, then **Submit**.
- **Delete Post Repetition**: stops the repetition after you confirm. You can choose to **Also delete all posts in this repetition**.

If you have no repeating posts, the tab shows **Repeated posts not available**.

## Tips

- Watch for networks that don't like identical content posted often. For those, schedule a few differently worded posts instead.
- Bulk posts can repeat too, with a number of repetitions and an interval in the CSV file. See [Bulk posts](/guide/publishing/bulk-posts).
