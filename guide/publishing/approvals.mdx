Approvals let someone check a post before it is published. A post waiting for a decision has the status **Needs approval** and won't go out until it is approved. Approvers can work inside the workspace, and clients can approve or reject posts from a [shared calendar](/guide/publishing/shared-calendars) without signing in.

## Who needs approval

Whether a team member can approve posts depends on the role their workspace admin gives them. See [Team and roles](/guide/account/roles-and-permissions).

| Member | What happens when they schedule |
| --- | --- |
| Can approve | The post is scheduled straight away. They also see **Post now**, **Add to Queue** and **Approve**. |
| Can't approve | They only see **Schedule**. The post is saved as **Needs approval** and waits. A badge on the button explains: "Post must be approved before it can be published." |

A member without a role has no post approval access.

## Ask for approval on purpose

Even if you can approve, you can ask a colleague to review a post first:

1. In the composer, click **Schedule** and pick the time.
2. Tick **Post must be approved before it can be published**.
3. Click **Done**, then **Schedule**.

The post waits as **Needs approval** instead of being scheduled.

## Find posts waiting for approval

- **Posts list**: the **Needs approval** tab appears when there are posts waiting, with a count. See [Posts list](/guide/publishing/posts).
- **Home**: the attention bar shows "... posts are waiting for approval". See [Home](/guide/publishing/home).
- **Calendar**: waiting posts show their status on their cards.

## Approve a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the post">
    Open it from the **Needs approval** tab or the calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review it">
    Check the text, media, accounts and time. Leave a comment on the **Activity** tab if something needs changing. See [Comments and activity](/guide/publishing/comments-and-activity).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Click **Approve**. The post is scheduled for its time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The post's activity records who scheduled it for approval and who approved it, for example "... approved scheduling this post for ...".

If you don't approve a post, edit it or leave a comment for its author; it stays in **Needs approval** until someone approves it or moves it back to drafts.

## Let clients approve from a shared calendar

A shared calendar with **Enable/Disable Post Approval** set to **Allowed** lets anyone with the link approve or reject posts that need approval:

1. The client opens the link and clicks a post.
2. They choose **Approve Post** or **Reject Post** and confirm.
3. An approved post is scheduled for its time. Either way, your team is notified by email ("Post Approved" or "Post Not Approved"), so you can follow up on a rejected post.

Posts whose scheduled time has already passed can't be approved from the shared calendar. See [Shared calendars](/guide/publishing/shared-calendars).

<Tip>
A common set-up: your team members can't approve, so everything they schedule waits as **Needs approval**, and the client approves posts from a shared calendar with post approval allowed.
</Tip>
