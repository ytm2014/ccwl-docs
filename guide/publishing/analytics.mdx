**Analytics** shows how your social accounts are doing. The **Summary** compares all your accounts at once; each account also has its own report with figures from its network. Post-level figures are on each post. This page covers the summary and how figures are collected.

In the sidebar, click **Analytics**.

## The layout

The list on the left shows **Summary** and every account that supports analytics. Use the search box to find an account. Click an account to open its report; see [Account analytics](/guide/publishing/account-analytics).

## Choose a period

Use the period picker at the top right:

- **This week**, **Last week**
- **This month** (the default), **Last month**
- **Last 3 months**, **This year**
- **Custom**: choose a start and end date and click **Apply date range**.

Changes are compared with the period before, for example this month against last month.

## Summary

**Summary** compares your accounts for the period:

| Figure | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total followers** | Followers across all accounts. |
| **Follower growth** | How many followers you gained or lost. |
| **Posts published** | Posts that went out in the period. |
| **Top performing account** | The account that did best. |
| **Needs attention** | Accounts to look at. |
| **Combined audience** | Your total audience across networks. |
| **Most active** / **Least active** | The accounts you posted to most and least. |
| **Posting consistency** | How regularly you posted. |
| **Account leaderboard** | Your accounts ranked side by side. |
| **Follower distribution** | How your followers are split between accounts. |

## How figures are collected

- When an account is first connected, its figures are imported in the background. A notice says the import is in progress; if you see no data yet, try a different period.
- Some networks only provide posts from a recent number of days; a notice tells you how many.
- Follower growth starts from the date the account was connected.
- If an account loses its connection, a notice asks you to re-authenticate it, and its figures stop updating until you do. See [Social accounts](/guide/getting-started/social-accounts#reconnect-an-account).

## Networks without analytics

Some networks have no reports in <AppName /> yet, for example X. Choosing such an account shows that analytics are on the way. Scheduling and publishing keep working as normal, and you don't need to do anything.

## Where else you see figures

- **Home** turns your figures into a weekly recap, your best posts and your best time to post. See [Home](/guide/publishing/home).
- **Post analytics** shows how one post did on each account. See [Post analytics](/guide/publishing/post-analytics).
- **Best times to post** in the schedule window uses your past results. See [Schedule or publish a post](/guide/publishing/schedule-a-post#best-times-to-post).
