Two pages tell you how your publishing is going. **Home** is a quick daily overview of your schedule and recent results. **Analytics** goes deeper, with a summary across all accounts and a report for each account.

## Home

In the sidebar, click **Home**. Until your workspace is set up, Home shows **Finish setup** with two steps: **Connect your first account** and **Write your first post**. If an administrator connects the accounts, you see **Nothing to publish to yet** and can start drafting meanwhile.

Once set up, Home shows these cards:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Attention bar | Things to deal with: posts that failed, posts waiting for approval, disconnected accounts, and messages waiting for a reply. |
| **Content runway** | How many days ahead already have a post waiting, over the next two weeks. |
| **This week** | The last seven days against the seven before: posts published, follower growth and your best post. |
| **Reuse a winner** | Your strongest posts of the last 90 days, with **Post it again**. |
| **Consistency** | How many of the recent days had a post, as a heatmap with your streak. |
| **Today & tomorrow** | What is planned, with free slots you can fill and **Open the calendar**. |
| **Best time to post** | The soonest hour your posts tend to do well in that has nothing scheduled yet. Appears once your accounts have published posts with imported statistics. |
| **Team activity** | Recent activity by your teammates. |

Depending on your plan, Home can also show a **Bio Links** card and a **Salesbot this week** card for the sales inbox.

## Open analytics

In the sidebar, click **Analytics**. The list on the left shows **Summary** and every account that supports analytics; use the search box to find one.

When an account is first connected, its figures are imported in the background. A notice says the import is in progress; if you see no data, try a different period. Some accounts only import posts from a recent number of days, and a notice tells you how many.

## Choose a period

Use the period picker at the top right: **This week**, **Last week**, **This month** (the default), **Last month**, **Last 3 months**, **This year**, or **Custom** with a start and end date and **Apply date range**.

## Summary

**Summary** compares all your accounts for the period: **Total followers**, **Follower growth**, **Posts published**, the **Top performing account**, accounts under **Needs attention**, your **Combined audience**, **Most active** and **Least active** accounts, **Posting consistency**, an **Account leaderboard** and the **Follower distribution**.

## Account reports

Click an account to see its report. Tabs across the top split it into sections. Which tabs you see depends on the network:

| Network | Tabs |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram | Overview, Engagement, Audience, Reach, Content, Hashtags, Insights, Competitors |
| Facebook Page | Overview, Engagement, Video, Content, Hashtags, Insights |
| LinkedIn Page | Overview, Engagement, Content, Hashtags, Insights |
| LinkedIn profile | Overview, Engagement |
| Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, Pixelfed | Overview, Engagement, Content, Hashtags, Insights |
| Pinterest | Overview, Content, Hashtags, Insights |
| TikTok, YouTube | Overview, Content, Insights |
| Google Business Profile | Overview, Engagement, Content, Search terms, Reviews |

**Competitors** is only offered for Instagram accounts connected through a Facebook Page.

Examples of the figures in each overview:

- **Instagram**: followers, reach, views, accounts engaged, total interactions, follows and unfollows, likes, comments, shares, saves.
- **Facebook Page**: total followers, media views, post engagements, page views, new follows, unfollows, reactions, video views.
- **LinkedIn**: followers, impressions, clicks or reactions, comments, shares, page views (pages) or members reached (profiles).
- **YouTube**: total subscribers, views, likes, comments.
- **TikTok**: total followers, views, likes, comments, shares.
- **Pinterest**: total followers, impressions, engagement, pin clicks, outbound clicks, saves and their rates.
- **Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon**: total followers and the network's own interactions (likes or favourites, replies, reposts or reblogs, quotes).
- **Google Business Profile**: Maps and Search impressions, website clicks, phone calls, direction requests, bookings, the search terms people used, and your reviews.

Follower growth starts from the date the account was connected.

<Note>
If Instagram hasn't approved detailed insights for an account yet, its report says so. Followers, posts and publishing still work, and the figures appear once insights are approved and the account is reconnected.
</Note>

If an account has lost its connection, a notice asks you to re-authenticate it; until then its figures stop updating.

## Networks without analytics

Some networks have no reports in <AppName /> yet, for example X. Choosing such an account shows that analytics are on the way. Scheduling and publishing to it keep working as normal, and you don't need to do anything.

## Analytics for a single post

Open a published post and choose **Analytics** in the composer's side panel to see that post's figures. See [Create a post](/guide/publishing/create-a-post#the-side-panel).
