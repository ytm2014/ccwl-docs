Importing copies recent posts from a connected Facebook Page, Instagram or TikTok account into your workspace. Imported posts appear in your posts list, where you can reuse them as the starting point for new posts.

## Import posts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the import dialog">
    In the sidebar, open **Posts** > **All Posts** and click **Import Existing Posts** at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the account">
    Under **Select Account**, pick a Facebook Page, Instagram or TikTok account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how many">
    Under **Number of Posts to Import**, choose how many recent posts to bring in, up to 60.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Click **Import**. A message confirms "Posts imported successfully".
  </Step>
</Steps>

The button only appears when your workspace has an account it can import from. If none is connected, the dialog says "Connect a Facebook Page, Instagram or TikTok account to import its posts."

## Find imported posts

In the posts list, open **Filters** > **Source** and choose **Imported**.

## Reuse an imported post

Imported posts are already published, so they open read-only. To post one again:

1. Open its **More actions** menu and choose **Duplicate**.
2. Open the copy, adjust the text, media and accounts.
3. Schedule it. See [Schedule or publish a post](/guide/publishing/schedule-a-post).

<Tip>
**Home** also suggests strong posts to send again in **Reuse a winner**, with **Post it again**. See [Home](/guide/publishing/home).
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Please select both account and number of posts** | Choose both before clicking **Import**. |
| **The account needs to be reconnected. Re-authenticate it and try again.** | Reconnect the account on the **Accounts** page, then import again. |
| **The posts could not be imported. Please try again.** | The network didn't return the posts. Try again later. |
