Links can go anywhere in your text. The composer can shorten them, use one of your own short-link accounts, and suggest where a link works best on each network.

## Shorten links

When your platform has a link shortener, the editor toolbar has a link button:

1. Click it to switch **URL shortening: On**. The links in the post are shortened.
2. Click it again to switch **URL shortening: Off**.

The setting applies to the post you are writing.

## Use your own short-link account

If your workspace has added a short-link account, a second link button appears in the toolbar (**Choose a short-link account**):

1. Click it. The menu lists your **Short-link account**s.
2. Pick one. The links in this post are shortened with that account when it is published, and the button shows its name.
3. To stop using it, choose **Do not use a short-link account**.

**Add a short-link account** takes you to the page where your workspace's short-link accounts are managed.

## Where to put a link

Networks treat links differently. The **Suggestions** tab points this out as you write:

- **Instagram and other caption-only networks**: links in captions can't be clicked. The suggestion says "Links in ... captions cannot be clicked. Point people to the link in your bio instead."
- **Networks that reach fewer people with links**: the suggestion says "Posts with a link can reach fewer people on ... Put the link in the first comment instead." Click **Move the link to the first comment** to move it; a message confirms "The link was moved to the first comment" and lets you undo it. See [Threads and first comments](/guide/publishing/threads-and-first-comments).
- **Pinterest**: add the link in the **Link** field of the Pinterest options, so the Pin opens it.
- **Google Business Profile**: use a call-to-action **Button** with a **Button link**, or an **Offer Link** on offers.
- **Reddit and Tumblr**: use their **Link** fields for link posts.

## Link previews

The composer's **Preview** tab shows a preview card for a link on networks that display one, such as X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Threads and Bluesky. The card is built by the network from the page's own title and image, so the real post can differ. Previews are approximate.

## X: posts with links

If your workspace counts X posts, the X options show **X posts: used / limit this period** and how much this post counts. Posts with a link can count as more than one post ("Posts with a link count as ..."). See [X](/guide/publishing/networks/x).

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Enter a valid link, starting with https://.** | A link field (Pinterest, Google Business Profile) needs a full address. |
| **Only {count} posts can contain a link.** | Your X allowance limits link posts. Remove the link from some posts. |
| **Links are not allowed.** | The network or allowance doesn't accept links here. Remove the link. |
