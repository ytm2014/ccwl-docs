Images, GIFs and videos are added to each post (and to each item of a thread) from the composer. The media itself lives in your [media library](/guide/publishing/media-library), so the same file can be used in many posts.

## Add media

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the picker">
    Under the text, click **Add Media**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the files">
    Pick files from **Upload** (your library), or use another tab such as **Stock Photos**, **GIFs**, **Canva**, **Google Drive** or **Dropbox**. See [Media sources](/guide/publishing/media-sources).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert">
    Click **Insert** (it shows how many items you chose). The files appear as tiles under the text.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Drag the tiles to change their order. Hover over a tile and click **Remove** to take it out of the post; the file stays in your library.

A video that is still being processed shows a processing state on its tile. You can schedule the post; it is published once the video is ready.

<Note>
Each network limits how many photos, videos and GIFs a post can have, and whether you can mix them. If you go over, the composer shows an error such as "Maximum of 4 photos allowed." or "Combining different media types (videos, photos, GIFs) is not allowed." See [Networks: options and limits](/guide/publishing/networks/overview).
</Note>

## Media actions

Hover over a media tile and open **More actions**. What you see depends on the file and the selected networks:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit alt text** | Describes the image for people using screen readers. |
| **Add video thumbnail** / **Change video thumbnail** | Picks an image as the video's cover. |
| **View video thumbnail** | Shows the cover you chose, with **Remove**. |
| **Choose frame from video** / **Change frame** | Picks a moment of the video as its cover. |
| **Tag people** | Tags Instagram users on an image. |
| **Create for Adobe Express** | Opens the image in Adobe Express, when your platform offers it. |

Tiles with alt text or a video cover show a small badge.

## Alt text

1. Choose **Edit alt text** on an image.
2. Write a description under **Alt text**. The dialog says which of your networks use it ("Add an alt text for ...").
3. Click **Save**. Where AI is available, **Generate with AI** writes a first draft for you.

Alt text is saved on the file, so it is reused when you add the same image to another post. If an image has none, the **Suggestions** tab reminds you with **Add alt text**.

## Video covers

Networks handle covers in two ways, and the composer offers the right one:

- **An image cover** (**Add video thumbnail**): used by YouTube, LinkedIn posts, Facebook posts, Instagram Reels, Pinterest, Mastodon and Telegram. The dialog says "The video thumbnail is available across" and lists your networks.
- **A frame of the video** (**Choose frame from video**): used by TikTok and by Instagram feed videos and Stories. The dialog makes frames from the video; click one and choose **Use this frame**. The frame is also used as the image cover on the networks listed in the dialog. **Remove cover** clears it.

If frames cannot be made from a video, the dialog says "Frames could not be made from this video." Try another file.

## Tag people on Instagram

1. Save the post first: the option says **Save the post first, then tag people.** until it has been saved.
2. Choose **Tag people** on an image.
3. Type each Instagram username and press Enter, up to 10 per image.
4. Click **Save**.

Tags are used by Instagram accounts only; other networks ignore them.

## Media credits

When you use a stock photo or GIF, the composer can add a credit to the text, such as "Photo by ... on ...", as the source asks.

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **The {format} file is not supported.** | Convert the file to one of the formats listed in the message. |
| **A reel needs exactly one video.** | Keep one video in the post, or choose a different post type. |
| **A story needs exactly one photo or video.** | Keep one item for the Story. |
| **The video is shorter/longer than ...** | Trim the video to the network's allowed length. |
| **A video in this post had not finished processing an hour after it was uploaded...** | The video never finished processing, so the post was not published. Publish again once it shows as ready in the library. |
