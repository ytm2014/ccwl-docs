Each social account that supports analytics has its own report, built from the figures its network provides. Tabs across the top split the report into sections, and the tabs differ by network.

In the sidebar, click **Analytics** and pick the account in the list on the left. Choose the period at the top right; see [Analytics overview](/guide/publishing/analytics#choose-a-period).

## Tabs by network

| Network | Tabs |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram | Overview, Engagement, Audience, Reach, Content, Hashtags, Insights, Competitors |
| Facebook Page | Overview, Engagement, Video, Content, Hashtags, Insights |
| LinkedIn Page | Overview, Engagement, Content, Hashtags, Insights |
| LinkedIn profile | Overview, Engagement |
| Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, Pixelfed | Overview, Engagement, Content, Hashtags, Insights |
| Pinterest | Overview, Content, Hashtags, Insights |
| TikTok, YouTube | Overview, Content, Insights |
| Google Business Profile | Overview, Engagement, Content, Search terms, Reviews |

**Competitors** is only offered for Instagram accounts connected through a Facebook Page.

## What the tabs contain

- **Overview**: the headline figures for the period, with the change against the period before.
- **Engagement**: likes, comments, shares and the network's other interactions over time.
- **Content**: your posts in the period, which you can sort to find the best and worst performers.
- **Hashtags**: the hashtags you used and how posts with them performed.
- **Insights**: further figures and highlights for the account.
- **Audience**, **Reach**, **Video**, **Search terms**, **Reviews**, **Competitors**: sections that only some networks have.

## Figures by network

| Network | Examples of figures |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram | Followers, reach, views, accounts engaged, total interactions, follows and unfollows, likes, comments, shares, saves. |
| Facebook Page | Total followers, media views, post engagements, page views, new follows, unfollows, reactions, video views. |
| LinkedIn | Followers, impressions, clicks or reactions, comments, shares, page views (Pages) or members reached (profiles). |
| YouTube | Total subscribers, views, likes, comments. |
| TikTok | Total followers, views, likes, comments, shares. |
| Pinterest | Total followers, impressions, engagement, pin clicks, outbound clicks, saves and their rates. |
| Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, Pixelfed | Total followers and the network's own interactions (likes or favourites, replies, reposts or reblogs, quotes). |
| Google Business Profile | Maps and Search impressions, website clicks, phone calls, direction requests, bookings, the search terms people used, and your reviews. |

Hover over a figure's info icon to read how the network defines it.

## Instagram insights not approved yet

If Instagram hasn't approved detailed insights for an account, its report says "Instagram hasn't approved detailed insights for this account yet." Followers, posts and publishing still work. The figures appear once insights are approved and the account is reconnected.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A notice that the import is in progress | Wait for the first import to finish, or try another period. |
| No data for the period | Choose a longer period; some networks only provide recent posts. |
| A notice to re-authenticate | Reconnect the account on the **Accounts** page. |
| Analytics are on the way | The network has no reports yet. Publishing isn't affected. |
