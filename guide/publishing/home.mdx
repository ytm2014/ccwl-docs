**Home** is the first page you see in a workspace. It brings together what needs your attention, what is planned, and how your recent posts did, so you can decide what to do next.

In the sidebar, click **Home**.

## Finish setup

Until your workspace is ready, Home shows **Finish setup** with two steps and a counter ("1 of 2 done"):

1. **Connect your first account**, with **Connect an account**.
2. **Write your first post**, with **Compose a post**.

If you aren't an Admin and no account is connected yet, you see **Nothing to publish to yet**: "An administrator is connecting the social accounts. You can start drafting in the meantime."

## The cards

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Attention bar | Things to deal with: posts that failed to publish, posts waiting for approval, disconnected accounts, and messages waiting for a reply (with the oldest waiting time). |
| **Content runway** | How many of the next two weeks' days already have a post waiting, per account or for **All accounts**. "Out of content" when nothing is scheduled. Includes a **Fill the queue** button. |
| **This week** | The last seven days against the seven before: posts published, **Follower growth**, and your best post with its engagement rate. |
| **Reuse a winner** | Your strongest posts of the last 90 days, with **Post it again** and **View the original**. |
| **Consistency** | How many of the recent days had a post, as a heatmap, with your current streak. |
| **Today & tomorrow** | What is planned, with **Free slot · schedule a post** where your posting schedule has a gap, and **Open the calendar**. |
| **Best time to post** | The soonest hour your posts tend to do well in that has nothing scheduled yet, with other good hours, **Schedule a post** and **Tune your schedule**. |
| **Team activity** | Recent comments, scheduled and published posts by your teammates. If you work alone, **Add teammates**. |

Depending on your plan, Home can also show a **Bio Links** card (views, clicks and click rate for the last 7 days) and a card for the sales inbox.

## Act from Home

- Click **Post it again** on a strong post to open it as a new post in the composer.
- Click a free slot to start a post for that time.

## When cards are empty

Several cards need history before they show anything:

- **This week**: "Not enough data yet — come back once a few posts have gone out."
- **Reuse a winner**: "No performance data yet."
- **Best time to post**: "A suggestion appears once your accounts have published posts with imported statistics."

Statistics are imported from the networks that provide them. See [Analytics](/guide/publishing/analytics).
