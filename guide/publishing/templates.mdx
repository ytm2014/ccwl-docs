Templates hold text and media you reuse, such as a weekly offer or a standard announcement. Start a new post from a template, or insert one into the post you are writing.

Templates are for members with the **Admin** or **Member** role. In the sidebar, open **Templates**.

## Create a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a template">
    On the **Templates** page, click **Create Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Template name**. It is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the content">
    Write the text and add media. The editor has emoji, the **Hashtag Manager**, the **Variable manager** and, when AI is available, **AI Assist**. A template can hold several posts, for example a thread; each shows as **Post #1**, **Post #2** and so on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save template**, or **Save and create new** to save it and start another.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Variables such as the account name are filled in for each account when the post goes out, so one template can serve many accounts.
</Tip>

## Save a post as a template

While writing a post, open the **Template manager** in the composer toolbar and click **Save As Template**. Give it a **Template name** and click **Save template**. The template takes the content of the version you are looking at.

## Use a template

- On the **Templates** page, click **Use** on a template. A new post opens with the template's content.
- From an open template, click **Use template**.
- In the composer, open the **Template manager** and click **Use** to insert a template into the current post.

After that, edit the post as usual: choose accounts, adjust the text and schedule it. Changing the post does not change the template.

## Edit or delete a template

On the **Templates** page, open a template's **More actions** menu and choose **Edit** or **Delete**. When editing, you can also delete it with the bin button, or click **Create new** to start another template.

<Warning>
Deleting a template removes it straight away. Posts already created from it are not affected.
</Warning>
