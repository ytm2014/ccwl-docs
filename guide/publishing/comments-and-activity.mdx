Every post has an **Activity** tab where your team can talk about it and see what happened to it: who created it, scheduled it, approved it, and how publishing went. Use it to ask for changes, answer questions and keep the decisions next to the post.

## Open the activity

Open a saved post in the composer and click the **Activity** tab in the side panel. A brand-new post shows its activity once it has been saved.

## Comment on a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write the comment">
    Type in **Write a comment...** at the bottom of the tab. Mention a teammate with `@` so they are notified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Click **Send**, or use the keyboard shortcut shown on the button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Comments show the author and time. To answer a comment, click **Reply**; replies are grouped in a thread under it ("... replies"), and the thread opens in its own view (**Thread by** ...). You can add a reaction to a comment, and delete your own comment with **Delete comment** after you confirm.

## Watch a post

The bell next to the tab title holds the post's **Notification settings**:

- **Watch**: "Notify me on all activity for this task."
- **Unwatch**: "Notify me only on mentions."

New comments and changes on watched posts reach you through the notifications bell in the top bar and, depending on your notification settings, by email.

## What the history shows

Along with comments, the tab lists what happened to the post:

| Entry | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| "... created this post" | Who started it. |
| "... scheduled this post for ..." | Who scheduled it and for when. |
| "... scheduled this post for ... and submitted it for approval" | The post was scheduled by someone who can't approve it. See [Approvals](/guide/publishing/approvals). |
| "... approved scheduling this post for ..." | Who approved it. |
| "... updated the schedule time from ... to ..." | The time was changed. |
| "... moved this post to drafts" | The scheduled time was cleared. |
| **Publishing process initiated** | Publishing has started. |
| **Post successfully published** | It went out, per account. |
| **Publishing process failed** | It failed on an account. See [Failed posts](/guide/publishing/failed-posts). |
| **Post deleted from social platforms** | It was removed from the networks. |
| "The video in this post has finished processing, and a platform will not accept it as it is..." | A scheduled post's video breaks a network's rules. Fix it before the post is due. |

Older activity may be cleared after a while; a note at the top of the tab says so when it applies.

## Comments from clients

If a shared calendar allows comments, a client can click a post and **Leave a comment** with their name. The comment is sent to your team. See [Shared calendars](/guide/publishing/shared-calendars#what-the-recipient-sees).

## See who else is working

- When a teammate has the same post open, their avatar shows in the composer header.
- If they save a change while you are editing, you are told your screen is out of date, with **Refresh**.
- **Home** has a **Team activity** card with recent comments, scheduled and published posts. See [Home](/guide/publishing/home).

## Tips

- Use comments rather than editing someone else's text when you want them to make a change.
- Combine with [approvals](/guide/publishing/approvals): reviewers comment, the author fixes, the reviewer clicks **Approve**.
