Deleting a post first moves it to the trash, where you can restore it. For posts that have been published, you also choose whether to delete them from the social networks themselves.

You need the **Admin** or **Member** role to delete or restore posts.

## Delete one post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    In the posts list, open the post's **More actions** menu and choose **Delete**. You can also delete it from the menu in the composer header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where to delete from">
    For a post that has been published, choose:
    - **From application only**: moves it to the trash; it stays on the networks.
    - **From application and social platforms**: removes it from the networks and moves it to the trash.
    - **From social platforms only**: removes it from the networks; the post stays in your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A post that was never published just asks "Are you sure you want to delete selected posts?".

If a network doesn't support deleting that kind of post, the dialog tells you, for example "... doesn't support deleting ...". The post then stays on that network.

<Warning>
Deleting from the social platforms removes the post from the network itself. That can't be undone here.
</Warning>

## Delete several posts

1. In the posts list, tick the posts.
2. Click the bin button (**Delete**) in the bar that appears.
3. Choose where to delete from and confirm.

A message confirms the result, such as "Selected posts moved to Trash" or "Selected posts deleted from social platforms".

## Find posts in the trash

In the posts list, open **Filters** and switch on **Trash**. Trashed posts open read-only ("Posts in trash cannot be edited.").

## Restore a post

In the trash, open the post's **More actions** menu and choose **Restore**. The post returns to the list ("Post restored").

## Delete permanently

Deleting a post that is already in the trash deletes it permanently ("Post deleted permanently").

## Deleting repeating posts

When you delete a post that belongs to a repetition, the dialog offers to **Also delete repetition schedule and all posts created from it**. To manage repetitions themselves, use the **Repeated Posts** tab: **Delete Post Repetition** stops it, and you can choose to delete the posts created from it too. See [Repeating posts](/guide/publishing/repeating-posts).
