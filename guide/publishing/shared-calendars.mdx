A shared calendar is a link to a calendar of selected accounts and dates. Anyone with the link can open it without signing in to <AppName />. Use it to show a client what is planned and, if you allow it, let them approve or reject posts.

## Create a shared calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the shared calendars">
    In the sidebar, open **Calendar** and click **Shared Calendar**. The list shows your existing shared calendars.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new one">
    Click **Create Shared Calendar Setup**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    Set the fields below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the link">
    Click **Enable Link Sharing**. The calendar is saved and its link created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Shared calendar name** | A name to recognise it by. Only you and your team see it. |
| **Start Date** | The starting date of the calendar to be shared. |
| **End Date** | The final date that will be displayed. |
| **Filter by Profile** | The accounts whose posts appear. **Search Accounts**, or pick a group under **Select Group (optional)**. Pick at least one account. |
| **Filter Posts by Tags** | Only show posts with these labels. |
| **Enable/Disable Post Approval** | **Allowed** lets people with the link approve or reject posts. **Not allowed** makes the calendar view-only. |
| **Shared calendar expiration date** | The date the link stops working. |

## Manage shared calendars

The shared calendar list shows each calendar's **Accounts**, **Labels**, **Link Sharing**, **Post Approval**, **Expiration Date** and **Date Created**.

- Switch **Link Sharing** off to stop the link working, and on again to restore it. You confirm each change.
- Switch **Post Approval** on or off, after you confirm with **Proceed**.
- From a row's **Actions** menu, use **Copy link** to copy the address to send (shown while link sharing is on), or **Delete** it.

## What the recipient sees

The recipient opens the link and sees the posts for the chosen accounts, dates and labels in a **Month** or **Week** view. Clicking a post shows a preview.

### Approving and rejecting

When post approval is allowed, posts that need approval show **Approve Post** and **Reject Post**. The recipient confirms the choice:

- An approved post is scheduled for its time ("Post was successfully approved.").
- A rejected post is reported back ("Post was successfully rejected.").

Your team gets an email either way ("Post Approved" or "Post Not Approved"). Posts whose scheduled time has passed can't be approved ("Scheduled date for post is in the past").

### Comments

Where comments are available, the recipient can **Leave a comment**: they enter **Your name** and the comment, and click **Send comment**. The comment is sent to your team. Too many comments in a short time are refused with "Too many comments. Please wait a minute and try again."

## Set up a client approval workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Limit who can approve in your team">
    Give the team members who write posts a role without post approval, so their scheduled posts wait as **Needs approval**. See [Approvals](/guide/publishing/approvals).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the shared calendar">
    Choose the client's accounts and set **Enable/Disable Post Approval** to **Allowed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the link">
    Use **Copy link** and send it to the client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up">
    Approved posts go out on time. Watch for the approval emails and the client's comments, and update rejected posts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Anyone with the link can see the calendar. Set an expiration date and switch **Link Sharing** off when the client no longer needs it.
</Warning>
