The **Posts** list shows every post in your workspace, from drafts to published and failed posts. Use it to find a post, check how it went out, and open it to make changes.

In the sidebar, open **Posts** (in the newer sidebar, **All Posts** under the posts group).

## Status tabs

The tabs along the top filter the list by status:

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every post that is not in the trash. |
| **Drafts** | Posts with no scheduled time yet. Everything you write is saved as a draft until you schedule it. |
| **Needs approval** | Posts waiting for someone with approval rights. Only shown when there are some; the badge shows how many. |
| **Scheduled** | Posts waiting to go out. |
| **Published** | Posts that have gone out. |
| **Failed** | Posts that could not be published to at least one account. Only shown when there are some. |
| **Repeated Posts** | Your repeating posts. See [Repeating posts](#repeating-posts). |

Each row shows the post's status, its text, media, accounts and labels. A post that went out to some accounts but not others shows how many succeeded, for example "(2 of 3)". The **Publishing** status means the post is on its way right now.

## Search, filter and sort

- **Search**: type in the search box to find posts by their text.
- **Filters**: narrow the list by **Date** (created, scheduled or published date, with ranges such as **Last 7 days** or a **Custom range**), **Labels**, **Social Accounts**, **Platforms**, **Created by**, **Media** (text only, images or video), **Source** (RSS feed, repeating or imported posts) and **Publishing result** (partly published, or has failed accounts).
- **Sort by**: choose an order such as **Created: newest first**, **Scheduled: earliest first** or **Published: newest first**.

The filters you apply show as chips under the tabs. Remove one with its **x**, or click **Clear all**. If a filter leaves nothing, **Clear filter** brings the full list back.

## Open and edit a post

Click a row to open the post in the composer. You can edit drafts, scheduled posts and posts that need approval. Published and failed posts open read-only (posts in history cannot be edited), as do posts in the trash. Click the media thumbnail to preview the media without opening the post.

To change a published post, duplicate it and edit the copy.

## Post actions

Open the **More actions** menu (three dots) at the end of a row:

- **Duplicate**: makes a copy of the post as a new draft.
- **Delete**: moves the post to the trash, or deletes it from the social networks too. See below.
- **Restore**: brings a post back from the trash.
- **Copy UUID**: copies the post's ID for use with the [REST API](/quickstart). Only shown when your workspace can use the API.

These actions need the **Admin** or **Member** role.

## Delete posts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the posts">
    Use **Delete** in a row's menu, or tick several rows and click the bin icon in the bar that appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where to delete from">
    For posts that have been published, choose **From application only**, **From application and social platforms**, or **From social platforms only**. If a network doesn't support deleting that type of post, a note tells you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Posts deleted from the application go to the trash first. To see them, open **Filters** and switch on **Trash**. Deleting a post from the trash deletes it permanently.

<Warning>
Deleting from the social platforms removes the post from the network itself. That can't be undone from here.
</Warning>

## Approve posts

If you have the **Can approve** permission, open a post from the **Needs approval** tab and click **Approve**. The post is then scheduled for its time. See [Create a post](/guide/publishing/create-a-post#approval).

## Repeating posts

The **Repeated Posts** tab lists posts set to repeat, with their **Frequency** (every so many days), **Start Date**, **End Date** and accounts. From a row you can view the dates the post is scheduled for, edit the repetition (**Repeat Frequency (Days)** and **Repeat Until**), or delete the repetition. You set a repeat when you schedule a post, with **Repeat this post**.

## Import existing posts

**Import Existing Posts**, at the top of the page, brings recent posts from a connected Facebook Page, Instagram or TikTok account into your workspace so you can reuse or republish them.

1. Click **Import Existing Posts**.
2. Choose the account under **Select Account** and the **Number of Posts to Import** (up to 60).
3. Click **Import**.

If the account needs reconnecting, you see a message asking you to re-authenticate it first.
