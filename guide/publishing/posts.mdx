The posts list shows every post in your workspace, from first drafts to published and failed posts. Use it to find a post, check how it went out, and open it to make changes.

In the sidebar, open **Posts** > **All Posts**.

## Status tabs

The tabs along the top filter the list by status:

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every post that isn't in the trash. |
| **Drafts** | Posts without a scheduled time. Everything you write is a draft until you schedule it. |
| **Needs approval** | Posts waiting for someone who can approve them. Shown only when there are some, with a count. See [Approvals](/guide/publishing/approvals). |
| **Scheduled** | Posts waiting to go out. |
| **Published** | Posts that have gone out. |
| **Failed** | Posts that could not be published to at least one account. Shown only when there are some. See [Failed posts](/guide/publishing/failed-posts). |
| **Repeated Posts** | Your repeating posts. See [Repeating posts](/guide/publishing/repeating-posts). |

## Reading a row

Each row shows the post's **Status**, **Content**, **Media**, **Accounts** and **Labels**.

- **Publishing** means the post is going out right now ("This post is being published, check back shortly!").
- A post that went out to some accounts but not others shows how many succeeded, for example **Published (2 of 3)**.
- Click the media thumbnail to preview the media without opening the post.

When posts are added or changed by someone else while you are looking, a bar says "... posts added or changed" with **Refresh**.

## Search, filter and sort

- **Search**: click the search icon and type to find posts by their text.
- **Filters**: narrow the list by any of these:

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Date** | **Filter by** **Created date**, **Scheduled date** or **Published date**, for **Today**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Next 7 days**, **Next 30 days**, **This month** or a **Custom range**. |
| **Labels** | One or more [labels](/guide/publishing/labels). |
| **Social Accounts** | One or more accounts. |
| **Platforms** | One or more networks. |
| **Created by** | The team member who created the post. |
| **Media** | **Text only**, **Images** or **Video**. |
| **Source** | **RSS feed**, **Repeating** or **Imported** posts. |
| **Publishing result** | **Partly published** or **Has failed accounts**. |
| **Trash** | Show posts in the trash. See [Delete and restore posts](/guide/publishing/delete-and-restore). |

- **Sort By**: **Default order**, **Created: newest first** or **oldest first**, **Scheduled: earliest first** or **latest first**, **Published: newest first** or **oldest first**, or **Updated: newest first**.

The filters you apply show as chips under the tabs. Remove one with its **x**, or click **Clear all**. When a filter leaves nothing, **No posts found.** appears with **Clear filter**.

## Open and edit a post

Click a row to open the post in the composer. You can edit drafts, scheduled posts and posts that need approval. Published and failed posts open read-only ("Posts in history cannot be edited."), as do posts in the trash.

To change something that has already been published, duplicate the post and edit the copy.

## Post actions

Open the **More actions** menu (three dots) at the end of a row:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Duplicate** | Makes a copy of the post as a new draft. |
| **Delete** | Moves the post to the trash, or deletes it from the networks too. |
| **Restore** | Brings a post back from the trash. |
| **Copy UUID** | Copies the post's ID for use with the [REST API](/quickstart). Shown when your workspace can use the API. |

These actions need the **Admin** or **Member** role.

## Select several posts

Tick the boxes on several rows to select them, then use the bin button in the bar that appears to delete them together. See [Delete and restore posts](/guide/publishing/delete-and-restore).

## Bring in posts from your networks

**Import Existing Posts**, at the top of the page, brings recent posts from Facebook, Instagram or TikTok into your workspace. See [Import existing posts](/guide/publishing/import-posts).

## Empty list

A brand-new workspace shows **No posts yet**, with a prompt to write your first post or, if no account is connected yet, to connect one first.
