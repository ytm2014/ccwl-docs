When your post is ready, the buttons at the bottom of the composer decide when it goes out: at a time you choose, in the next free slot of your posting schedule, or straight away. Until you do one of these, the post stays a draft.

## Schedule for a date and time

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the schedule window">
    Click **Schedule** (or **Pick time**) at the bottom of the composer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the date">
    Choose a day in the calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the time">
    Type or choose the time. The timezone is shown next to it; "Post will be scheduled according to this timezone. Click to update it."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the window">
    Click **Done**. The button now shows the date and time you chose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Schedule**. The post moves to **Scheduled** and appears on the [calendar](/guide/publishing/calendar).
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you pick a time that has passed, the window says **The selected date and time is in the past** and **Done** stays disabled.

## Best times to post

The schedule window shows **Best times to post** for the selected account, worked out from how your own past posts performed, in your timezone. Taller bars are hours when this account tends to do better than its own average.

- **Top picks** lists the strongest hours; click one to use it.
- Each hour is rated **Best hour**, **Good hour**, **Average hour** or **Quiet hour**, and hours with no posts yet are marked **Estimated**.
- Recommendations appear once an account has at least 10 published posts with imported statistics. Until then you see "Not enough data yet."

## Give each account its own time

When a post goes to several accounts, you can stagger them:

1. In the schedule window, click **Set a time per account**.
2. **Delivery times** lists **All accounts** and each account. Click an account and set its date and time.
3. Click **Use each account's best time** to give every account its strongest hour, or **Same time for all** (then **Clear times**) to go back to one time.

The **Schedule** button shows "+2" when, for example, two accounts leave at their own times.

If you later move a post whose accounts have their own times (for example by dragging it on the calendar), **Some accounts have their own time** asks you to **Keep the gaps between accounts** (every account moves by the same amount) or **Give every account the new time**.

## Repeat or send for approval

The schedule window also has two options:

- **Repeat this post**: publishes the post again every so many days until a date. See [Repeating posts](/guide/publishing/repeating-posts).
- **Post must be approved before it can be published**: holds the post as **Needs approval** until someone approves it. See [Approvals](/guide/publishing/approvals).

## Add to the queue

**Add to Queue** puts the post in the next free time of your [posting schedule](/guide/publishing/posting-schedule) for each selected account, so you don't have to pick a time. It only appears when the schedule has times and you can approve posts.

## Publish now

If you have permission to approve posts and no time is set, the main button reads **Post now**. Click it, check the list of accounts in **Confirm publication**, and click **Post now** again. The confirmation also lists any warnings and suggestions.

Videos can take a few minutes to process and appear on your profile after publishing.

## Unschedule a post

Click the **x** next to the scheduled time (**Clear time**). You are asked to confirm: "Clearing the scheduled time moves this post back to draft." The post won't go out until you schedule it again.

## Who can do what

| You | Buttons you see |
| --- | --- |
| Can approve posts | **Schedule**, **Post now**, **Add to Queue**, and **Approve** on posts waiting for approval. |
| Can't approve posts | **Schedule** only. Scheduled posts wait as **Needs approval**. |
| Viewer | The scheduled time, without a way to change it. |

## Troubleshooting

- **The schedule buttons are disabled**: the post has errors. Hover over the button to see how many, and fix them. See [Preview and checks](/guide/publishing/preview-and-checks).
- **"This post cannot be scheduled!"**: check that accounts are selected and the post has no errors.
- **"The posting schedule has no available times."**: add times to your [posting schedule](/guide/publishing/posting-schedule) before using **Add to Queue**.
- **A post didn't go out**: see [Failed posts](/guide/publishing/failed-posts).
