A post fails when a network refuses it, for example because the account was disconnected, the video was not accepted, or the network had a problem at that moment. A post can fail on one account and succeed on others. This page shows where to look and how to get it out.

## Find failed posts

- **Posts list**: the **Failed** tab appears when there are failed posts. Use **Filters** > **Publishing result** > **Has failed accounts** or **Partly published** to find posts that failed on some accounts only. See [Posts list](/guide/publishing/posts).
- **Home**: the attention bar shows "... posts failed to publish". See [Home](/guide/publishing/home).
- A partly published post shows its score on the list and calendar, for example **Published (2 of 3)**.

## See why it failed

Open the post and click the **Delivery** tab in the side panel. A dot on the tab means an account failed. For each account it shows:

- The status (**Published**, **Failed**, **Pending**).
- The time it left, when accounts have their own times.
- The error from the network, in red.
- **View on platform** for accounts where it was published.

The **Activity** tab also records **Publishing process failed** with the account.

## Retry an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fix the cause">
    Use the error message and the table below. For example, reconnect the account or replace the video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry">
    On the **Delivery** tab, click **Retry** next to the failed account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    The status updates when publishing finishes. "... of ... accounts published" shows the progress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only an account that failed can be retried, and not while the post is still publishing ("This post is still publishing. Wait for it to finish before retrying.").

### Threads that stopped halfway

If part of a thread was published, the account shows "... of ... parts published" and the button reads **Continue publishing**. "Publishing continues from the part that failed. The parts already published stay as they are."

### Change the post and publish again

Failed posts can't be edited. To change the content, open **More actions** > **Duplicate** in the posts list, fix the copy and schedule it. Duplicating copies the whole post, so remove accounts that already published it if you don't want them to post twice.

## Common causes

| What you see | Cause and fix |
| --- | --- |
| "The account needs to be reconnected. Re-authenticate it and try again." | The connection expired or the password changed. Reconnect the account on the **Accounts** page, then **Retry**. See [Social accounts](/guide/getting-started/social-accounts#reconnect-an-account). |
| "The connection to the platform dropped while the media was being uploaded." | A network hiccup. Try publishing again. |
| "The upload stopped making progress and was cancelled." | Try again; if it keeps failing, use a smaller file. |
| "A video in this post had not finished processing an hour after it was uploaded..." | The video never finished processing. Wait until it shows as ready in the media library, then publish again. |
| "The video in this post has finished processing, and a platform will not accept it as it is." | The video breaks a network's rules (length, format). Replace or trim it before the post is due. |
| "You've reached TikTok's posting limit for now." | TikTok limits how often you post. Retry later. |
| "TikTok did not confirm this publication in time." | Check the TikTok account first; it may have been published. |
| "Your X posts for this period are used up." | Your X allowance is used. See [X](/guide/publishing/networks/x). |
| A subreddit refused the post | Check its flair and rules. See [Reddit](/guide/publishing/networks/reddit). |

## Prevent failures

- Fix every error the composer shows before scheduling; a scheduled post with errors won't publish on those networks ("This post is scheduled, but it will not be published on ... until you fix ... issue.").
- Keep accounts connected. **Home** warns you when an account is disconnected.
- Schedule large videos with some time to spare, so they finish processing.
- Use the network pages for limits: [Networks: options and limits](/guide/publishing/networks/overview).
