**Activity** is the log of everything the sales inbox does behind the scenes: messages sent and received, bot replies, flow runs, broadcasts, deliveries, webhooks and integrations. When a flow didn't run, a reminder never arrived or a message failed, this is where you find out why.

In the sidebar, open **Salesbot** > **More** > **Activity**. The page is titled **Logs**. You need the **View the activity log** permission.

## Scope

The log shows events for the channel picked at the top of the page, plus workspace-wide events that belong to no channel (for example a failed background job or a token warning). Members limited to some channels see only events on their channels.

## Status tabs

Three tabs at the top give a quick count and filter the list:

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All events (24h)** | The count of events in the last 24 hours. Selecting it shows every status. |
| **Warnings** | Only warnings, with their count. |
| **Errors** | Only errors, with their count. |

## Search and filter

- **Search events**: type a word to filter as you type.
- **Filters**: opens a menu with:
  - **Event**: **All events**, **Messages**, **Broadcasts**, **Bots**, **Automations**, **Webhooks**, **Integrations**, **Delivery**, **System** or **Errors**.
  - **Status**: **All statuses**, **Success**, **Warning** or **Error**.
  - **Platform**: **All platforms** or one platform.
  - **Date range**: **All time**, **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days** or **Last 30 days**.

The **Filters** button shows how many filters are active. **Clear filters** resets everything. If nothing matches, the page says **No log entries match your filters.**

## Read the list

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | Success, warning or error. |
| **Event** | What happened, with its type as a label. |
| **Platform** | The channel's platform, or a dash. |
| **Profile** | The connected account involved, or a dash. |
| **When** | How long ago. |

Long lists are split into pages.

## Open an entry

Click a row to open its details on the side:

- **Error**: the reason, in plain words where the platform gave one, for example a message refused by Meta.
- **Type**, **Platform**, **Profile** and **When** (the exact time).
- **Details**: the technical record of the event. Share it with support if you need help.

## Use it to troubleshoot

| Problem | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| A flow didn't run or stopped | Filter **Event** by **Automations** and **Status** by **Error**. |
| A message or reminder wasn't delivered | Filter by **Messages** or **Delivery**. A closed messaging window, an opted-out contact or a contact with no chat channel all show here. |
| A broadcast had failures | Filter by **Broadcasts**. |
| An integration or webhook isn't receiving data | Filter by **Webhooks** or **Integrations**. |
| The Overview says flow runs failed this week | Open **Errors**. |

Entries are removed after a retention period your platform sets.
