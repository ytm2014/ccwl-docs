Two sales inbox reports can be downloaded: **Pipeline** and **Ad attribution**. Each has an **Export** menu at the top right with **Download CSV** (for spreadsheets) and **Download PDF** (a landscape A4 page to share or print).

## Download a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    In the sidebar, open **Salesbot** > **More**, then **Pipeline** or **Ad attribution**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the filters">
    Choose **From** and **To** (and **Assigned to** on the Pipeline report) and click **Apply**. The export uses exactly these filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Click **Export**, then **Download CSV** or **Download PDF**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You need the same access as for the report itself: **View the overview** for Pipeline, **View ad attribution, spend and revenue** for Ad attribution.

## Pipeline export

The files are named `pipeline-report.csv` and `pipeline-report.pdf`. The CSV has one row per stage with these columns:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Stage | The stage name. |
| Outcome | `won`, `lost` or empty. |
| Entered, Moved on, Moved back | As in the report. |
| Removed | Stays that ended because the contact's stage was cleared. |
| Still in stage | Stays from the period that are still open. |
| Reached won, Lost | Stays whose contact later reached a Won or Lost stage. |
| Conversion % | Moved on divided by entered; empty for outcome stages. |
| Completed stays | Stays that ended in the period. |
| Avg days in stage, Median days in stage | Times in days, with two decimals. |
| In stage now, Avg days so far | Open stays and their average age in days. |

The CSV has a few columns the screen doesn't show (Removed, Still in stage, Completed stays), and gives times in days rather than minutes, hours or days.

## Ad attribution export

The files are named `ad-attribution.csv` and `ad-attribution.pdf`. The CSV has one row per ad:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Ad ID, Headline | The ad. |
| Conversations, Messages | Conversations from the ad and their messages. |
| First-ad contacts | Contacts first brought in by the ad. |
| Revenue, Revenue currency | Credited revenue and its currency. |
| Spend, Spend currency | Measured spend and its currency. |
| Impressions, Clicks | Measured delivery. |
| Cost per conversation, ROAS | As in the report. |
| Refund adjustment unavailable | `Yes` when a refund's net amount isn't known. |
| Measurement updated | When delivery was last measured. |
| Leads, Cost per lead | Lead form submissions and their cost. These are always the last two columns. |

A figure that isn't available (a dash on screen) is left blank in the CSV. Amounts in different currencies are never converted, so check the currency columns before adding them up.

## What doesn't export

- **Overview**, **Activity** and **Audit log** are on-screen only.
- Contacts are exported from the contacts list; see [Contacts](/guide/contacts/overview).
- Broadcast results are on each broadcast's report; see [Broadcasts](/guide/broadcasts/overview).
- Post and account analytics belong to publishing; see [Publishing analytics](/guide/publishing/analytics).

For anything else, developers can read your data through the API; see the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
