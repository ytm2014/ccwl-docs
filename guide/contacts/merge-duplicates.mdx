The same person sometimes ends up as two contacts. Typical causes: you imported or added them by hand before they messaged you, or they wrote in on a second channel that couldn't be matched to them. Merging combines the two into one profile, so the team sees one history and every channel replies from the same place.

Merging needs the **Merge contacts** permission. It's admin-only unless a custom role grants it, and a teammate limited to some channels can't merge.

<Warning>
A merge can't be undone. The history can't be split apart again afterwards.
</Warning>

## What a merge does

You always keep one contact and fold the other into it.

- The kept contact receives the other one's channels, conversations, notes and tags.
- Automations still running for the duplicate are stopped; the kept contact's automations carry on.
- The duplicate is then deleted.
- The deleted duplicate appears in **Recently deleted** marked **Merged: contact record only**. Restoring it brings back only the contact record, not the conversations that moved. See [Delete and restore contacts](/guide/contacts/delete-and-restore).

## Merge from a profile's suggestions

When other contacts share a phone number or email address with the one you're viewing, the profile shows a **Possible duplicates** panel listing each one with its phone or email and message count.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact to keep">
    Open the profile of the contact you want to keep.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the duplicate">
    In **Possible duplicates**, click **Merge** next to the other contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The **Merge contacts** dialog says which contact will be merged into which. Click **Merge into this profile**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The panel only appears for people who can merge.

## Merge any two contacts from the list

Use this when two contacts don't share a phone or email, for example an Instagram contact and a WhatsApp contact you know are the same person.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find both">
    In the contact list, search or filter so both contacts are on the same page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick exactly two">
    Tick the two rows. **Merge selected** appears in the selection bar only when exactly two contacts are ticked by hand (not with **Select all matching**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The dialog names the contact that is kept and the one whose channels, conversations, notes and tags move into it. Confirm to merge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The contact listed first on the page is the one that's kept. If you want to keep the other one, merge from its profile instead.

## Before you merge

- Decide which profile to keep: it's the one that remains, under its own name.
- Look at the **Channels** panel of each to be sure they're the same person.
- If one is a contact you restored from **Recently deleted** after the person wrote in again, merging is the way to put the two back together.

## Common questions

**I tried to restore a deleted contact and it isn't linked to its channel any more.** The person wrote in again after the delete and became a new contact, which kept the channel. Merge the two from the profile.

**Why don't I see Merge selected?** Tick exactly two contacts on the current page, and check that your role can merge.

**Can I merge three contacts?** Merge two, then merge the result with the third.
