The pipeline shows where each contact stands in your sales process, from first enquiry to won or lost. Each contact has one stage (or **No stage**), and the **Board** tab shows contacts as cards in stage columns.

## Open the board

In the sidebar, open **Salesbot** > **Contacts** and select the **Board** tab. Use **Search by keyword** to find a card.

- **No stage** is always the first column and can't be moved or deleted.
- Each stage column shows its colour, its name, a count of the cards in it and, for stages marked as won or lost, **Won** or **Lost**.
- Each card shows the contact's name, their last message or phone, their channel, up to two tags and when they were last active.

<Note>
The board shows the 300 most recently active contacts. When there are more, a notice says so, and column counts only describe the cards on the board. Use the [contact list](/guide/contacts/contact-list) with the **Stage** filter to find everyone in a stage.
</Note>

## Move a contact to another stage

You need the **Edit contacts, their stage and notes** permission.

- **Drag** a card into another column.
- Or open the card's menu and pick a stage under **Move to stage**. This works without dragging.

You can also change a stage from:

- the **Stage** drop-down in the contact profile's side panel or the inbox's contact details,
- the **Stage** field when editing a contact,
- the list, for many contacts at once: tick them and choose **Move to stage**.

Each card's menu also has **View**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

## Set up your stages

Adding, renaming, recolouring, reordering and deleting stages need the **Manage pipeline stages** permission.

### Add a stage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Click **Add stage** after the last column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Stage name** (up to 60 characters). Click the colour dot beside it to pick a **Stage colour**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose its outcome">
    Where the pipeline report is available, set **Counts as** to **In progress**, **Won** or **Lost**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Edit, reorder or delete a stage

Open a column's menu (three dots):

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit stage** | Change the name, colour and **Counts as**, then **Save**. |
| **Move left** / **Move right** | Move the column one place. You can also drag a column by its handle. |
| **Delete** | Delete the stage after you confirm. Its contacts move to **No stage**; they aren't deleted. |

The order of the columns is the order of your pipeline everywhere stages are listed.

## Won, lost and the pipeline report

**Counts as** tells the pipeline report which stages mean a deal was won or lost; **In progress** is everything in between. Click **View report** at the top of the board to open the report. See [Reports](/guide/analytics/overview).

Don't see **Counts as** or **View report**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include the pipeline report yet, or your role can't view reports.

## Stages elsewhere

- **Segments** can be limited to one **Contact stage**. See [Segments](/guide/contacts/segments).
- **CSV import** sets a stage when the `stage` column holds the exact name of an existing stage.
- **Automations** can start when a contact's stage changes. See [Automation](/guide/automation/overview).
