Tags are short labels you put on contacts, such as *VIP*, *Wholesale* or *Asked for a quote*. They're shared by every channel in the workspace, so a tag added on an Instagram conversation is the same tag you filter on for a WhatsApp broadcast. Use them to filter the list, build [segments](/guide/contacts/segments) and drive automations.

## Tag a contact

There are several places to add or remove tags. Each needs the **Edit contacts, their stage and notes** permission.

| Where | How |
| --- | --- |
| New or edit contact form | In **Tags**, pick tags or type a new tag name and press Enter to create it. |
| Contact profile | Click the pencil next to **Tags** (**Edit tags**) to open the edit form. |
| Inbox contact details | Click **+** (**Add tag**) to add one, or the **×** on a tag to remove it. |
| Contact list, many at once | Tick contacts, then **Add tag** or **Remove tag**. See [The contact list](/guide/contacts/contact-list#act-on-several-contacts). |
| CSV import | Fill the `tags` column. See [Import contacts from CSV](/guide/contacts/import-csv). |

The list shows a contact's first two tags and a **+n** for the rest; the board cards do the same.

## Manage the tag list

Choose **More contact actions** > **Manage tags** on the **List** or **Board** tab. You need the **Manage tags** permission.

### Add a tag

1. At the bottom of **Manage tags**, type a **New tag name** (up to 60 characters).
2. Optionally click the colour square beside it to pick a colour.
3. Click **Add**, or press Enter.

### Rename or recolour a tag

Change the name in its row, or click its colour square to pick a new colour, then click the tick (**Save**) at the end of the row. The change applies to every contact that has the tag.

### Delete a tag

Click the bin at the end of the row and confirm. The tag is removed from all contacts.

Click **Done** to close the dialog.

## The Unsubscribe tag

The tag named **Unsubscribe** marks people who opted out of messages. It's added and removed for you when someone opts out or back in, and it can't be renamed or deleted (*Required for opt-out compliance — can't be deleted.*). See [Opt-outs and blocking](/guide/contacts/opt-outs).

## Import and export tags

At the top of **Manage tags**:

- **Export** downloads every tag with its colour as a CSV.
- **Download sample** gives the format: a header row with `name` and `hex_color`, then one tag per row, for example `VIP,#ef4444`.
- **Import CSV** reads such a file. Tags that already exist (same name) are skipped, names longer than 60 characters are skipped, and a tag with no valid colour gets a neutral grey. A message says how many were imported and skipped.

Exporting from one workspace and importing into another copies your tag set across. The file can be up to 1 MB.

## Tips

- Keep names short and consistent; segments and filters match tags exactly.
- Use tags for facts that can be true at the same time (*VIP* and *Newsletter*). For a single step in a sales process, use a [pipeline stage](/guide/contacts/pipeline) instead.
- To find everyone with a tag, filter the list by **Tags**, or build a segment with **Contact tags**.
