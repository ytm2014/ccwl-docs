Each contact has a profile page that brings together who they are, how they reach you and what your team knows about them. Open it by clicking a name in the list, a card on the board, or **Open contact** in the inbox's contact details.

## The header

The header shows the contact's name with a **Contacts** link back to the list, their stage and their status. On the right:

- **Send message** opens this person's conversation in the inbox. It appears when the inbox is available to you. Without it, the main button is **Edit profile**.
- **More contact actions** (three dots) has, depending on your role:
  - **Edit profile**,
  - **Pause bot reply** or **Resume bot reply**,
  - **Delete contact**,
  - **Erase permanently** (admins). See [Delete and restore contacts](/guide/contacts/delete-and-restore).

## Metrics

Three figures sit at the top: **Messages exchanged**, **Tags applied** and **Last interaction**.

## The tabs

| Tab | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | **Conversation summary** with the **Last inbound message**, and **Flow answers**: the questions your automations asked and what the contact replied, with the time. |
| **Activity** | A timeline: when the contact was created and from which source, the last message received, every internal note, each time someone used **Download all data**, and meetings booked, completed, cancelled or missed (where meetings are part of your plan). |
| **Notes** | Internal notes, seen by your team only. The tab shows how many there are. |

### Work with notes

You need the **Edit contacts, their stage and notes** permission to add or change notes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write the note">
    On the **Notes** tab, type in the box under the notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **Add note**. It appears at the top with your name and the time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use the pencil on a note to edit it (then **Save**), or the bin to delete it. Deleting asks first, and removes the note for everyone. Notes added in the inbox's contact details appear here too, and the other way round.

## The side panel

The panel on the right shows the contact's avatar, name, handle or phone and channel, then:

- **Stage**: choose a different stage, or **No stage**, from the drop-down. Without edit permission it's read-only.
- **E-mail** and **Phone**, when known.
- **Channels**: the icons of every channel they use.
- **First seen**, when known.
- **Custom fields** that have a value.
- **Tags**, with a pencil (**Edit tags**) that opens the edit form.
- **Bot active** / **Bot paused** with a switch. Turn it off to stop automated replies to this person while your team handles them; turn it back on to resume.

Depending on your platform, the panel can also show:

- A promotions switch with **Receives promotions** or **No promotions since** a date. See [Opt-outs and blocking](/guide/contacts/opt-outs).
- *SMS skipped: the gateway said this number is a landline*, when text messages can't reach the number.

## Panels below

| Panel | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Channels** | Each channel identity: channel name, platform and the ID on that channel, with **Primary** on the main one. |
| **Messenger marketing permissions** | For contacts on a Facebook Page, the marketing message permissions they've given. See [Broadcasts](/guide/broadcasts/overview). |
| **Privacy** | **Download all data** and the **Consent history**. See [Data, privacy and consent](/guide/contacts/data-and-privacy). |
| **Possible duplicates** | Other contacts sharing this phone or email, each with **Merge**. See [Merge duplicate contacts](/guide/contacts/merge-duplicates). |
| **Profile** | Facts the channel provided, shown only when known: **Contact ID**, **Gender**, **Language**, **Timezone**, **Birthday**, **Opt-in through**, **Source** and **Subscribed** (when the contact was added). |

## Common questions

**Why can't I see Send message?** The inbox isn't part of your plan, or your role can't view the inbox.

**Why are there no buttons to edit anything?** Your role can view contacts but not edit them.

**The stage change didn't stick.** If saving fails you see *The stage could not be changed.* and the previous stage comes back. Reload the page and try again.
