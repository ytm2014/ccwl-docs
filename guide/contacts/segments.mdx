A segment is a saved set of rules, such as "tagged *VIP*, not tagged *Unsubscribe*, quiet for 30 days". It isn't a fixed list: each time you use it, it picks up whoever matches at that moment. Use segments to target [broadcasts](/guide/broadcasts/overview) and to filter the contact list.

## See your segments

In the sidebar, open **Salesbot** > **Contacts** and select the **Segments** tab. The table shows each segment's **Name**, its rules as badges (stage, included tags, crossed-out excluded tags, *Inactive ≥ n d*, and the number of conditions) and how many contacts match right now. A segment with no rules shows **All contacts**.

Anyone who can view contacts can see the list. Creating, editing and deleting need the **Manage segments** permission.

Don't see **Segments**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet.

## Create a segment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **New segment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the audience">
    Fill in the **Audience** panel (below). Only **Segment name** is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add conditions if you need them">
    In **Conditions**, add groups of rules to narrow the segment further.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the count">
    **Contacts matching** on the right updates as you edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### The Audience panel

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Segment name** | Up to 80 characters. |
| **Contact stage** | One stage, or **Any stage**. |
| **Contact tags** | Contacts that have *all* the selected tags are included. |
| **Exclude contacts with tags** | Contacts with *any* of these tags are removed. |
| **Number of days since last activity** | Only contacts inactive for at least this many days. Leave empty for no limit. |

## Conditions

Conditions apply on top of the Audience panel: a contact must match both. With no conditions, the segment uses the panel on its own.

1. Click **Add condition group**. A group starts with one rule.
2. For each rule, choose a field, an operator and, where needed, a value. Click **Add condition** for more rules in the group.
3. In each group, choose whether contacts must match **all** or **any** of its rules.
4. With several groups, choose at the top whether contacts must match **all** or **any** of these groups.

Remove a rule with **Remove condition** or a whole group with **Remove group**. Removing a group's last rule removes the group.

| Field | Operators | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Contact source** | is, is not, contains, does not contain, has any value, is empty | Text, such as *CSV import* |
| **Custom field** | is, is not, contains, does not contain, has any value, is empty, is greater than, is less than, is at least, is at most | Pick the field, then type a value |
| **Birthday month** | is, is not | A month |
| **Tag** | is applied, is not applied | A tag |
| **Channel** | is reachable on, is not reachable on | A channel type |
| **Subscription** | is subscribed, is unsubscribed | None |

Limits: up to 20 groups, with up to 30 rules in each.

If a rule is incomplete (no value, or no custom field chosen), a warning says how many will be ignored when saving; the count only uses complete rules.

## Use a segment

- **Broadcasts**: choose the segment as the audience. See [Broadcasts](/guide/broadcasts/overview).
- **Contact list**: open **Filters** and pick it under **Segment**. Bulk actions with **Select all matching** and **Export** then follow the segment too.

## Edit or delete a segment

Click a segment's name, or choose **Edit** from its actions menu, change it and click **Save**.

To delete, choose **Delete** from the actions menu and confirm. A segment can't be deleted while a broadcast that hasn't started yet is waiting to send to it; the message explains why. Deleting a segment never deletes contacts.
