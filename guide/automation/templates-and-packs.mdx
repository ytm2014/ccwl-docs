You rarely need to build a flow from nothing. The template gallery has working examples for common jobs, and your platform may also publish automation packs: sets of several automations you add in one go. Both create drafts that you review in the builder before anything runs.

## Templates

Templates appear in the **New flow** gallery after you choose a profile (see [Create a flow](/guide/automation/create-a-flow)). Only templates that work on that profile are shown, and a template's trigger is set for the profile you install it on.

### What's in the gallery

The gallery groups templates by goal, for example capturing leads, booking appointments, recovering revenue, supporting customers, driving traffic and engagement, and you can filter by industry (such as e-commerce, restaurants, salons, healthcare, real estate, education and events). Examples you may see, depending on your channels and plan:

| Job | Templates |
| --- | --- |
| Welcome new chats | **Welcome & qualify new chats**, **Welcome / Default Reply**, **Welcome + capture phone number** |
| Comments | **Comment-to-DM flow**, **Comment-to-DM Lead Magnet**, **Reply to comments with AI**, **Drive traffic from comments**, **Sell from Reel comments** |
| AI assistants | **AI Customer Service bot**, **AI Lead Qualification bot**, **AI review reply** |
| Leads | **Lead qualification flow**, **Collect Email & Phone**, **Quiz + Lead Tagging**, **Follow up on a Facebook lead form**, **Hot lead alert** |
| Bookings | **Book Appointment / Consultation**, **Salon Appointment Booking**, **Table Reservation**, **Test Drive Booking** |
| Stores | **Abandoned Checkout Recovery**, **Order Confirmation**, **Shipping Update**, **Back In Stock Alert**, **COD Confirmation**, **Order Status Self-Service** |
| WhatsApp | **WhatsApp support menu**, **WhatsApp Cart Checkout**, **Lead capture form (WhatsApp Flow)**, **Ask for phone number when missing**, **Post-chat CSAT survey** |
| Housekeeping | **Marketing Unsubscribe** (named after the channel), **Marketing Resubscribe**, **Flag unhappy customers**, **Scheduled announcement / campaign**, **Birthday offer** |

Store, payment, booking and AI templates only appear when the matching features are available in your workspace.

### Install a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the preview">
    Click a template card. The preview lists the **Trigger**, the **Flow steps**, which channels it **Works on** and **What to customize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the badges">
    **Needs AI insights** means the template starts when AI insights classify a message, so AI insights must be on in your inbox settings. **Not available yet** means it can't be installed in your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it">
    Click **Use this template**, or **Install & set up** when the template needs something from you first (for example a store, a meeting type or a WhatsApp template).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customise and publish">
    The flow opens as a draft. Work through **What to customize**: replace sample text, choose your tags, pick your WhatsApp templates and connect any service the steps use. Then publish and switch it on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Fix any items in the builder's **Fix N issues** list before you publish. A template step that still has "No template selected" or "No team member selected" will not do what you expect.
</Tip>

## Automation packs

When your platform has published packs, an **Automation packs** panel appears at the top of the Flows page: "Ready-made sets of automations. Add a whole pack in one go." The panel shows the packs for the profile the list is filtered to.

Each pack shows how many automations it holds (for example "6 automations") and the state of each one:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready to add** | It can be added to this profile. |
| **Added** | You already added it from this pack. |
| **Not on this channel** | Its trigger or steps don't work on this profile, so it is skipped. |

Packs that match your business type may be marked **Suggested for your business**.

### Add a pack

Click **Add all** on the pack. Every automation that is ready to add is created at once. When there's nothing left to add, the button reads **All added**.

"Each automation is added as a draft. Review it in the builder and publish it to switch it on." A pack never switches anything on for you and never skips the builder's checks.

### After adding

- Open each new draft from the **Drafts** tab, check its steps and fill in anything left blank.
- Publish each one and turn on **Live**.
- Your copies are yours: you can edit, rename or delete them like any other flow.
