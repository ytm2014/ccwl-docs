WhatsApp Forms (WhatsApp Flows in Meta's terms) are "native forms inside the chat": the customer taps a button, fills in a short form without leaving WhatsApp, and submits it. You design them here or in Meta's own Flow Builder, then send them from a flow.

In the sidebar, open **Salesbot** > **More** > **Forms**. The page is called **WhatsApp Forms**. You need a connected WhatsApp number.

<Note>
Don't see **Forms**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include WhatsApp Forms yet. Creating and editing forms needs permission to manage automations.
</Note>

## The forms list

Each form shows its **Name**, **Status**, **Origin** (**Designed here** or **Synced from Meta**) and **Actions**: **Edit**, **Publish** and **Delete**. A form marked **Not on Meta yet** exists only here so far. Use **Sync from Meta** to bring in forms you built in Meta's Flow Builder.

## Create a form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a starting point">
    Under **Create a form** ("Pick a starting point — every field is editable afterwards."), choose a starter: **Get in touch** (name, phone, email, message), **Request an appointment** (name, phone, service, preferred date, notes), **Share your feedback** (a rating and comments), **Register** (name, email, phone, consent) or **Untitled form**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form details">
    Under **Form details**: a **Name** (for you), a **Heading** ("Shown at the top of the form in WhatsApp.") and the **Submit button** label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the fields">
    Under **Fields**, each field has a type, a **Question** and a **Required** switch. Drag to reorder, **Remove** to delete, **Add field** for more. Dropdown and multiple choice fields take **Options (one per line)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. "Saving uploads this form to WhatsApp and refreshes the preview." Fields without a question are dropped when you save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish** when the form is ready. Only a saved draft with no errors can be published.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Field types

| Type | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Short text** | Names and short answers |
| Email | An email address |
| Phone number | A phone number |
| **Number** | Quantities, ages |
| **Long text** | Messages and notes |
| **Dropdown** | One choice from a list |
| **Multiple choice** | One choice shown as options |
| Date | A date, such as a preferred appointment day |
| **Consent checkbox** | "I agree to be contacted" and similar |

### Preview and errors

The **Preview** shows the form as WhatsApp renders it. "This form only exists here so far. Connect a live WhatsApp number and save again to upload it and get a preview." If WhatsApp refuses the form, the editor shows **WhatsApp rejected this form** with the reason; fix it and save again.

## Send a form from a flow

Add the **WhatsApp Form (Flow)** step (under **Ask a question**) to a WhatsApp flow:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Form** | **Choose a form…** from your forms. **Design a form** opens the forms page in a new tab; **Sync from Meta** refreshes the list. |
| **Flow name (manual)** | If the form isn't listed, the "Exact name in Meta's Flow Builder". |
| **Button text (CTA)** | The button that opens the form, up to 20 characters. |
| **Entry screen** | The first screen, for example WELCOME. |
| **Message** | The text above the button, for example "Tap below — it takes 30 seconds." |

"Draft forms send in test (draft) mode." When the customer submits, the step's **COMPLETED** output continues, and each answer is available to later steps as `{{nfm.field_name}}`, for example `{{nfm.email}}`. Use them in later steps, for example in a message, a Google Sheets row or an HTTP Request.

Outside the 24-hour window the form can't be sent; give the step a template fallback under **Advanced**. See [WhatsApp steps](/guide/automation/whatsapp-steps#the-24-hour-fallback).

## Send a form from the inbox

Agents can also send a form in a WhatsApp conversation with **Send a form** in the inbox's reply box. See [Reply to conversations](/guide/inbox/reply).
