A sequence is a drip campaign: "ordered messages delivered on a schedule after a contact subscribes." Day 0 might welcome them, day 2 share a tip, day 5 make an offer. Contacts join from a flow and leave when the messages run out, when they reply (if you choose), when they opt out, or when a flow removes them.

## Sequence, flow or broadcast?

- A **flow** reacts to something the contact does, right away.
- A **sequence** sends the same planned series to each subscriber, counted from the day they joined.
- A **broadcast** sends one message to many contacts at once. See [Broadcasts](/guide/broadcasts/overview).

## Where to find them

In the sidebar, open **Salesbot** > **More** > **Sequences**, or click **Sequences** on the Flows page. Sequences belong to a profile, so the list shows those of the profile you're working in.

<Note>
Don't see **Sequences**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include them yet. If you see "Connect a channel first", connect a messaging channel before creating one.
</Note>

The list shows each **Sequence**, its **Channel**, **Steps**, **Subscribers**, **Status** (**Active** or **Paused**) and when it was **Updated**, with **Edit**, **Pause** / **Resume** and **Delete**.

## Build a sequence

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new sequence">
    Click **New sequence**. Enter a **Name** (for example "Welcome nurture") and check the **Channel**: "Sequences belong to the selected profile." Set the **Status** to **Active** or **Paused**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when people leave">
    Turn on **Stop when someone replies** if a reply should end it: "Only that person leaves the sequence — everyone else keeps receiving it."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the messages">
    Under **Messages**, click **Add step** for each message and fill in:

    | Field | What to enter |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Day** | Days after subscribing. Day 0 is the day they join. |
    | **From** / **To** | The time window the message goes out in on that day. |
    | **Send as** | How the message is sent (see below). |
    | **Message** | The text, or for a template step the **Template** and its **Template variables**. |

    Reorder steps with **Move up** and **Move down**, or **Delete** one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the timeline and save">
    The **Timeline** shows each step by day. Click **Create sequence** (or **Save sequence** when editing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What a step can send

The **Send as** choices depend on the channel:

| Channel | Send as |
| --- | --- |
| WhatsApp | **Standard send** ("Inside the 24-hour messaging window."), **Utility template** ("Pre-approved utility template (order updates, reminders), delivers outside the 24-hour window.") or **Marketing message** ("Pre-approved marketing template, delivers outside the 24-hour window.") |
| Facebook, Instagram | **Standard send**, or **Human agent** ("Live-agent reply within 7 days of the contact's last message.") |
| Other channels | **Standard send** |

For a template step, pick an approved template of that kind under **Template** and fill every **Template variable**. With none you see "No approved templates of this kind on this number yet." and a link to **Manage templates**.

<Tip>
Sequence messages are sent days later, usually outside WhatsApp's 24-hour window. On WhatsApp use **Utility template** or **Marketing message** for any step after day 0, or the message won't be delivered.
</Tip>

## How contacts join

"Delivery starts when a contact is subscribed via the flow builder's Sequence step." Add the **Sequence** step to a flow and choose the sequence. The Sequence step is available on Facebook and Instagram flows, and the sequence must belong to the same profile.

- A new subscriber starts at the first step.
- A contact who finished or left earlier starts again from the first step.
- A contact who is already in the sequence stays where they are.

## Taking a contact out

A contact leaves a sequence when:

- the last step has been sent;
- they reply, if **Stop when someone replies** is on;
- they opt out (an **Unsubscribe Contact** step, or the STOP keyword);
- a flow reaches a **Remove from sequence** step, which works on every channel. Choose one sequence or **Every sequence they are in**. "It does not unsubscribe them or change their tags."

## Pause, resume and delete

- **Pause** stops sending for the whole sequence ("Sequence paused"); **Resume** carries on ("Sequence resumed").
- **Delete** removes it: "Flows that subscribe to it will need a new target." Update or remove those Sequence steps, or the flows will fail to publish.
