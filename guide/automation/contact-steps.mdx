These steps change the contact or the conversation rather than sending a message. Most are under the **Update contact** tile; hand-over and closing steps are under **Connect** ("End or hand over"). They work on every channel unless noted.

## Tag Action

| Field | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Action** | **Add tag** or **Remove tag** |
| **Tag** | The tag name, for example "Engaged Lead" |

Adding a tag can start other flows that use the **Tag applied** trigger. Tags are managed under [Contacts > Tags](/guide/contacts/tags).

## Update Contact Stage

"Moves the contact along your CRM pipeline when they reach this step." Choose the stage under **Move contact to pipeline stage**. With no stages yet you see "No pipeline stages yet — add stages on the Contacts → Pipeline page." Moving a contact can start **Pipeline stage changed** flows. See [Pipeline](/guide/contacts/pipeline).

## Assign Conversation

Routes the conversation to someone in your team.

| Field | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Assignment mode** | **Specific team member**, or **Round robin**: "Distribute new assignments across available workspace team members in turn." (Round robin appears where your plan includes it.) |
| **Team member** | For a specific member: "Routes the conversation to this member's inbox. Invite more members from workspace settings." |

Assigning does not pause the bot. To hand over and stop automated replies, use **Handover to Human**. See [Assign conversations](/guide/inbox/assign).

## Handover to Human

Passes the conversation to your team. "Works on WhatsApp, Messenger & Instagram — the conversation lands in your team inbox."

| Field | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Assign to** | **Anyone available** or a team member. |
| **Pause bot replies** | On by default. "Stops automated replies so a human can take over the conversation." |
| **Message to contact** (optional) | For example "A team member will be with you shortly." |

While the bot is paused for a contact, flows don't reply to them until the bot is resumed, for example by a flow with a **Resume Bot** step.

## Resume Bot

"The inverse of "Handover to Human": lifts the bot pause so automated replies continue for this contact, and drops a note in the team inbox." A flow containing this step may run even while the contact is paused, so it can lift its own pause.

**Message to contact** (optional) is "Sent just before the bot takes over again. Leave blank to resume silently."

## Close Conversation

"Marks the conversation done — the same as pressing **Close** in the inbox. No configuration needed." It also fires the conversation-closed webhook and the **Conversation closed** trigger, so another flow can follow on, for example a satisfaction survey. A conversation that's already closed is left alone, which also stops a conversation-closed flow ending in this step from looping. It's usually the last step of a flow.

## Clear AI Memory

"Resets what the AI Reply step remembers for this contact, so the next AI step starts fresh." Useful for a "start over" branch, or before switching the assistant to a different persona.

| **What to clear** | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Everything (history + tools)** | Both of the below. |
| **Chat history only** | Hides the earlier transcript from the AI (per channel). |
| **Tool memory only** | Clears this run's record of tool calls and its turn count. |

Nothing is deleted from your inbox.

## Unsubscribe Contact

Opts the contact out of automated messages, for example when they reply STOP.

| Field | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Also tag the contact** | On by default. "Adds a tag so unsubscribed contacts are easy to find." |
| **Tag name** | By default "Unsubscribe". |
| **Confirmation message** (optional) | "Sent before opt-out so it isn't blocked. Then automated messages stop." |
| **Reply buttons** | Optional buttons under the confirmation, for example "Resubscribe". On WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram they're tappable; a tap sends its label as a reply, so a button named "Resubscribe" can start your resubscribe flow. SMS sends text only. |

## Resubscribe Contact

Reverses an unsubscribe.

| Field | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Also remove the tag** | On by default. |
| **Tag name** | The tag to remove, by default "Unsubscribe". |
| **Welcome-back message** (optional) | "Opt-out is cleared first, so this message sends." |

The **Marketing Unsubscribe** and **Marketing Resubscribe** templates set both up for you. See [Opt-outs](/guide/contacts/opt-outs).

## Sequence and Remove from sequence

- **Sequence** (Facebook and Instagram flows): "Subscribes the contact to the selected drip sequence." Pick one of the profile's sequences; **Manage sequences →** opens the list.
- **Remove from sequence** (every channel): "Takes the contact out of the chosen sequence, so it sends them nothing more. It does not unsubscribe them or change their tags." Choose a sequence, or **Every sequence they are in**.

See [Sequences](/guide/automation/sequences).

## Common problems

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Tag name is empty." | Enter a tag. |
| "No pipeline stage chosen." | Pick a stage. |
| "No team member selected." | Pick a member, or use round robin. Only members who can use the inbox are listed. |
| "Set to "…" — pick a workspace member from the list to enable routing." | The step came from a template or import; choose a real member. |
| "No sequence selected." | Pick a sequence. |
| "The bot is paused for this contact." in a failed run | The contact was handed over. Resume the bot, or add **Resume Bot** to the flow. |
