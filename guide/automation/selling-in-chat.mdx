Flows can take a customer from browsing to paying without leaving the conversation: show products, catch the cart they send, and send a secure pay link. This page covers the selling steps and triggers; order lookups and store events are in [Store automations](/guide/automation/store-automations).

<Note>
The steps here depend on your channels, plan and connected services. Don't see one? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet, or the service isn't connected under **Integrations**.
</Note>

## Sell on WhatsApp

### 1. Switch on catalog and cart

Your product catalog must be connected to your WhatsApp Business Account in Meta's Commerce Manager. Then open **Salesbot** > **More** > **Channel settings**, pick the WhatsApp number and open the **Commerce** tab ("Control how your catalog and cart appear to customers in chat."):

- **Show catalog**: "Let customers browse your connected product catalog."
- **Enable cart**: "Allow customers to add products to a cart and send an order."

Click **Save changes**.

### 2. Put products in the conversation

Add the **Catalog / Product** step (under **Sell**). "Requires a catalog connected to your WhatsApp Business Account in Commerce Manager."

| Field | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **What to send** | **Full catalog**, **Single product** or **Product list**. |
| **Body** | The message, for example "Browse our products". |
| **Product ID** / **Product IDs (comma-separated)** | For a single product or a list: the product IDs from your catalog. |
| **Section title** | For a product list, for example "Featured". |

### 3. Catch the cart

When a customer sends their cart, the **Cart order received** trigger ("A customer sends their WhatsApp cart") starts your flow. It isn't offered when the cart is switched off for the number.

The cart is available to later steps as `{{cart.items_summary}}`, `{{cart.item_count}}`, `{{cart.total}}`, `{{cart.currency}}` and `{{cart.note}}`, with `{{customer.name}}`, `{{customer.phone}}` and `{{customer.email}}`.

If no Live flow picks the cart up, or it can't be handled automatically, the conversation is handed to your team: the bot is paused and a note explains why.

### 4. Take the money

Follow with a **Request Payment** step (below), using `{{cart.total}}` as the amount. The **WhatsApp Cart Checkout** template does exactly this: "Turn a sent WhatsApp cart into a payment link, with a human fallback if anything goes wrong."

## Product Catalog (Facebook and Instagram)

On Messenger and Instagram flows, **Product Catalog** sends products from a connected store:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Store** | The store name or ID. |
| **Products** | Product IDs or a search term. |
| **Buy button text** | For example "Buy now". |

## Send Product (any channel)

"Sends a product card: image, title, price and a Buy link — rendered natively per channel." Pick a synced product, or use the product from a back-in-stock trigger. See [Store automations](/guide/automation/store-automations#send-product).

## Request Payment

"Creates a secure payment link and sends it as a "Pay now" button. The amount and message accept contact and order variables."

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Gateway** | One of your connected gateways. With none: "No payment gateway connected. Connect Stripe, PayPal, Paystack, Razorpay or MyFatoorah under Integrations first." |
| **Amount** | A number such as 49.00, or a variable such as `{{order.total}}` or `{{cart.total}}`. |
| **Currency** | The currency to charge, for example USD. |
| **Message** (optional) | "What is this payment for?" |
| **Button text** | By default "Pay now". |

A payment link can only be used once.

## Follow up on payments

Payment events start a flow for the paying contact. They're on the **Payment events** tab of the trigger picker ("A payment-gateway event (succeeded / failed / refunded) that starts your flow."):

| Trigger | Starts when |
| --- | --- |
| **Payment succeeded** | A contact completes a payment |
| **Payment failed** | A contact's payment fails |
| **Payment refunded** | A payment is refunded |

"Only payments made through our pay links." Payments made elsewhere don't start these flows; use [store triggers](/guide/automation/store-automations#store-triggers) for store orders.

Use them to thank the customer, tag them as a customer, move them to a pipeline stage, or send a new link after a failed payment.
