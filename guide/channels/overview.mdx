A channel is a profile, number, bot or widget your workspace sends and receives messages on. Messages from every channel land in the same [Inbox](/guide/inbox/the-inbox), and flows, broadcasts and growth tools run on them. Most channels come from accounts you connect to <AppName /> on the **Accounts** page; once connected, they appear in the messaging area on their own.

<Note>
Connecting, renaming and disconnecting channels is for workspace admins, or members whose custom role allows it. Which channels you can add depends on your plan and on what your platform offers.
</Note>

## The channels

| Channel | Private messages | Comments or reviews | Flows | Broadcasts | Reply window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [WhatsApp](/guide/channels/whatsapp) | Yes | No | Yes | Yes | 24 hours, then templates |
| [Facebook Messenger](/guide/channels/facebook-messenger) | Yes | Comments | Yes | Yes | 24 hours |
| [Instagram](/guide/channels/instagram) | Yes | Comments | Yes | Yes | 24 hours |
| [TikTok](/guide/channels/tiktok) | Yes, with a Business account | Comments | Yes | No | 48 hours |
| [Telegram](/guide/channels/telegram) | Yes, through your own bot | No | Yes | Yes | None |
| [SMS](/guide/channels/sms) | Yes | No | Yes | Yes | None |
| [Live chat](/guide/channels/live-chat) | Yes, on your website | No | Yes | No | None |
| [Mastodon and Bluesky](/guide/channels/other-networks) | Yes | Comments | Yes | No | None |
| [Threads, YouTube, LinkedIn, Reddit](/guide/channels/other-networks) | No | Comments | From comments | No | None |
| [Google Business Profile](/guide/channels/other-networks) | No | Reviews | From reviews | No | None |

Reply windows are set by each platform. See [Reply windows](/guide/inbox/reply-windows).

## Where you connect a channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Accounts">
    In the sidebar, open **Accounts** and click **Add account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tile">
    Social profiles such as **Facebook Page**, **Instagram** or **TikTok** connect the same way as for publishing. Messaging-only channels have their own tiles: **WhatsApp** (*Connect a business number*), **Telegram bot (Salesbot)**, **SMS** and **Live Chat**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the steps for that channel">
    Each channel's page explains what happens next.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Telegram bots, SMS gateways and live chat widgets then appear as cards on the **Accounts** page. Their menu has **Edit name**, **Copy ID**, **Disconnect** and, for a widget, **Customize widget**. Disconnecting removes the connection; past conversations stay in your Inbox.

## Set up each channel's bot

In the sidebar, open **Salesbot** > **More** and choose **Channel settings**. Pick the channel under **Workspace channel** at the top of the page. What you can set depends on the channel:

| Channel | What you set |
| --- | --- |
| Facebook and Instagram | Welcome message, ice breakers, default reply and persistent menu. See [Welcome message, ice breakers and menus](/guide/automation/channel-settings). |
| Telegram | Welcome message on `/start`, ice breakers, default reply and command menu. See [Telegram](/guide/channels/telegram). |
| WhatsApp | Number configuration, business profile, commerce, compliance and conversational components. See [WhatsApp number settings](/guide/channels/whatsapp-settings). |
| Live chat | The widget's look, behaviour and where it appears. See [Live chat](/guide/channels/live-chat). |

For other channels, the page says there is nothing else to set up. Editing these settings needs the permission *Edit the bot setup*, which admins and members have unless a custom role removes it.

Tags and custom fields are shared by every channel. Manage tags from **Contacts**; custom fields are managed by an admin on the workspace **Settings** page.

## Choosing channels

- **Customers who already message you** on WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger: connect those first; the conversations come to you.
- **Visitors on your website**: add a [live chat widget](/guide/channels/live-chat).
- **Customers you need to reach first**: WhatsApp templates and SMS can start a conversation; most other channels can only answer people who wrote to you.
