Connecting a TikTok account brings its direct messages into the Inbox and the comments on its videos into the **Engagements** tab. Flows can answer both. Messaging only works with a TikTok Business account, because TikTok's Business API isn't available for personal accounts.

## Before you start

Make sure the account is a TikTok Business account. If it's a personal account, switch it first:

1. In the TikTok app, go to **Profile**, open the menu (☰) and choose **Settings and privacy**.
2. Tap **Account**, then **Switch to Business Account**.
3. Choose a category and finish the prompts. Switching is free and can be undone.

## Connect the account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the account">
    In the sidebar, open **Accounts**, click **Add account** and choose **TikTok**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with TikTok">
    Sign in, choose the account and allow the permissions TikTok asks for, including messaging.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Inbox">
    Send the account a direct message from another TikTok account and check that it appears in your Inbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you connected the account before switching it to Business, reconnect it on **Accounts** after switching.

## What you get

- **Direct messages** in the **DMs** tab of the Inbox.
- **Comments** on your videos in the **Engagements** tab, with public replies.
- **Flows** that start from messages and comments.

TikTok channels can't be used for broadcasts.

## The 48-hour window

TikTok lets a business reply for 48 hours after the person's last message. There is no longer window for replies by hand. After 48 hours, wait for the person to write again. The chip above the reply box shows the time left. See [Reply windows](/guide/inbox/reply-windows).

## When you see a personal-account warning

If you pick a personal TikTok account under **Workspace channel**, or open the Inbox with one connected, a notice says *This looks like a personal TikTok account* or *To manage TikTok DMs, switch your account to a Business Account and reconnect it.* Switch the account as described above, then reconnect it on **Accounts**.

## Troubleshooting

**Messages don't arrive.** Check that the account is a Business account and that you allowed the messaging permissions when you connected it. Reconnect it if you're not sure.

**A reply was refused.** The 48-hour window may have closed, or TikTok may not accept that kind of message. The reason appears under the reply box.

See [Channel troubleshooting](/guide/channels/troubleshooting) for more.
