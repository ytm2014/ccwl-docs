Connecting a Facebook Page brings its Messenger conversations into your Inbox and its post comments into the **Engagements** tab. The same connection is used for publishing, so if you already post to the Page from the dashboard, its messages may already be arriving.

## Before you start

- You need to sign in with a Facebook account that manages the Page.
- Messages are received for Pages only, not for personal Facebook profiles.

## Connect the Page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the account">
    In the sidebar, open **Accounts**, click **Add account** and choose **Facebook Page**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Facebook">
    Facebook opens. Choose the Page or Pages to connect and allow every permission Facebook asks for. Skipping a permission can stop messages from arriving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Inbox">
    Send the Page a test message from another Facebook account. It should appear in your Inbox within moments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the Page was connected before messaging was switched on for your workspace, reconnect it from **Accounts** so it's picked up as a messaging channel.

## What you get

- **Messenger conversations** in the **DMs** tab of the Inbox, with text, images, files and voice notes where Messenger supports them.
- **Comments** on the Page's posts in the **Engagements** tab, with public replies and, where Facebook allows it, a private reply to the commenter (**Send as DM**).
- **Flows** that start from a message, a keyword or a comment.
- **Broadcasts** to your Messenger contacts, within Meta's rules.
- **Bot setup**: a welcome message, ice breakers, a default reply and a persistent menu. See [Welcome message, ice breakers and menus](/guide/automation/channel-settings).

## The reply window

Messenger lets a business reply freely for 24 hours after the person's last message. Where your platform has Meta's approval, a person on your team replying by hand can answer for up to 7 days; automated messages are always held to 24 hours. After the window, wait for the person to write again. See [Reply windows](/guide/inbox/reply-windows).

## Marketing permissions

For a Facebook Page, **Channel settings** can show a **Marketing permissions** tab, where your platform offers Messenger marketing messages. It sets up the permissions those messages need. See [Broadcasts](/guide/broadcasts/overview).

## Messages from ads

When someone messages the Page from a click-to-Messenger ad, the conversation can start a flow and is reported against the ad. See [Ad attribution](/guide/analytics/ad-attribution).

## Facebook lead forms

Leads from Facebook lead ads can start flows. A separate **Facebook lead forms** page, linked from the lead form trigger in the flow builder, lets you choose per form whether a yes to the form's WhatsApp consent question counts as WhatsApp opt-in, and which WhatsApp number the lead is added to. Filling in a lead form is not WhatsApp opt-in on its own. See [Flows](/guide/automation/overview).

## Troubleshooting

**The Page shows *Reconnect to enable messages*.** Its connection doesn't let the workspace receive messages yet. Reconnect it on **Accounts** and allow every permission Facebook asks for.

**Messages stopped arriving.** The connection may have expired or lost a permission. Reconnect the Page. See [Channel troubleshooting](/guide/channels/troubleshooting).

**A reply was refused.** The 24-hour window may have closed, or the person may have blocked the Page. The reason appears under the reply box.
