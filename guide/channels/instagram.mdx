Connecting an Instagram account brings its direct messages into your Inbox and the comments on its posts into the **Engagements** tab. Flows can answer both, and broadcasts can reach your Instagram contacts within Meta's rules.

## Choose how to connect

The **Add account** list can offer two Instagram tiles:

| Tile | How it connects |
| --- | --- |
| **Instagram (with Facebook)** | Through the Facebook Page the Instagram account is linked to, so the account must be linked to a Page you manage. You sign in with Facebook. |
| **Instagram** (*Direct login*) | With the Instagram account's own login, without going through a Facebook Page. Only shown where your platform offers it. |

Use the one your platform shows. If both appear, connect each Instagram account only once.

## Connect the account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the account">
    In the sidebar, open **Accounts**, click **Add account** and choose one of the Instagram tiles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and allow access">
    Sign in with Facebook or Instagram, pick the account and allow every permission requested. Messages need the messaging permissions in particular.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test message">
    Send the account a direct message from another Instagram account. It should appear in your Inbox within moments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

- **Direct messages** in the **DMs** tab of the Inbox.
- **Comments** on your posts in the **Engagements** tab, with public replies and, where Instagram allows it, a private reply (**Send as DM**).
- **Flows** that start from a message, a keyword or a comment.
- **Broadcasts** to your Instagram contacts.
- **Bot setup**: a welcome message, ice breakers, a default reply and a menu. See [Welcome message, ice breakers and menus](/guide/automation/channel-settings).

<Note>
Instagram shows ice breakers on its mobile app only.
</Note>

## The reply window

Instagram lets a business reply freely for 24 hours after the person's last message. Where your platform has Meta's approval, a person replying by hand gets up to 7 days; automated messages always have 24 hours. See [Reply windows](/guide/inbox/reply-windows).

## Troubleshooting

**The account shows *Reconnect to enable messages*.** Its connection doesn't let the workspace receive messages yet. Reconnect it on **Accounts** and allow every permission requested.

**Messages don't arrive, but publishing works.** Publishing and messaging use different permissions. Reconnect the account and allow all of them.

**Comments arrive but direct messages don't.** Reconnect and check the messaging permissions, as above.

See [Channel troubleshooting](/guide/channels/troubleshooting) for more.
