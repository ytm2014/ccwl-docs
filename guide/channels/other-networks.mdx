Besides the messaging channels, several social networks you connect for publishing also feed your Inbox. Their comments, mentions and reviews appear in the **Engagements** tab, and on Mastodon and Bluesky private messages appear in the **DMs** tab as well. You don't need a separate setup: connect the account on **Accounts** and it starts showing up.

<Note>
Which networks appear here depends on your plan and your platform. Don't see the **Engagements** tab? See [Comments, mentions and reviews](/guide/inbox/engagements).
</Note>

## What each network brings

| Network | In the Inbox | Flows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mastodon | Private messages and comments | From messages and comments |
| Bluesky | Private messages and comments | From messages and comments |
| Threads | Replies to your posts | From comments only |
| YouTube | Comments on your videos | From comments only |
| LinkedIn | Comments on your posts | From comments only |
| Reddit | Comments | From comments only |
| Google Business Profile | Reviews of your business | From reviews only |
| X | Replies and mentions, with a paid X API tier | No |

None of these networks has a reply window, and none of them can be used for broadcasts, sequences, growth tools or bot setup.

## Connect a network

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the account">
    In the sidebar, open **Accounts**, click **Add account** and choose the network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and allow access">
    Follow the network's sign-in. For Mastodon and Bluesky you enter your server or handle details in a form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for activity">
    New comments, mentions and reviews appear in the **Engagements** tab as they come in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Connect social accounts](/guide/getting-started/social-accounts) for each network's sign-in.

## Answer comments and reviews

Open **Inbox** > **Engagements**, pick the thread and reply. Comments get a public reply under the post. A review takes one public reply; sending another replaces it. On Mastodon and Bluesky you can also answer in a private message. See [Comments, mentions and reviews](/guide/inbox/engagements).

## Automate replies

You can attach a flow to any of these accounts. On the comment-only networks, a flow starts from a new comment or review; triggers that need a private conversation, a contact, a store or a payment aren't offered there. Ready-made flows include AI replies to comments and to new Google reviews. See [Flows](/guide/automation/overview).

## X

Replies and mentions from X are imported, and replies sent, only when your X account has a paid X API tier (Basic or higher). X authors aren't added to your contacts.

## Common questions

**A comment I can see on the network isn't in the Inbox.** New activity is fetched regularly, so it can take a little while to appear.

**Can I send a private message to a YouTube or LinkedIn commenter?** No. These networks have no private messaging here; reply publicly.
