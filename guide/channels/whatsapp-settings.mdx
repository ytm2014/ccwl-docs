Each connected WhatsApp number has its own settings: its health and limits as Meta reports them, the business profile customers see, your catalogue and cart, and the ice breakers and commands shown in the chat.

## Open the number's settings

In the sidebar, open **Salesbot** > **More** > **Channel settings** and choose the WhatsApp number under **Workspace channel**. You can also click **Manage** next to the number on the WhatsApp page. The settings are split into tabs: **Configuration**, **Profile**, **Commerce**, **Compliance**, **Message Templates** and **Conversational Components**.

If a tab says the number isn't fully connected, connect it first. See [WhatsApp](/guide/channels/whatsapp).

## Configuration

### Number health

**WhatsApp number info** shows the number, its **Quality** rating and these details:

| Detail | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Phone number**, **Verified name** | The number and the business name WhatsApp shows. |
| **Phone number ID**, **WABA ID** | Meta's identifiers, useful when you contact support. |
| **Number registration** | Whether the number is registered for sending. |
| **Display name status** | Whether Meta has approved your display name. |
| **Messaging limit** | How many customers you can start conversations with in 24 hours: from 50 up to 100,000, or **Unlimited**. |

Warnings can appear here:

- *Meta caps this number at 5 messages a day*: Meta provided the number and hasn't approved its display name yet. Connect your own number, or wait for the approval.
- *Your new display name is waiting for Meta's review*: customers keep seeing the old name until it's approved. Then register the number again (below).

### Register the number again

If you changed your WhatsApp Business display name, register the number again to keep sending. Under *Changed your display name?*, click **Register phone number**, enter the number's 6-digit two-step verification PIN and click **Register**.

### Meta settings

Links take you to Meta for things managed there: **Payment method** (**Open Meta Billing**), **Business verification**, which raises your messaging limits (**Open Business verification**), and **WhatsApp Manager** for numbers, display name and limits.

### Click-to-WhatsApp purchase reporting

Where your platform offers it, this reports a paid order or payment to Meta when the customer first messaged this number from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad. Only the ad click ID, the purchase value and the currency are sent; refunded purchases and blocked or opted-out contacts aren't reported. It may ask you to **Connect Meta dataset** first; then click **Enable reporting**. See [Ad attribution](/guide/analytics/ad-attribution).

### Disconnect

**Disconnect WhatsApp** removes the number from this workspace. You can reconnect it later.

## Profile

The business profile customers see in WhatsApp:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile photo** | Square JPG or PNG, 640×640 recommended (at least 192×192), up to 5 MB. Click **Choose photo**, then **Upload**. |
| **About** | A short tagline, up to 139 characters. |
| **Description** | Up to 512 characters. |
| **Category** | Your business category. |
| **Email**, **Address** | Contact details. |
| **Website 1**, **Website 2** | Up to two web addresses. |

Click **Save profile**.

## Commerce

- **Show catalog**: let customers browse your connected product catalogue.
- **Enable cart**: let customers add products to a cart and send an order.

Click **Save changes**.

## Compliance

**India commerce compliance** holds the details required to sell to customers in India: your legal entity and your customer-care and grievance-officer contacts. WhatsApp publishes them on your business profile.

## Message Templates

A link to your approved templates, which you need to message customers after 24 hours. See [WhatsApp templates](/guide/broadcasts/message-templates).

## Conversational Components

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Ice breakers** | Up to 4 tappable prompts shown to first-time customers. Click **Add ice breaker**. |
| **Show a welcome message to new conversations** | Sends a welcome message when a customer opens a new chat. |
| **Commands** | Up to 30 slash commands customers can type, such as `/menu`. Names use lowercase letters, numbers and underscores only. Click **Add command**. |

Click **Save changes**.
