Most channel problems come down to one of three things: the connection lost a permission, the platform's reply window closed, or something on the platform's side (a token, a webhook, an account type) changed. This page goes through the messages you may see and what to do about each.

## Start with these checks

1. **Send a test message** to the channel from another account or phone. Does it reach the Inbox at all?
2. **Look at the channel's status.** Check the number on the WhatsApp page or the account on **Accounts**. In the sidebar, **Salesbot** > **Overview** also points out channels that need reconnecting. See [Overview dashboard](/guide/analytics/sales-overview).
3. **Check your access.** If only you can't see the messages, your role may limit you to some channels or to your own conversations. Ask an admin.

## Nothing arrives

| What you see or know | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Reconnect to enable messages* on a Facebook Page or Instagram account | The connection doesn't let the workspace receive messages yet. Reconnect it on **Accounts** and allow every permission requested. |
| **Needs reconnecting** or **Missing a permission** | Reconnect the account on **Accounts** and allow everything it asks for. |
| The profile was connected before messaging was switched on for your workspace | Reconnect it on **Accounts** so it's added as a messaging channel. |
| WhatsApp: *Incoming WhatsApp messages are not set up yet* | Click **Retry** next to the number on the WhatsApp page. |
| WhatsApp: *This connection expires soon* | Connect the number again. |
| TikTok: a personal-account notice | Switch the account to a TikTok Business account and reconnect it. See [TikTok](/guide/channels/tiktok). |
| Telegram: messages stopped after the token was used elsewhere | Another tool took the bot over. Connect it again here and choose **Take it over**. See [Telegram](/guide/channels/telegram). |
| SMS: messages send but replies never arrive | Set the **Inbound & delivery webhook** URL in your gateway's dashboard. Custom gateways can't receive replies at all. See [SMS](/guide/channels/sms). |
| Live chat: the widget isn't on the site | Check the snippet and the **Where it appears** rules. See [Live chat](/guide/channels/live-chat). |
| X: no replies or mentions | X needs a paid X API tier (Basic or higher). |

## Replies are refused

When a reply can't be sent, the reason appears under the reply box and your draft is kept.

| Reason | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Window closed* on WhatsApp | Send an approved template with **Choose template**. See [Reply windows](/guide/inbox/reply-windows). |
| The reply window closed on Messenger, Instagram or TikTok | Wait for the customer to write again, or reach them on another channel under **Reply on**. |
| WhatsApp only allows a reply after the customer has messaged you | Start the conversation with an approved template. |
| *This contact is blocked* | **Unblock contact** if you want to reply. |
| *Your role lets you read conversations but not reply to them.* | Ask an admin for permission to send replies. |
| The channel needs reconnecting | Reconnect it on **Accounts**. |
| *This conversation cannot be replied to right now.* or a platform message | The platform refused the message. Check the channel's status and try again later. |

## WhatsApp-specific problems

**Only 5 messages a day.** Meta provided the number and hasn't approved its display name yet. Connect your own number or wait for approval.

**Sending stopped after a display name change.** Register the number again with **Register phone number** on the **Configuration** tab of the number's settings.

**Can't reach many new customers in a day.** Your number's **Messaging limit** caps how many customers you can start conversations with in 24 hours. Completing **Business verification** at Meta raises it.

**Your plan's connected-account limit has been reached.** You can reconnect a number you had before; adding a new one needs room in your plan.

See [WhatsApp number settings](/guide/channels/whatsapp-settings).

## Telegram connection errors

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *That bot token was rejected by Telegram.* | Copy the token again from @BotFather. |
| *This bot is already connected to another workspace.* | Disconnect it there, or create another bot. |
| *Telegram could not be reached to check this bot.* | Try again in a moment. |

## Still stuck?

Reconnecting a social profile or connecting a WhatsApp number again keeps its past conversations. (Disconnecting a Telegram bot, by contrast, removes its conversations.) If a channel still doesn't work after reconnecting with every permission, contact your platform's support with the channel name and the time of a message that didn't arrive.
