SMS uses your own account with an SMS gateway. You connect Twilio, Plivo or a custom HTTP gateway once, and your workspace gets one SMS channel for the Inbox, SMS broadcasts and the **Send SMS** flow step. Messages are sent from your gateway account and billed by your gateway.

<Note>
Connecting SMS needs the permission to manage integrations, which is admin-only unless a custom role grants it. Don't see SMS? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet.
</Note>

## Before you start

Have an account with your gateway and a number or sender that can send SMS:

| Gateway | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| Twilio | **Account SID**, **Auth token**, and a **From number / Messaging Service SID** (a Twilio number, or a Messaging Service ID starting with MG). |
| Plivo | **Auth ID**, **Auth token** and a **Sender number**. |
| Custom HTTP gateway | A **Send URL** that uses the `{to}`, `{text}` and `{from}` tokens, the **HTTP method** (GET or POST), and optionally an **Authorization header value** and a **Sender ID**. |

## Connect a gateway

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SMS settings">
    In the sidebar, open **Accounts**, click **Add account** and choose **SMS**. This opens the **SMS** tab of **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the gateway and enter the credentials">
    Pick Twilio, Plivo or a custom gateway and fill in the **API credentials**. The page's **Setup guide** shows where to find each value in your gateway's console.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. Keys are encrypted and never shown in the browser again; to change them later, enter new ones and click **Save & reconnect**, or leave a field blank to keep the saved value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the webhook">
    Copy the **Inbound & delivery webhook** URL (**Copy**) and set it in your gateway's dashboard, as both the inbound-message and the status or delivery-report URL. Without it, replies don't reach the Inbox and delivery reports don't update.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The panel shows what your gateway supports, for example *Inbound SMS: available · Delivery reports: available*, and your **Sender**, with counts of messages **Sent** and **Received**.

<Warning>
Custom gateways can only send. They don't deliver inbound replies or delivery reports, so you won't see customers' answers in the Inbox.
</Warning>

## Delivery settings

Under **Delivery settings**, click **Save settings** after changing these:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Add an opt-out line to SMS broadcasts** | Adds an opt-out line, on a new line, to each person's first SMS broadcast from this number, then at most once every 30 days, unless the message already mentions STOP. Broadcasts only, never replies or **Send SMS** flow steps. |
| **Check failed numbers with a line type lookup** | When the gateway reports an invalid number, one lookup per number (billed by your gateway, remembered for 30 days) tells a landline from a typo. Landlines are then skipped. |

## What you get

- SMS conversations in the Inbox, with replies sent from your gateway.
- SMS broadcasts.
- The **Send SMS** step in flows.

SMS has no reply window: you can write to a contact with a phone number at any time, within your gateway's and your country's rules. Long messages are split into several SMS parts by the network, and your gateway bills each part.

## Troubleshooting

**Replies don't arrive in the Inbox.** Check that the webhook URL is set in your gateway's dashboard for inbound messages, and that you're not using a custom gateway.

**Messages stay without a delivery status.** The status or delivery-report URL isn't set to the webhook in your gateway.

**Sending fails straight away.** Check the credentials and the sender number, and that your gateway account has credit. The Inbox shows the gateway's reason under the reply box.
