A bio page is a single mobile-friendly page with your profile, links and other content, at a short address you can put in your social profiles. You build it from blocks in an editor with a live preview, then switch it on when it's ready.

In the sidebar, open **Bio Links** → **Pages**. (If your dashboard shows **Bio Links** as a single page with tabs across the top, use the **Pages** tab.) The list shows each page with its address, status, views, clicks, click rate and when it was last updated, with workspace totals above it. Use the **All pages**, **Active** and **Paused** tabs and the search box to find a page.

Don't see **Bio Links**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet. Viewers can browse pages, previews and analytics; creating and changing pages needs the **Admin** or **Member** role.

## Create a page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the new page dialog">
    On the **Pages** list, click **New page**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to start">
    Leave **Blank page** selected for a starter page with a profile and a link to fill in, or click **From a template** to pick a designed theme first (see [Templates](/guide/bio-links/templates)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the address">
    Under **Short URL**, type the part after the slash, for example `yourname`. Leave it empty for a randomly generated one. If your workspace has [connected custom domains](/guide/bio-links/settings#custom-domains), pick the domain to host the page on from the list in front of the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create biolink page**. The editor opens on your new page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A new page starts **paused**, so nobody can open it until you publish it. It comes with starter blocks (an avatar, a heading, a short bio, social icons and a first link) for you to replace.

If your plan limits the number of bio pages, the list shows how many you have used, and you can't create more once you reach the limit.

## The editor

The editor has three tabs, **Blocks**, **Settings** and **Appearance**, next to a phone preview (on smaller screens, open it with **Show preview**). The preview follows your edits as you type, and you can click a block in it to open that block. Click **Desktop** to see the page at desktop width.

On wide screens a **Page details** column on the right shows where the page lives (**HTTPS on** for your own domain, or **Shared address**), a QR code of the saved address with **Download** and **Customise**, the page's **Views** and **Clicks**, its **Top links**, the **Tracking pixels** it uses, and **View analytics**. On smaller screens, open it with the **Page details** button. Everything in it is read-only; you make changes in the tabs.

The header shows the page's state (**Live**, **Paused** or **Disabled**), its public address, and whether you have **Unsaved changes** or **All changes saved**.

<Warning>
Nothing you change goes live until you click **Save** (or press Ctrl+S / Cmd+S). Visitors see the saved version until you save. If you try to leave with unsaved changes, you're asked first.
</Warning>

If someone else saved the page while you were editing, a banner says so. Click **Reload** to load the latest version; your unsaved edits are lost.

## Add and arrange blocks

On the **Blocks** tab:

- Click **Add block**, then pick a block from the picker or search for one. The new block goes in after the block that is open, or at the end.
- Click a block's title to open it and edit its fields in place. Under its fields, **Display settings** lets you schedule the block or show it only on some devices, countries or languages.
- Drag a block by its handle to reorder, or use **Move up** and **Move down** in its menu.
- Use the switch on a block to hide it without deleting it (it shows **Hidden**). The block menu also has **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Hide**/**Show** and **Delete**.

Without a search, the picker starts with a **Popular** group (Link, Socials, Image and Contact form), then groups every block into four categories:

| Category | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| **Basics** | Link, Heading, Paragraph, Avatar, Socials, List, Divider |
| **Media** | Image, Gallery, Video, Music, File |
| **Engage** | Email collector, Phone collector, Contact form, FAQ, Countdown, Modal text, Business hours |
| **Embeds** | Embed, Map, RSS feed |

See [Bio page blocks](/guide/bio-links/blocks) for what each block does and its fields.

## Choose a look

On the **Appearance** tab:

- **Theme**: pick one of the theme tiles. Switching theme clears the button, font and accent changes you made, so you're asked first if you have any.
- **Background**: the theme's own, one from the gallery (**Gradients**, **Mesh**, **Solid**), a **Custom** colour or gradient, an **Image** (with blur and dim) or a looping **Video** (mp4).
- **Buttons**: **Button style**, **Button shape**, **Shadow** and **Accent** colour.
- **Text**: **Body font** and **Heading font**.
- **Layout**: **Column width**, a **Share button** that shares or copies your page link, and **Motion** (gentle entrance animations).

You can also start from a designed theme in the [Templates](/guide/bio-links/templates) gallery.

<Note>
A page brought over from an earlier link-in-bio setup may keep the look it was imported with. The **Appearance** tab then offers **Preview the new look**, where you pick a theme and compare **Now** with **New look** before clicking **Switch to the new look**. Save your changes first. You can undo it later with **Back to classic** (or **Back to the previous look**).
</Note>

## Page settings

The **Settings** tab groups the rest:

| Section | What you can set |
| --- | --- |
| **Page** | **Title**, **Tagline**, **Avatar colour** (fills the avatar circle when there's no image) and, where offered, **Hide … branding** to remove the "Powered by" line. |
| **Address** | **Domain** (when you have custom domains) and **Short URL**. Leave the short URL empty to keep the current address. |
| **SEO and sharing** | **Title**, **Description** and **Share image URL** used by search engines and link previews, and **Discourage search engines (noindex)**. |
| **Protection** | A **Password** visitors must enter (tick **Remove password** to clear it), **Sensitive content (18+ age gate)**, and **Show a splash intro** with a **Splash title**, **Message** and **Seconds** (1–10). |
| **Tracking & UTM** | Tick the **Tracking pixels** to load on this page (create them first: see [Pixels](/guide/bio-links/settings#pixels)), and set **UTM defaults** (**Source**, **Medium**, **Campaign**). |
| **Custom CSS** | Styles applied on your public page, after the theme. |
| **Custom JavaScript** | Code such as a widget snippet, `<script>` tags included. It runs only when the page is opened on your own connected domain, never on shared domains or in the preview. Only add code you trust. |
| **Danger zone** | **Delete this page**. This removes the page and all its blocks and can't be undone. |

If a save is refused, the editor opens the tab with the problem and shows the message under the field.

## Publish or pause a page

In the editor, turn on **Published** and click **Save**. The header badge changes to **Live**. Turn it off and save to pause the page again. On the **Pages** list, the switch in each row's **Status** column publishes or pauses a page straight away.

A page can still be closed to visitors when it isn't paused: the editor shows a banner if the page has been **Disabled** (the **Published** switch can't undo this) or if the workspace's plan has lapsed.

## Share the public page

Your page's address is shown under its title, for example `yourdomain.com/yourname` or the platform's own short domain followed by your short URL. In the editor, open **Share** for **Copy link**, **QR code**, **Open public page**, **Public preview** and **Analytics**.

**Public preview** shows the saved page at **Phone** or **Desktop** size, with **Open**, **Copy**, **QR** and, when the page has a splash intro, **Replay splash**.

On the **Pages** list, each row has **Copy link** and **QR code** buttons. Its **More** menu also has **Create QR**, **Edit page**, **Analytics**, **Public preview**, **Duplicate** (the copy starts paused) and **Delete**.
