You can design branded QR codes for your bio pages, links, Wi-Fi details, contact cards and more, save them in your workspace and download them for print or screens. In the sidebar, open **Bio Links** → **QR Codes** (or the **QR Codes** tab). The page shows how many codes you have and their **Total scans**, then each saved code with its name, content and scan count. Filter with the **All**, **Bio pages**, **Links** and **Other** tabs, or **Search QR codes**.

Viewers can see and download saved codes. Creating and deleting them needs the **Admin** or **Member** role. If your plan limits QR codes, the page shows how many you have used; at the limit you can still design and download a code, but not save it.

## Create a QR code

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new code">
    Click **New QR code**. To make one for a bio page, click the **QR code** button on its row in the **Pages** list, or **Share** → **QR code** in the editor: the page's address, name and colour are filled in for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    Under **QR type**, pick **Link**, **Bio page**, **Wi-Fi** or **Contact**, or open **More types** for **Text**, **Phone**, **Email** and **Event**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Give the code a **Name**, then fill in the fields for its type (see below). The **Preview** updates as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Style it">
    Open **Style** to change colours, shapes, the centre logo and the frame.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or download">
    Click **Save** to keep it on the **QR Codes** page, or download it straight away as **PNG** or **SVG** from under the preview.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fields for each type

| Type | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Link** | The web address, for example `https://example.com`. |
| **Bio page** | **Bio page address**. Tip: open a bio page and choose "QR code" to fill this in. |
| **Wi-Fi** | **Network name (SSID)**, **Security** (WPA / WPA2 / WPA3, WEP or **No password**), **Password** and **Hidden network**. |
| **Contact** | **First name**, **Last name**, **Phone number**, **Email address**, **Company**, **Job title**, **Website** and **Address**. |
| **Text** | Any text. |
| **Phone** | **Phone number**. |
| **Email** | **Email address**, **Subject** and **Message**. |
| **Event** | **Event title**, **Starts**, **Ends**, **Location** and **Description**. |

To type the exact content yourself instead, turn on **Advanced: edit the raw content**. If the content is too long, the preview says **Too long to fit in a QR code.**

## Style options

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Foreground** | The colour of the code. |
| **Background** | The colour behind the code. |
| **Module style** | **Square**, **Rounded** or **Dots**. |
| **Eye style** | The three corner squares: **Square** or **Rounded**. |
| **Center logo** | **None**, a preset symbol, or up to four characters of your own. |
| **Add a frame** | Puts a frame around the code with **Frame text** (up to 20 characters). |

## Download or delete a saved code

On the **QR Codes** page, open a code's menu and choose **Download PNG** or **Download SVG**, or **Delete** to remove it from the list. Deleting a code doesn't stop copies you've already printed from working.
