The analytics page shows how one bio page is performing over a period you choose: how often it's viewed, how often visitors click, which links get the clicks, and where visitors come from. Open **Bio Links** → **More** → **Analytics**. You can also get there from **Analytics** in a page's menu on the **Pages** list, from **Share** → **Analytics** in the editor, or with **View analytics** in the editor's **Page details**. Everyone in the workspace, including viewers, can see analytics.

## Choose the page and period

- Pick the page from the **Bio page** list. When you open analytics without choosing, it shows a live page if you have one.
- Pick the period with the date picker next to it: one of the preset ranges, or your own start and end dates.

No pages yet? The page asks you to [create a bio page](/guide/bio-links/pages#create-a-page) first.

## What you see

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Page views** | How many times the page was viewed. |
| **Total clicks** | How many times visitors clicked on the page. |
| **Click rate** | Clicks as a percentage of views. |
| **Unique visitors** | Distinct visitors to the page. |
| **Views & clicks** | A chart of daily views and clicks in the selected period. |
| **Top links** | Your **Link** blocks ranked by clicks, with a bar for each. Hidden links are marked **Hidden**. |
| **Traffic sources** | Where visitors came from, as percentages. |
| **Devices** | The share of visitors on each type of device. |
| **Top locations** | The countries visitors came from, as percentages. |

<Tip>
**Top links** only lists Link blocks. If it says **No link blocks on this page yet.**, add a Link block in the editor. See [Bio page blocks](/guide/bio-links/blocks).
</Tip>

## Quick numbers elsewhere

- The **Pages** list shows totals for the workspace (**Bio pages**, **Total views**, **Link clicks**, **Avg click rate**) and each page's views, clicks and click rate.
- In the editor, each Link block shows its click count in the block list, and **Page details** shows the page's views, clicks and top links.
- The **Links** list shows clicks for each short link and **Total clicks** across them. See [Short links](/guide/bio-links/short-links).
- The **QR Codes** page shows a scan count for each saved code.
