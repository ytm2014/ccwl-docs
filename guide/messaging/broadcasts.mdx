A broadcast sends one message to many contacts on one channel: a promotion, an announcement or a service update. You choose the audience, write the message (or pick an approved WhatsApp template), decide when it goes, and then follow the results.

Open **Broadcasts** in the sidebar, or the **Broadcasts** card under **More**.

<Note>
Don't see **Broadcasts**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet. A lock badge next to the name means it needs a plan upgrade.
</Note>

## The broadcasts list

The list shows each **Campaign**, its **Channel**, **Delivery** and the counts **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Replies** and **Failed**. Filter by **Status**. Depending on its state, a broadcast's actions include **See campaign report**, **View analytics**, **Edit**, **Approve & schedule**, **Pause**, **Resume**, **Cancel send** and **Delete**.

- **Cancel send** stops it: messages already sent stay sent, the rest are never delivered. This can't be undone.
- **Delete** removes the broadcast with its recipient records and delivery report.

**WhatsApp templates** in the header opens your [templates](/guide/messaging/whatsapp-templates).

## Create a broadcast

Click **New broadcast**. The broadcast is fixed to the channel selected at the top of the page. Work through the four steps; each one shows when it's complete.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Audience">
    Choose **Contact segment** (all eligible contacts, active eligible contacts or a saved [segment](/guide/messaging/contacts#segments)) or **Retarget a past broadcast**: pick an earlier broadcast on this channel and an engagement, for example everyone who read it but never replied. Recipients are worked out when the broadcast sends. Opted-out and blocked contacts are always left out.

    On WhatsApp, contacts without a recorded opt-in are left out of a marketing template; a utility template reaches them. Admins can choose to include them anyway, which is recorded in the audit log.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Message">
    Give the campaign a name. On WhatsApp, select an approved template and fill in a value for each variable; you can't type a free message. On other channels, type the message and use `{ }` to personalise it, for example with the first name. Telegram only reaches people who have started a chat with your bot. SMS shows the character and segment count, and can add an opt-out line.

    Set the **Message purpose**: a **Promotion** skips contacts who opted out of promotions, a **Service update** (an order, booking or account notice) still reaches them. Tick **Track link clicks** to send links as short links that count clicks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    Choose **Send immediately** or **Schedule for later**. For a scheduled send, pick **One moment for everyone** or **Each contact's local time**, so nobody is messaged at 3am. Contacts without a known timezone use your workspace timezone. Tick **Require team approval before sending** if a teammate should approve it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Check the summary, the **Preview** and the **Checks**, then click **Save broadcast**, or **Submit for approval**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
On WhatsApp, use **Send test message** to send the template to your own WhatsApp number first. Normal messaging charges apply.
</Tip>

If your role can't send broadcasts, the broadcast is submitted for approval automatically. A broadcast awaiting approval shows **Awaiting approval**; someone allowed to send it clicks **Approve & schedule**.

### WhatsApp messaging limits

WhatsApp limits how many people a number can message in 24 hours. If your audience is larger, the review tells you how many go out now; the rest are sent automatically over the following days.

## Results

Open a broadcast to see its report:

- **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Read**, **Replies**, **Clicks** and **Failed**,
- **Delivery breakdown**: how each person was reached, inside the 24-hour window, via a notification opt-in, or not at all,
- **Conversion funnel** and **Engagement over time**,
- **Link clicks** per link,
- the **Message** that was sent,
- **Failures and skips**,
- **Recipients and send times**, filterable by status, with **Retry** where available.

On Facebook Pages set up for paid marketing messages, a broadcast can also go to paid subscribers through a Meta campaign with a lifetime budget. Its report shows what Meta accepted, delivered and spent.
