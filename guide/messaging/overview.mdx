The sales inbox is the messaging side of <AppName />. It brings your customer conversations, contacts, automated replies, broadcasts and bookings together in one place, across WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, SMS, your website live chat and the other channels your platform supports. In a few places the menu calls this area **Salesbot**.

<Note>
Don't see the sales inbox at all? Your plan or your platform doesn't include it yet, or your team role doesn't give you access. Viewers can't open it; members need inbox access from an admin.
</Note>

## Where to find it

How it appears in the sidebar depends on the layout your platform uses.

| Layout | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Sidebar with folding groups | **Inbox** at the top of the sidebar, and a **Salesbot** group with **Overview**, **Contacts**, **Flows**, **Broadcasts** and **More**. |
| Classic sidebar | A **Salesbot** item and a **Unified Inbox** item. The Salesbot pages then have a row of tabs across the top: **Overview**, **Contacts**, **Broadcasts**, **Automations**, **Growth**, **Settings** and **Reports**. |
| Older sidebar | A **Salesbot** section with **Inbox**, **Contacts**, **Automations**, **Channels**, **Team roles** and **Bot setup**. |

You only see the items your role allows. For example, **Overview** needs permission to view reports, and **Bot setup** needs permission to manage channels.

### The More page

In the folding sidebar, **More** opens a page of cards for everything that isn't in the group itself. Each card shows a short count, such as how many broadcasts are scheduled or templates are pending.

| Group | Cards |
| --- | --- |
| Grow | **Broadcasts**, **Growth tools**, **Appointments**, **Sequences** |
| WhatsApp | **Numbers**, **Message templates**, **Forms** (shown when you have a WhatsApp number) |
| Channels | **Channel settings**, **Live chat widget**, **Conversation rules** |
| Reports | **Activity**, **Ad attribution**, **Pipeline**, **Audit log** |
| Shortcuts | **Integrations**, **AI Knowledge**, **Team roles** |

## The workspace channel selector

Many sales inbox pages work on one channel at a time. At the top of those pages, **Workspace channel** shows two menus: the platform (for example WhatsApp or Instagram) and the profile on that platform. Change either one and the page reloads with that channel's data. A profile that can't use the sales inbox on your plan is marked in the list.

If no channel is connected yet, pages such as **Contacts**, **Broadcasts**, flows, appointments and growth tools show **Connect a channel first** with a **Set up a channel** button that takes you to your social accounts. See [Connect channels](/guide/messaging/channels).

<Tip>
A personal TikTok account can't be used for messages, comments or automations. The page explains how to switch to a TikTok Business account and reconnect it.
</Tip>

## The Overview page

**Overview** shows how your messaging is performing. Use the period picker at the top to choose a date range and, when you have more than one channel, the channel filter to narrow it to one channel or **All channels**.

- **Key figures**: **Conversations**, **Orders from chats**, **Revenue from chats** and **Reply rate**, each with the change from the previous period. Hover a figure for its definition.
- **Needs attention**: conversations waiting more than an hour, flow runs that failed this week, and channels that need reconnecting, each with a button (**Open inbox**, **View**, **Reconnect**). When there's nothing to do, it says so.
- **Get started**: a checklist for new workspaces: **Connect a channel**, **Start a flow from a template**, **Go live** and **Add your first contacts**.
- **Conversations**: a chart of people who wrote in, per day.
- **Mood of incoming messages**: the share of positive, neutral and negative messages, when AI insights are on. If they're off, you can **Turn on sentiment insights**.
- **Top flows**: your busiest automations, with runs and completions, and a link to **All flows**.
- **Conversations by channel**: each channel's share of conversations.

Open **All metrics** at the bottom for all-time totals (contacts, messages sent, reply rate, flow completion, broadcast delivery, growth clicks, opt-out rate, active sequences) and panels for message volume, flow outcomes, the broadcast funnel, contact sources, growth-tool attribution and recent activity.

| [Broadcasts](/guide/messaging/broadcasts) | One message to many contacts, now or later. |

| [Appointments](/guide/messaging/meetings) | Bookable meeting types and the bookings people make. |
| [AI Knowledge](/guide/messaging/ai-knowledge) | What the AI answers from. |
| [Reports and settings](/guide/messaging/reports-and-settings) | Activity log, audit log, ad and pipeline reports, and conversation rules. |
