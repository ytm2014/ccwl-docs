Sequences and growth tools work alongside your [flows](/guide/messaging/automations). A sequence sends a series of messages over days after a contact subscribes. Growth tools are links, QR codes and pop-ups that open a chat with you and can start a flow.

<Note>
Don't see **Sequences** or **Growth tools**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include them yet. Growth tools aren't offered for live chat, TikTok, SMS and some other channels.
</Note>

## Sequences

Open **Sequences** under **More**, or the **Sequences** button on the **Flows** page. The list shows each **Sequence**, its **Channel**, **Steps**, **Subscribers**, **Status** and when it was **Updated**, with **Edit**, **Pause**, **Resume** and **Delete**.

### Create a sequence

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New sequence">
    Give it a **Name** and check the **Channel**: sequences belong to the selected profile. Set the **Status** to **Active** or **Paused**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide when people leave">
    Tick **Stop when someone replies** so a contact who answers leaves the sequence; everyone else keeps receiving it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the messages">
    Click **Add step** for each message. Set its **Day**, the time window (**From** and **To**) and what to **Send as**: a message, or on WhatsApp a pre-approved marketing or utility template, which delivers outside the 24-hour window. For a template, choose it and fill in the **Template variables**. Reorder steps with **Move up** and **Move down**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Check the **Timeline** and click **Create sequence**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Delivery starts when a contact is subscribed with the **Sequence** step in the flow builder. Deleting a sequence means flows that subscribe to it need a new target.

## Growth tools

Open **Growth tools** under **More** (or the **Growth** tab). Each tool belongs to the profile selected at the top of the page. The list shows its clicks, whether it's enabled and which flow it starts.

### Create a growth tool

Click **New growth tool** and fill in:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | For example *Spring campaign link*. |
| **Type** | **WhatsApp link**, **Ref link**, **QR code** or **Website popup**, depending on the channel. |
| **Prefilled message (keyword)** | WhatsApp links prefill this text, so your keyword flows can match it. |
| **Ref parameter** | For ref links on other channels. |
| **Send people to (optional)** | Leave empty to open a chat on this profile, or enter any web page; clicks are still counted. |
| **Starts flow (optional)** | The flow to start, or **No flow — just opens the chat**. |

A **Website popup** also has a **Headline**, **Description**, **Button label**, the **Website origins** it may run on, optional extra consent wording, **Show after (seconds)** and **Remember dismissal (days)**. Visitors leave their number, then open WhatsApp and send your prefilled message.

Click **Create**. Each tool's menu has **Copy link**, **Copy embed code** (pop-ups), **Download QR**, **Design it in QR Codes**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

<Tip>
In the flow builder, a WhatsApp link trigger can start a flow from **A specific link** or **Any of my links**.
</Tip>

## Facebook lead forms

Filling in a Facebook lead form isn't WhatsApp opt-in. The **Facebook lead forms** page lets you decide, per form, whether a yes to the form's WhatsApp consent question counts as opt-in. It's linked from the **Facebook lead form** trigger in the flow builder (*Set up WhatsApp consent per form*).

For each form:

1. Turn on *A yes to this form's WhatsApp consent question is WhatsApp opt-in* (off by default).
2. Under **WhatsApp consent question**, choose the question, or enter its key if no leads have arrived yet. Only a yes answer counts.
3. Choose the **WhatsApp number** the lead is added to, then click **Save**.

A lead who answered yes and gave a phone number can then be sent approved templates. A form appears here once a lead arrives from it or your Facebook Page is reconnected with lead access.
