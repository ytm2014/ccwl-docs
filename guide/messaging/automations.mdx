Flows answer for you. A flow starts when something happens, such as a message with a keyword, a comment on a post, a new tag or a store order, and then runs its steps: send a message, ask a question, let the AI answer, wait, tag the contact, hand over to a person and more.

Open **Flows** in the sidebar (called **Automations** in some layouts). Editing flows needs permission to manage automations; with view-only access you can open a flow but not change it.

## The flows list

Each row shows the **Flow**, where it **Runs on**, its **Type**, **Status**, the **Live** switch, **Runs (30 d)** and **Last run**. Filter by **Type** and **Status**, or use **Search flows**. The row menu has **Open builder** (or **Edit**), **Analytics**, **Test bot**, **Duplicate**, **Export JSON**, **Move to a profile**, **Duplicate to another profile** and **Delete**.

Use **Import** to load a flow from a JSON file; it arrives as a draft. The **Sequences** button opens [sequences](/guide/messaging/sequences-and-growth).

## Create a flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New flow">
    Choose **Which profile should this flow run on?** Pick one profile, or **All profiles** for a flow that reacts to contact, schedule and payment events, or to a message trigger on every profile of a platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a template or start blank">
    Templates are working examples you can change step by step. Filter them by builder, trigger, industry or goal, or choose **Start from scratch**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to build it">
    **Quick automation** is a simple form with one trigger and its replies, best for one-step rules. **Flow builder** is a visual canvas with branches and several steps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quick automations

A quick automation has a **Name**, a **Channel**, a **Trigger**, optional **Conditions** and a list of **Actions**.

- **Trigger**: what starts it, with **Trigger keywords** (comma-separated; leave empty for any message) and, for comments or story replies, **All posts** or **Specific posts**.
- **Conditions**: for example comment text, first-time contact or a tag. Without conditions the actions run every time.
- **Actions**: such as **Reply to comment**, **Reply to Comment with AI**, **Send a DM**, **Send WhatsApp template**, **Like the comment**, **Hide the comment**, **Delete the comment**, **Add a tag**, **Wait**, **Handover to a human** and **Send a message**. Some actions can have up to five versions, rotated so replies don't look automated.
- **Advanced** → **Timing & limits**: **Reply delay**, **Active hours**, **Business hours** and **Only respond once per contact**.

A **Live preview** shows the result. Click **Save flow**. **Edit in Flow Builder** turns it into a visual flow, saved as a draft.

## The flow builder

The canvas starts with the trigger. Click the trigger to see **Start flow when…** and **Change trigger**. Triggers are grouped into the channel's own events, **Contact Events** (such as **New contact**, **Tag applied**, **Field changed**, **Pipeline stage changed**, **Conversation closed**, **Scheduled date**, **Birthday / key date**), **Store events** (Shopify or WooCommerce orders, abandoned checkouts, stock) and **Payment events**.

For message triggers you choose how to match: **Any message (no keyword filter)**, **Message contains a keyword**, **Message is an exact match** or **Message starts with a keyword** (comment triggers can also run on **Only questions**). Other triggers have their own settings, for example **No reply within** a time, outgoing messages **Sent by** an agent or the bot, a **Facebook lead form**, or a WhatsApp link from your growth tools.

### Ad triggers

Where your platform supports them, **Click-to-WhatsApp ad**, **Messenger ad** and **Instagram ad** triggers start a flow when someone messages you from an ad. Under **Which ad?** choose **Any ad** or **Specific ad** and paste the **Ad ID** from Meta Ads Manager. A specific ad takes priority over any-ad and keyword replies, and the ad's details are available in the variable picker. See [Ad referrals](/ad-referrals).

### Add steps

Open **Add a step** and click a tile to add it to the canvas, then connect the dots between steps:

| Tile | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Send message** | Text, media, cards, templates, comment replies |
| **Ask a question** | Quick replies, e-mail, phone, lists, forms |
| **AI reply** | Answers with [AI Knowledge](/guide/messaging/ai-knowledge) and your Brand Voice |
| **Condition** | Tag, field, channel, time, sentiment, label |
| **Wait** | Delay, wait for a reply, business hours |
| **Update contact** | Tag, field, stage, assign, pause bot |
| **Sell** | Payment, product, checkout, order status |
| **Connect** | Webhook, Sheets, Slack, hand over, go to flow, end |

Click a step to edit it in the panel on the right. The toolbar has **Undo** and **Redo**, **History**, and under **More**: **Test on the real channel**, **Arrange**, **Stats** (per-step figures), **Reset view**, **Export flow (.json)** and **Import flow…**. **Test run** lets you try the flow in a preview first.

## Drafts, publishing and going live

Changes in the builder are kept as a draft until you publish:

- **Save draft** keeps your work without changing what runs.
- **Publish** makes the current version the one that runs.
- The **Live** switch (in the builder and on the list) turns the flow on or off.

A flow that has never been published can't go live: you're asked to publish it first. If the builder found problems (**Fix N issues**), you see them before going live and can still choose **Go live anyway**. A flow moved to another profile stays off after the move.

## Flow analytics

Open **Analytics** from the row menu or the builder's **Analytics** tab to see **Runs**, **Completion rate**, **Messages sent**, **Active now**, **Failed** and **Last run**, plus **Recent failed runs** and the flow's **Activity**.

## WhatsApp forms

**WhatsApp Forms** (the **Forms** card under **More**) are native forms that open inside a WhatsApp chat.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a form">
    Pick a starting point under **Create a form**, or click **Sync from Meta** to bring in forms built in Meta's own Flow Builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the fields">
    Give it a **Name** (internal), a **Heading** and a **Submit button** label, then add fields: **Short text**, **Number**, **Long text**, **Dropdown**, **Multiple choice** or **Consent checkbox**, each with a **Question** and **Required** setting. **Save** uploads the form to WhatsApp and refreshes the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it">
    Click **Publish**. Send it from a flow's WhatsApp form step or with **Send a form** in the inbox. If WhatsApp rejects it, open the form to see why.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Don't see **Flows**, ad triggers or **WhatsApp Forms**? Your plan or your platform doesn't include them yet.
</Note>
