A channel is a profile or number the sales inbox sends and receives messages on. Most channels come from the accounts you connect to <AppName /> on the **Social Accounts** page: once a Facebook Page, Instagram account, TikTok account or other profile is connected, it appears in the sales inbox on its own. WhatsApp numbers, Telegram bots, SMS gateways and live chat widgets are added from the same page.

<Note>
Connecting channels needs permission to manage channels. Which channel types you see depends on your plan and your platform.
</Note>

## Add a channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Social Accounts">
    In the sidebar, open **Social Accounts** and click **Add account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the channel">
    Choose a tile: **Facebook Page**, **Instagram**, **WhatsApp** (*Connect a business number*), **Telegram bot (Salesbot)**, **SMS** or **Live Chat**, among others.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the connection">
    Follow the steps for that channel, described below. The new channel then shows in the **Workspace channel** selector on the sales inbox pages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Messenger and Instagram

Connect the Facebook Page or Instagram account as usual and allow every permission Facebook asks for. Messages then reach the inbox automatically. If a profile shows **Reconnect to enable messages**, its connection doesn't let the workspace receive messages yet: reconnect it on **Social Accounts** and allow all permissions.

### WhatsApp

Choosing **WhatsApp** opens the WhatsApp page. Depending on your platform you either click **Connect WhatsApp**, or review **What you need before you get started!** (a phone number, access to your Business Manager and your business documents) and click **Configure** under *Configure WhatsApp via Embedded Signup*.

On the connection screen:

- **Login with Facebook** opens Meta's window, where you choose or create your WhatsApp Business Account and verify the number.
- **Connect Business app number** keeps an eligible number working in the WhatsApp Business app on your phone at the same time. Keep the phone nearby to scan the QR code.

The page lists the requirements: the number must be owned by you, have a country code and be able to receive a code by SMS or voice call. If you're moving from another provider, turn off two-step verification in WhatsApp Business Manager first.

Once connected, the WhatsApp page shows each number with its status. From there you can:

- **Retry** if incoming messages aren't set up yet for a number,
- set a **Default WhatsApp number** for new conversations and notifications (replies always keep the number the customer contacted),
- for a number shared with the Business app, tick **Pause the bot when someone replies from the Business app**,
- **Disconnect** a number. Its conversations are kept, and connecting it again brings it back.

Inside 24 hours of a customer's last message you can reply freely; after that only an approved [WhatsApp template](/guide/messaging/whatsapp-templates) can be sent.

### Telegram

Choose **Telegram bot (Salesbot)**. Create a bot with BotFather in Telegram, paste the **Bot token** it gives you, add an optional **Name** and click **Connect**. This bot answers messages; it's separate from a Telegram channel you post to.

### SMS

Choose **SMS** to connect Twilio, Plivo or a custom SMS gateway. Enter the **API credentials** and click **Connect**, then copy the **Inbound & delivery webhook** URL into your gateway's dashboard so replies reach the inbox and delivery reports update. The page has a setup guide for each gateway. Custom gateways can only send: no inbound replies or delivery reports. Under **Delivery settings** you can add an opt-out line to SMS broadcasts and check failed numbers with a line type lookup.

### Live chat on your website

Choose **Live Chat**, give the widget an optional name and click **Create widget**. The customizer opens (see [Live chat widget](#live-chat-widget)).

## The Channels page

On some platforms the sales inbox also has a **Channels** page listing **Connected channels** with their state (**Connected**, **Needs reconnecting**, **Missing a permission**). Use **Rename** to change what the workspace calls a channel (nothing changes on the platform) or **Disconnect** to stop messaging on it; the account itself keeps publishing. Profiles connected before the sales inbox was switched on are listed separately; reconnecting them adds them.

## Set up each channel's bot

Open **Channel settings** (under **More**, or **Settings** → **Bot setup**), then pick the channel at the top of the page.

### Facebook and Instagram

The **Messages** tab has:

- **Welcome message**: turn on *Send a welcome message when someone taps Get Started*, write the **Message** and add up to three **Buttons** (**Postback**, **Open URL** or **Start flow**).
- **Ice breakers**: up to four suggested questions for first-time visitors, each answered with a **Text reply** or by starting a flow. Instagram shows them on mobile only.
- **Advanced** → **Default reply**: *Reply automatically when no keyword or flow matches*, sent **Every time** or **Once per 24 hours per contact**.

The **Persistent menu** tab adds a menu to the conversation. A **Live preview** shows how a first-time visitor sees the bot. Click **Save changes**.

### Telegram

Telegram has the same sections, adapted: the welcome message is sent when someone sends `/start`, ice breakers appear as buttons under it, and the **Command menu** turns each item into a `/command` on your bot. Under **Danger zone**, **Disconnect bot** removes the channel with its conversations; the bot itself stays in BotFather.

### WhatsApp

Where available, a WhatsApp channel's settings have tabs:

| Tab | What you manage |
| --- | --- |
| **Configuration** | Number info (quality, messaging limit, display name status), **Register phone number** after a display name change, **Click-to-WhatsApp purchase reporting**, links to Meta's payment, verification and WhatsApp Manager pages, **Disconnect**. |
| **Profile** | Business profile photo, **About**, **Description**, **Category**, **Email**, **Address**, two websites. |
| **Commerce** | **Show catalog** and **Enable cart**. |
| **Compliance** | India commerce compliance details. |
| **Message Templates** | A link to your templates. |
| **Conversational Components** | Up to four **Ice breakers**, a welcome message, and up to 30 slash **Commands**. |

### Live chat widget

The widget page has a **Customizer** with these sections: **Widget** (name), **Design** (**Classic** or **Home** version), **Brand** (accent colour, agent name, greeting), **Layout** (header, channels, dock side, greeting teaser, spacing), **Channels** (links to chat on other apps), **Behavior** (online status, typing indicator, status, launcher and offline texts, **Require email before chat**, up to six quick replies) and **Where it appears** (page rules, devices, **Show after (seconds)**, **Show on exit intent**).

Under **Install**, click **Copy snippet** and paste it before `</body>` on every page that should show the widget. Messages land in the inbox and run your flows. **Retire widget** stops new chats but keeps conversations; a retired widget can then **Erase widget data** for good.

Tags and custom fields are shared by every channel: manage tags from **Contacts**, and custom fields (admins) on the workspace **Settings** page.

<Tip>
Messages that come from click-to-message ads can start flows and are reported per ad. See [Ad referrals](/ad-referrals).
</Tip>
