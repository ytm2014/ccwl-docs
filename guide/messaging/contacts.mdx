**Contacts** holds everyone who has messaged your channels, plus anyone you add or import. People who write to you appear here on their own, and one contact can be reached on several channels: replies, history and details are shared across them.

Open **Contacts** in the sidebar (or the **Contacts** tab). Three views sit at the top: **List**, **Board** (the pipeline) and **Segments**. The **More contact actions** menu has **Import**, **Export**, **Manage tags**, **Opted-out contacts** and **Recently deleted**.

<Note>
Each action needs the matching permission in your team role, for example to create, edit, merge, delete or bulk-delete contacts. Buttons you can't use are hidden.
</Note>

## The contact list

Search with **Search by keyword**, or open **Filters** to narrow by **Segment**, **Stage**, **Tags**, **Status** and **Channel**. The table shows each **Contact**, **Stage**, **Tags**, **Channel**, **Status** and **Last seen**. A line under the header shows how much of your plan's contact limit you've used; on some plans only contacts active in the last few days count.

Each row's actions menu has **View details**, **Edit**, **Delete** and, with permission, **Erase permanently**.

### Bulk actions

Tick contacts (or **Select all** matching) to:

- **Add tag** or **Remove tag**,
- **Move to stage**,
- **Merge selected** when exactly two are ticked: the first is kept and the other's channels, conversations, notes and tags move into it,
- delete them.

## Add or edit a contact

Click **New contact**. Fill in **Name** (required), **Phone** (with country code), **Channel**, **Email**, **Tags** (type a new name and press Enter to create one), **Stage**, **Notes** and any **Custom fields** (**Add field**, then a name and value). For WhatsApp, tick *This contact agreed to receive WhatsApp messages from us* if they did. Click **Create**.

Some roles see phone and email hidden (*Hidden by your role*).

## The contact profile

Click a contact to open their profile. The header has **Send message** (opens the conversation in the inbox), **Edit profile** and the **More contact actions** menu (**Pause bot reply** / **Resume bot reply**, **Delete contact**, **Erase permanently**). Below are **Messages exchanged**, **Tags applied** and **Last interaction**.

Tabs:

- **Overview**: the last inbound message and the answers the contact gave in your flows.
- **Activity**: a timeline of what happened.
- **Notes**: team notes. Type `@` to mention a teammate.

The side panel shows the stage, contact details, channels, tags and custom fields, plus:

- a **Bot active** switch to pause or resume automated replies,
- a promotions switch: turn off promotions and the contact stops getting promotional broadcasts, sequences and automated marketing templates, while service messages and your replies still go,
- **Channels**, listing every channel identity with the primary one marked,
- **Privacy**: **Download all data** (a ZIP for a data-access request) and the **Consent history**,
- **Possible duplicates**: contacts sharing a phone or email, with **Merge**. Merging can't be undone.

## Tags and custom fields

Tags are shared by every channel. Manage them from **More contact actions** → **Manage tags**, or add them on a contact or in the inbox. Custom fields are also shared; a workspace admin manages them on the workspace **Settings** page.

## Import contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the import page">
    Choose **More contact actions** → **Import**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a channel">
    Pick the **Channel** to import into, or **No channel** to add them as records only. For WhatsApp, tick *These contacts agreed to receive WhatsApp messages from us*; without it no WhatsApp number is linked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the CSV">
    Use **Download sample** for the expected columns, then drop your file under **Upload CSV**. Check the **Preview** of the first rows and click **Import**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The pipeline board

**Board** shows contacts as cards in stage columns. Drag a card to move it to another stage, or use its menu (**View**, **Edit**, **Move to stage**, **Delete**). The board shows the most recently active contacts; use the list to find everyone.

To change stages, use **Add stage** (a **Stage name**, a colour and **Counts as**: **In progress**, **Won** or **Lost**) or a column's menu: **Edit stage**, **Move left**, **Move right**, **Delete**. Deleting a stage moves its contacts to **No stage**. **View report** opens the [pipeline report](/guide/messaging/reports-and-settings#pipeline).

## Segments

Segments are saved audiences you can target with [broadcasts](/guide/messaging/broadcasts). Open **Segments** and click **New segment**.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Segment name** | Required. |
| **Contact stage** | Limit to one stage, or **Any stage**. |
| **Contact tags** | Contacts with all the selected tags are included. |
| **Exclude contacts with tags** | Contacts with any of these tags are removed. |
| **Number of days since last activity** | Only contacts inactive for at least this many days. |
| **Conditions** | Extra rules in groups. Choose whether contacts must match **all** or **any** of the groups, and within each group all or any of its conditions (a field, an operator and a value). |

The **Preview** shows how many contacts match. Click **Create**.

## Opted-out contacts

**More contact actions** → **Opted-out contacts** lists blocked and unsubscribed contacts, who get no automated messages. Use the **All suppressed**, **Blocked** and **Unsubscribed** tabs and search. **Unblock** a blocked contact, or **Resubscribe** someone only after they asked to receive messages again: you confirm *This person asked to receive messages again* and can note how they asked.

## Recently deleted

Deleted contacts can be restored for 30 days. **More contact actions** → **Recently deleted** lists them with when they expire. **Restore** brings back the contact record; deleted files stay erased and automations stay stopped. With permission, **Erase permanently** removes the recovery copy for good.
