WhatsApp only lets you send a free-form message within 24 hours of the customer's last message. Outside that window, and for every WhatsApp broadcast, you need a template that Meta has approved. The templates page lets you bring in templates from Meta, start from ready-made copy, or build your own and submit it for review.

Open **Message templates** under **More** (in the WhatsApp group), or **WhatsApp templates** from the sidebar or the **Broadcasts** page. Pick a WhatsApp profile in the channel selector first: templates belong to a WhatsApp number.

<Note>
Creating and editing templates needs the matching permission and isn't available on every platform. Without it you can still see and send approved templates.
</Note>

## The templates list

The page has two tabs: **Your templates** and **System** (templates provided for you). Use **Search templates** and **Sort By** to find one. Each row shows the **Name**, **Category**, **Language** and **Status**. A rejected template shows Meta's reason.

Header buttons:

- **Refresh templates** syncs approved templates and review status from Meta.
- **Browse library** opens the copy library.
- **Add template** starts a new one.

Row actions include **Submit to WhatsApp**, **Edit to fix** (for a rejected template), **View performance** (sent, delivered, read, replied and failed over the last 30 days, with recent failed sends), **Preview**, **Edit**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**. A system template you've edited is marked **Customized**; **Reset to default** restores the product's wording and re-submits it.

## Create a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add template">
    Fill in the **Template name** (lowercase letters, numbers and underscores only), the **Category** (**Marketing**, **Utility** or **Authentication**) and the **Supported language**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    For marketing, pick the **Type of marketing message**: **Custom Message** (text, media and buttons), **Catalog Message** (products from your connected catalog), **Carousel Message** (2 to 10 swipeable cards) or, where available, **Limited-time offer** (an offer line, optional countdown and a coupon code).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the content">
    Add an optional header (**Text**, **Image**, **Video** or **Document**; media needs a sample for Meta's review), the **Body**, and an optional **Footer**. Use **Add variable** to personalise the message, and enter a sample value for each variable so Meta can review it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add buttons">
    Choose **Quick reply**, **Visit website**, **Call phone number** or **Copy code**. Meta limits how many of each you can add, and the page tells you when you reach the limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Check the **Preview** and click **Save** to submit it. The status changes once Meta has reviewed it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Authentication templates have fixed content. You choose how customers send the code to your app, and can add a security recommendation, an expiry time for the code and a custom validity period.

## The template copy library

**Browse library** opens ready-made marketing copy written against Meta's review rules. Search it or filter by industry and occasion, then choose **Customize** to edit it in the builder or **Submit as-is** to send it for review exactly as written.

## Use your templates

- In the [inbox](/guide/messaging/inbox), use the template button in the reply box, or **Choose template** when the 24-hour window is closed.
- In [broadcasts](/guide/messaging/broadcasts) and [sequences](/guide/messaging/sequences-and-growth), only approved templates are listed.
- In [flows](/guide/messaging/automations), use the WhatsApp template step or action.

<Warning>
If WhatsApp refuses submissions for your business account, the page says so. Check the account and any restrictions in WhatsApp Manager; changing the wording may not help.
</Warning>
