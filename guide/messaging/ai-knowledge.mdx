AI Knowledge is what the AI answers from when it replies to your customers. You add sources (files, web pages, FAQs or pasted text), group them into collections, and point any **AI reply** step in your [flows](/guide/messaging/automations) at them. The inbox's AI suggestions use it too.

Open **AI Knowledge** under **More** (Shortcuts) or next to **Flows**. On some layouts admins find it in the workspace settings under **AI**.

<Note>
AI Knowledge needs a knowledge-capable AI provider. If the page says so, connect your own OpenAI or Gemini key under **Integrations**. Knowledge is private to your workspace.
</Note>

## Create a collection

Collections group related sources, for example *Support*, *Products* or *Policies*. Under **New collection**, enter a **Collection name** and click **Add collection**. Each collection shows how many sources it has and how many automations use it.

## Add sources

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add source">
    In the collection, click **Add source** and choose the **Source type**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    | Source type | What to enter |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Upload file (PDF / TXT / MD)** | A file up to 20 MB. |
    | **Web page URL** | The page address; its readable text is fetched and indexed. |
    | **FAQ (question & answer)** | A **Question** and its **Answer**. |
    | **Pasted text** | An optional **Title** and the text. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The source may show as processing for a moment while it's indexed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use the search box to find sources by name or address, and **Edit** or **Remove** them. Editing an FAQ or text source re-indexes it.

## Import many pages at once

Click **Import pages** and choose the **Source**:

- **Sitemap (imports the pages it lists)**: enter the **Sitemap address** (many sites publish one at `/sitemap.xml`).
- **Paste a list of page addresses**: one per line.

Up to 100 pages are imported per run, and pages already in the collection are skipped. For a bigger site, run **Import** again with the same address and it carries on from where it stopped. Each page keeps its address, so the AI can link back to it.

## Test what the AI finds

Under **Test knowledge**, pick a collection (or **All collections**), type a question and click **Test**. **Retrieved snippets** shows what the AI would answer from. If the answer is missing, add or improve a source.

## Delete a collection

**Delete collection** removes it and all its sources from the AI provider. AI steps that searched it answer without knowledge afterwards. This can't be undone.
