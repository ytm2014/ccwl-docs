The inbox collects direct messages from every connected channel in one list, so your team can answer them in <AppName /> without switching apps. Each conversation opens with the full thread in the middle and the contact's details on the right. On some platforms the inbox also has an **Engagements** tab for comments, mentions and reviews on your posts.

Open **Inbox** (or **Unified Inbox**) in the sidebar. A red count next to it shows unread conversations.

<Note>
Your team role decides what you can do here. With read-only access the reply box says *Your role lets you read conversations but not reply to them.*
</Note>

## The conversation list

The header shows how many conversations are open. Next to it are a bell to mute or unmute the new-message sound, **AI settings** (where available) and the gear menu (**Inbox settings**) with **Conversation rules** and **Saved replies**.

At the top of the list:

- **DMs** and **Engagements** tabs, when your platform has both, each with an unread count.
- A status menu: **Open**, **Done**, **Archived**, **Spam** or **All**.
- The search icon (**Search Conversations**).
- Quick filters: **Mine**, **Unassigned**, **AI handling** and **Waiting on customer**.
- Where comments share the list with messages, a switch between **DMs** and **Comments**.

The **Filter** button opens more filters: **Channel**, **Platform**, **Assigned to**, **Label** (your contact tags), **Sentiment** and **AI label** (when AI insights are on) and **Unread only**. Use **Clear filter** to reset them.

<Tip>
Turn on AI insights from the banner above the list to have every DM and comment labelled and rated, so you can filter by mood or by labels such as question, complaint or lead. Insights use your workspace's AI credits.
</Tip>

## Reply to a conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Click it in the list. The thread shows messages, replies to specific messages, and who on your team is typing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check where the reply goes">
    If the contact can be reached on more than one channel or number, choose it under **Reply on** or **Send from** above the reply box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and send">
    Type your message and press Enter (Shift+Enter adds a new line), or click **Send**. To answer one message in particular, use **Reply** on it; **Cancel reply** removes the link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The toolbar under the reply box offers, depending on the channel:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Attach image / video**, **Attach file** | Send media or a document. |
| **Record a voice note** | Record in the browser and send it as audio. |
| **WhatsApp text formatting** | Bold, italic, strikethrough or monospace on WhatsApp. |
| **WhatsApp template** | Pick an approved template and fill in its values. |
| **Send a form** | Send one of your [WhatsApp forms](/guide/messaging/automations#whatsapp-forms). |
| **Saved replies** | Insert a saved answer, or create one with **New saved reply**. |
| **Insert booking link** | Send a link to one of your [appointment types](/guide/messaging/meetings). |
| **Suggest a reply with AI** | Draft a reply using your Brand Voice and [AI Knowledge](/guide/messaging/ai-knowledge). |
| **Request a payment** | Send a payment link through a connected payment gateway. |

If a message can't be sent, the reason appears under the reply box and your draft is kept.

### The WhatsApp 24-hour window

On WhatsApp you can reply freely for 24 hours after the customer's last message. After that the reply box tells you the window is closed and offers **Choose template**: only an approved template can be sent until the customer writes again. See [WhatsApp templates](/guide/messaging/whatsapp-templates).

## Add an internal note

Switch the reply box from **Reply** to **Note**, type the note and click **Add note**. Only your team sees notes. Type `@` to mention a teammate. Notes also appear under **Notes** in the contact panel, where you can edit or delete them.

## Manage the conversation

The thread header shows the contact and these controls:

- **Assign**: choose a teammate or **Unassigned**.
- **Pause the bot** / **Resume the bot**: stop or restart automated replies for this contact. The header shows **AI on** or **AI off**.
- **More actions**: **Mark as unread**, **Mark as done** / **Reopen conversation**, **Mark as spam** / **Not spam**, **Block contact** / **Unblock contact**, **Archive conversation** / **Unarchive conversation**, **Refresh** and **Delete permanently**.

Badges such as **Spam**, **Hate speech** and **Blocked** appear next to the name. A blocked contact's thread says so and offers **Unblock contact**. Deleting a conversation removes the thread and its messages for good. On some platforms archived conversations are deleted automatically after 30 days, and the thread tells you.

### When you take over from an automation

Depending on your [conversation rules](/guide/messaging/reports-and-settings#conversation-rules), replying yourself can pause a running flow. The thread then shows **Flow paused** with three choices:

- **Resume**: hand the conversation back; the automation carries on from the same step.
- **Skip step**: you answered that step yourself, so continue from the next one.
- **End flow**: you finished it manually, so end the automation.

If you mark a conversation as done while a flow is still paused, you're asked what should happen to it first.

## The contact panel

The right-hand panel shows the contact's **Stage**, **Owner**, **E-mail**, **Phone**, **Channels**, **Orders** and **First seen**. From here you can:

- change the stage,
- add or remove **Tags** (**Add tag**, **Edit tags**),
- get a **Suggested reply** from AI and **Use** it, try **Another**, or **Try again**,
- add **Notes** for your team,
- **Open contact** to see the full profile in [Contacts](/guide/messaging/contacts).

## Saved replies

Open the gear menu and choose **Saved replies** to manage answers your team sends often. Each has a **Title**, a **Message** and an optional **Shortcut**. Insert them from the saved replies button in the reply box.

## Engagements

Where the **Engagements** tab is available, it lists comments, mentions and reviews from your connected profiles. You can reply publicly, use **Send as DM** to answer privately where the platform allows it, **Send & resolve**, like, **Hide** or **Unhide** a comment, assign it, block the author, and **Resolve** or **Reopen** the conversation. The panel on the right shows the author, their mood and the AI insight, and lets you add them to your contacts.
