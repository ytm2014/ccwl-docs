Everyone who works in a workspace is a member of it, with a role that decides what they can do there. Admins manage members on the **Team** page. A person can belong to several workspaces with a different role in each; their profile and password stay the same everywhere.

## Open the Team page

The Team page is for workspace admins. In the sidebar, open **Settings** > **Team Members**. Inside workspace settings, it is the **Team** entry of the settings menu. Admins may also find **Team Members** in the user menu (top right).

If the sales inbox is part of your workspace and you can manage roles, the page has two tabs: **Members** and **Roles**. Roles are described in [Roles and permissions](/guide/account/roles-and-permissions).

The **Members** panel lists everyone in the workspace and every invitation that hasn't been accepted yet:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The person's name, or a **Pending** badge for an invitation. Hover the information icon on a pending row to see who sent it and when. |
| **Email** | Their email address. |
| **Role** | **Admin**, **Member** or **Viewer**, or **Owner** for the person who owns the workspace. A badge such as **Limited to 2 channels** shows when their access is limited to some channels. |
| **Can approve** | **Yes** if they can approve posts that need approval. |
| **Joined At** | When they joined. Pending invitations show a dash. |

## Invite someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation dialog">
    On the Team page, click **Invite member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email address">
    Type the address in **Email**. The invitation is sent there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a role">
    Choose **Admin** (can access and edit everything), **Member** (can access and edit everything except the configuration) or **Viewer** (can access everything except the configuration, but cannot edit or schedule posts). See [Roles and permissions](/guide/account/roles-and-permissions) for the details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their access">
    For a member or viewer, choose what else they can do. The dialog shows the choices your workspace uses (see below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Click **Invite**. You see **Member invited successfully.** and the person appears in the list as **Pending**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The person gets an email. When they open it and sign in (or sign up), they see **Join … workspace** with the name of whoever invited them, and click **Accept** or **Decline**. Once they accept, their row shows their name and **Joined At** date.

### Access options in the dialog

Depending on what your workspace includes, the dialog shows one of these sets of options.

**Individual options.** Tick what the person needs:

| Option | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| **Can Approve** | Approve posts that are waiting for approval. |
| **Inbox Access** | Open the inbox and work on conversations. |
| **Bulk Posts Access** | Use **Bulk Posts**. |
| **RSS Feeds Access** | Use **RSS Campaigns**. |

If the sales inbox is part of your workspace you can also pick a **Team role**, which replaces the Inbox, Bulk Posts and RSS options, and tick channels under **Salesbot channels** or **Channels (posting and Salesbot)** to limit the person to those channels. Tick no channel to give them every channel.

**A custom role.** In workspaces where team roles decide access, the dialog has a **Custom role** list instead. A member with a role gets exactly that role's permissions. A member without a role gets no Inbox, Bulk Posts, RSS or post approval access, so choose a role to grant them. If no role exists yet, the dialog offers a link to create one. On a plan without team roles, an **Inbox Access** box is shown instead.

Admins always have every permission, so these options don't apply to them.

### Members limit

Your plan may limit how many people can work in the workspace. When the limit is reached, the invitation is refused with **Members limit reached** and the limit. Remove a member or change your plan; see [Usage and plan limits](/guide/billing/usage-and-limits).

## Cancel an invitation

Open the menu at the end of the pending row, click **Cancel**, and confirm. The invitation is withdrawn. You can invite the same address again later. Inviting an address that already has a pending invitation shows **This member is already invited.**, and inviting someone who is already in the workspace shows **This user is already a member of this workspace.**

## Change someone's role or access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu on their row">
    Click the menu at the end of the person's row, then **Edit role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    The **Edit role for …** dialog has the same choices as the invitation. If the person has no custom role, it tells you which access they keep today. Saving with a role replaces their own channel limit with the role's channels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update**. You see **Role updated**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can't change your own role or the owner's: those rows have no menu.

## Remove someone

Open the menu on their row, click **Remove**, and confirm **Detach user**. They lose access to this workspace straight away. Their account is not deleted, and they keep any other workspaces they belong to. The owner can't be removed.

## Common questions

**The person never got the invitation email.**
Check the address on the pending row. If it's wrong, cancel the invitation and send a new one. Ask them to look in their spam folder.

**Can a member invite people?**
No. The Team page is for admins only.

**Someone left the company. Do their posts disappear when I remove them?**
Removing a member only takes away their access to the workspace.
