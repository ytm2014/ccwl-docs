Your profile belongs to you, not to a workspace. Your name, sign-in email, password and preferences are the same in every workspace you belong to, and changing them never affects your teammates.

## Open your profile

Click your avatar in the top-right corner to open your user menu, then click **My Settings**.

The profile area has its own menu on the left:

| Item | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **My Settings** | Profile information, preferences, two-factor authentication, password and account deletion. |
| **Theme** | A choice of look for the dashboard, where your platform offers more than one theme. |
| **Workspaces** | Every workspace you belong to, with your role in each. See [Workspaces](/guide/getting-started/workspaces). |
| **API**, **Integrations** | Your personal API keys and the AI tools connected to your account. See [API keys](/guide/integrations/api-keys). |
| **Connected apps** | Apps you allowed to sign in to your account. See [Password and two-factor](/guide/account/security#connected-apps). |
| **Agency** | Your reseller console, if you run an agency on the platform. See [Agency console](/guide/account/agency-console). |

Items your platform doesn't offer are not shown.

## Profile Information

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Avatar** | Your picture, shown in your user menu, on the Team page and next to your replies. |
| **Name** | The name your team sees. |
| **Email** | The address you sign in with and receive emails at. |

Change what you need and click **Save**.

### Assignment alerts

If the sales inbox can alert you about conversations assigned to you, an **Assignment alerts** panel appears under your profile information. Enter your **WhatsApp number** with its country code (for example +44 20 1234 5678) and click **Save**.

The number alone sends nothing. Turn the alerts on in each workspace from the notifications bell: see [Notifications](/guide/account/notifications#when-a-conversation-is-assigned-to-me).

## My Preferences

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Language** | The language of the dashboard. |
| **Timezone** | The time zone used for scheduling and for every date and time you see. |
| **Time format** | **12 hour** or **24 hour**. |
| **First day of week** | **Sunday** or **Monday**, used by the calendar. |
| **Appearance** | **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your device's light or dark mode. |

Click **Save** to apply them.

<Tip>
Check **Timezone** before you schedule your first posts. Posts go out at the time you pick in your own time zone, so a wrong zone moves every post.
</Tip>

You can also switch between light and dark quickly from your user menu.

## Theme

Where your platform offers several themes, **Theme** lets you pick the one you see. Only your own view changes; your teammates keep theirs. The platform's default theme is marked **(default)**. Choose one and click **Save**.

## Delete your account

Where your platform allows it, a **Delete Account** panel appears at the bottom of **My Settings**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **Delete Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the warning">
    Deleting your account permanently removes all your account data, including the data of every workspace you own. Any active subscription on the workspaces you own is cancelled immediately, and you are not charged again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm with **Delete Account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
This cannot be undone. If other people work in a workspace you own, they lose it too. To leave a workspace someone else owns without deleting your account, use **Workspaces** instead.
</Warning>

## Common questions

**I changed my email address. How do I sign in now?**
Use the new address with your existing password.

**Why does a teammate see a different time for the same post?**
Each person sees times in their own **Timezone**. The post still goes out at one moment.

**Can I set my profile per workspace?**
No. Your profile is shared by all your workspaces. What changes per workspace is your role and, in the sales inbox, your notification settings.
