Your password and two-factor authentication protect every workspace you belong to, so they live on your own profile rather than in a workspace. Open your user menu (your avatar, top right) and click **My Settings**; the security panels are below your preferences.

## Change your password

The **Update Password** panel asks for:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Current password** | The password you sign in with today. |
| **New password** | The new one. Use a long, random password you don't use anywhere else. |
| **Confirm new password** | The new password again. |

Click **Save**. You see **Password have been changed** when it worked; if the current password is wrong or the two new ones don't match, the field shows the error.

<Tip>
If your platform lets you sign in with an identity provider instead of a password, you may be asked to sign in again with that provider before a sensitive change, instead of typing a password.
</Tip>

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication adds a code from an authenticator app to your password each time you sign in. Someone who learns your password still can't get in without your phone.

The **Two Factor Authentication** panel only appears if your platform offers it. Its badge shows **Enabled** or **Disabled**.

### Turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install an authenticator app">
    Install a TOTP-compatible authentication app, such as Google Authenticator, on your phone or computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Enable">
    If you haven't confirmed your password recently, you are asked for it first ("For your security, please confirm your password to continue").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the account to your app">
    Scan the QR code with your app. If you can't scan it, type **Your secret key** into the app instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with a code">
    Type the **Code** your app shows and click **Confirm**. If the code is refused, check that your phone's clock is set automatically and try the next code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery codes">
    The panel lists recovery codes. Store them in a secure password manager. Each one gets you back into your account if you lose the device with your authenticator app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From then on, signing in asks for a code after your password.

### Recovery codes

While two-factor authentication is on, the panel offers:

- **Show recovery codes** to see your current codes again.
- **Regenerate recovery codes** to replace them, for example after you used some or think someone saw them. The old codes stop working.

### Turn it off

Click **Disable** (you may be asked to confirm your password). Your account goes back to password-only sign-in. You can turn it on again at any time; you'll scan a new QR code.

<Warning>
If you lose your device and your recovery codes, you can't pass the code step yourself. Contact your platform's support.
</Warning>

## Connected apps

**Connected apps**, in the profile menu, lists the apps you allowed to sign in to your account, such as ChatGPT or Claude, with the date each was **Connected**. An app connected this way acts as you, with your role in each workspace.

To remove one, click **Disconnect** on its row and confirm. Its access ends straight away. You can connect it again later from the app itself.

If you have connected nothing, the page says that apps you connect with sign-in appear there. Don't see **Connected apps**? Your platform doesn't offer it.

## API keys

Personal API keys also act as you. They are listed under **API** in the profile menu. See [API keys](/guide/integrations/api-keys) and [Authentication](/authentication).

## Who can see what you do

Roles decide what each person can do in a workspace, and the sales inbox keeps an [audit log](/guide/analytics/audit-log) of admin and destructive actions, including API keys created and revoked. See [Roles and permissions](/guide/account/roles-and-permissions).

## Common questions

**I'm not asked for a code when I sign in.**
Two-factor authentication is off for your account, or your platform doesn't offer it. Check the badge on the panel.

**Does turning on two-factor authentication affect my teammates?**
No. It's per person. Each teammate turns it on for their own account.
