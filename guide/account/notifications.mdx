The bell at the top right of the dashboard collects what happened in the current workspace that concerns you: a post waiting for your approval, a social account that needs reconnecting, a conversation assigned to you, a plan limit almost reached. Each person chooses their own notifications, separately in each workspace.

## The bell

Click the bell to open **Notifications**, newest first. A number on the bell shows how many you haven't read.

- Filter by section: **All**, **Posts**, **Accounts**, **Team**, **Billing**, **Jobs** and **Salesbot**. Only sections your workspace uses appear.
- Click a notification to open what it is about.
- Use an item's menu (**More actions**) to **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**, or, on a post notification, **Mute this post**. You still hear about mentions on a muted post.
- Click **Mark all read** to clear the count.
- Click **Settings** to open **Notification settings**.

When there's nothing to show, the panel says **Nothing new. You are all caught up.**

## Notification settings

Open the bell and click **Settings**. Your choices apply to you in this workspace only; repeat them in each workspace you belong to.

### In-app notifications

**In-app notifications** is the master switch for your bell in this workspace. Turning it off mutes every event below without losing which ones you picked.

### Events

Events are grouped, and each has a switch for the bell. The events you see depend on what your workspace includes and on your role:

| Group | Events |
| --- | --- |
| **Posts** | Post waiting for approval; Post approved; Post not approved; You were mentioned on a post; New comment on a post; Post published; Post failed to publish; Post still waiting for approval; Approval needed soon |
| **Social accounts** | Social account needs reconnecting; Social account connection expiring (within 7 days, when it can't renew itself); Move your Instagram connection; Scheduled posts running low |
| **Team** | New team member; Your channel access changed |
| **Plan and billing** | Plan limit almost reached; Plan limit reached; Trial ending soon; Trial ended; Payment failed; Subscription ended |
| **Imports and background jobs** | Bulk post import finished; Account import finished; Bulk contact update finished |
| **Inbox** | New conversation; Conversation assigned to you; You were mentioned (in a reply or internal note) |
| **Automation** | Automation failed |
| **Account** | Approaching your contacts limit (90%); Contacts limit reached; WhatsApp Business app sync failing |
| **Payments** | Payment received; Payment held for checking |

Billing events go to admins.

### Email and desktop choices

Where your platform offers them, each event row also has an **Email** choice and, once desktop notifications are on, a **Push** switch:

| Email choice | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Default** | Keeps the emails you get today. |
| **Each time** | An extra email every time the event happens. |
| **Daily summary** | Collects the event into one email a day. |
| **Off** | Stops the emails for that event. |

If any event is set to **Daily summary**, a **Daily email summary** section lets you choose when it is sent (**Send at**), in your own time zone.

### Desktop notifications

**Desktop notifications** sends a notification to this computer for every event you switch **Push** on for, even when the tab is in the background.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn them on">
    Click **Turn on desktop notifications** and allow notifications when your browser asks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test">
    Click **Send a test**. You should see "Desktop notifications work".
  </Step>
</Steps>

The section says **On in this browser** or **Off in this browser**; it's set per browser. Click **Turn off** to stop them here. If your browser blocked notifications for the site, allow them in the browser's site settings and try again.

### Quiet hours

Set **Quiet hours** with **From** and **To**, then click **Save quiet hours**. During those hours you get no desktop notifications, and **Each time** emails wait until the quiet hours end. The bell still records everything. The two times must be different.

### Daily digest

Switch on **Daily digest** for one notification a day summarising what is waiting for you, and choose **Send at**. The time zone is detected from your browser and saved with your settings.

### When a conversation is assigned to me

In workspaces with the sales inbox, this section has:

- **Email me**: an email when a conversation is assigned to you.
- **Send me a WhatsApp message**: a WhatsApp alert, where the workspace can send one. It uses the workspace's default WhatsApp number and an approved assignment template, so delivery depends on both. Add your own number first under **Assignment alerts** on your profile; the dialog warns you if it's missing.

Click **Save** to keep your changes. You see **Notification settings saved**.

## Common questions

**I don't get emails for some events.**
Check the event's **Email** choice. Not every event offers email.

**My teammate gets a notification I don't.**
Choices are per person and per workspace, some events only go to the people involved (for example the post's author or watchers, or the assignee), and some only to admins.

**The settings say they couldn't load.**
Close the dialog and open it again from the bell. If it keeps failing, reload the page.
