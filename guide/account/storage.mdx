Your plan includes an amount of storage for the files you keep in the workspace. Depending on your platform, that storage covers the images and videos in your media library only, or the media library and the files that arrive in the inbox (attachments and voice notes) together. The **Inbox storage** page shows where the space goes and lets you free some up.

## Check your storage

- **Usage**, in the workspace settings menu, lists your storage with your plan's limit next to your other plan resources. See [Usage and plan limits](/guide/billing/usage-and-limits).
- **Inbox storage**, in the same menu under **Billing**, appears when inbox files count toward your storage. It is for workspace admins.

Don't see **Inbox storage**? On your platform, inbox files don't count toward your storage.

To open it, in the sidebar open **Settings** > **Subscription**, then click **Inbox storage** in the settings menu.

## Storage used

The **Storage used** panel shows the space used against your limit (or **Unlimited**), split into **Media library** and **inbox**. The bar turns orange from 80% and red when storage is full.

## Keep inbox files for

Choose how long attachments and voice notes are kept. Files older than your choice are deleted every night; the messages themselves stay in the conversation.

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Keep every file** | Nothing is deleted automatically. |
| **Delete files older than 30 days** (or 60, 90, 180, 365 days) | Older files are removed each night. |

Pick one and click **Save**. You see **Inbox storage setting saved.** Your platform may set the longest period you can choose.

## Free up inbox storage

The **Free up inbox storage** panel lists the conversations holding the most inbox files, with how many files and how much space each uses. Click **Delete files** on a conversation and confirm to remove its files at once; the messages stay. You see how much was deleted, for example "Deleted 12 MB of inbox files."

If no conversation has files yet, the panel says **No conversation holds inbox files yet.**

## When storage is full

- New uploads to the media library are refused once the storage limit is reached.
- A file that arrives in the inbox while storage is full may not be kept. In the conversation you then see **Your storage is full, so this file was not kept.**, or, if it was kept anyway, a note asking you to free up space. A voice note you record isn't saved and you're told so.
- A file removed by your retention setting or by **Delete files** shows **This file was removed to free up storage.**

To get room back, delete files you no longer need, shorten **Keep inbox files for**, add a storage add-on, or move to a plan with more storage. See [Add-ons](/guide/billing/add-ons) and [Change your plan](/guide/billing/changing-your-plan).

<Tip>
Turn on the bell's **Plan limit almost reached** notification to hear about storage before it fills up. See [Notifications](/guide/account/notifications).
</Tip>
