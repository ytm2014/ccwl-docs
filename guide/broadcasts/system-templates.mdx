Some features have to message people on WhatsApp outside the 24-hour window, when only an approved template is allowed. For those, the product ships its own **system templates**. They appear on the templates page under the **System** tab, next to the templates you write yourself under **Your templates**.

To see them, in the sidebar open **Salesbot** > **More** > **Message templates**, select a WhatsApp number, and click the **System** tab.

## The system templates

All system templates are in the **Utility** category and written in English. Their names start with `salesbot_`.

| Template | When it is sent | What it says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `salesbot_conversation_assigned` | A conversation is assigned to a teammate who has a WhatsApp number on their profile. It goes from your workspace's default WhatsApp number to that teammate. | Tells the teammate which contact's conversation was assigned to them and asks them to open the inbox to reply. |
| `salesbot_request_phone` | A flow's request-phone-number step runs for a contact who reached you without a phone number on file, and the 24-hour window has closed. | Asks the contact to reply with their phone number. |
| `salesbot_csat` | A flow's satisfaction survey step runs after the 24-hour window has closed. | Thanks the contact and asks them to rate their experience with five buttons: **5 - Excellent**, **4 - Good**, **3 - Average**, **2 - Fair** and **1 - Poor**. |

If a system template isn't approved on the number, the feature that needs it can't reach people outside the window. The assignment alert, for example, is simply not sent.

## Badges

- **Managed**: the template has the product's wording. When the product updates that wording, the template is updated too.
- **Customized**: someone in your workspace edited the template. It is now yours, and product updates no longer change it.

## Submitting system templates

System templates are added to a number's business account as drafts. Depending on your platform, they are sent to Meta automatically, or you submit them yourself. When automatic submission is off, the **System** tab says: "Automatic submissions are paused. Open a template and save it to submit it manually. Refresh only checks review status."

To submit one yourself:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it">
    On the **System** tab, open the template's **...** menu and click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Click **Save** without changing anything. The template is sent to Meta for review and stays **Managed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the status">
    Click **Refresh templates** later to see whether Meta approved it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customise a system template

You can change a system template's wording, for example to match your tone of voice. Open **Edit**, change the text and click **Save**. The template is submitted again and marked **Customized**.

Keep in mind:

- The variables are filled in by position, so keep the same placeholders in the same order. Moving them around puts values in the wrong place.
- For the satisfaction survey, keep the button texts as they are: the flow reads the rating from the button that was tapped.
- System templates can't contain **Visit website** buttons. The builder doesn't offer them for these templates.

## Reset to default

If a customised template keeps being rejected, or you want the product's wording back, use **Reset to default** in the template's **...** menu. You are asked to confirm. The product's wording replaces yours, the template is submitted to WhatsApp again, and it goes back to **Managed**, so future product updates apply again.

**Reset to default** also appears on a managed template whose wording is older than the product's current version.

## Deleting

System templates can't be deleted: the menu doesn't offer **Delete template** for them, because the feature that sends them would stop working.
