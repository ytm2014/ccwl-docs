Every broadcast has a report that shows what happened to each recipient. Open it by clicking the broadcast in the list, or with **View analytics** in its **...** menu. Anyone who can see broadcasts can read reports, viewers included.

## The header

The report's header shows the broadcast's name, a link back to **Broadcasts**, its status, and **Edit** while it can still be edited. Banners above the numbers tell you when something needs attention:

- **Retargets** with the source broadcast's name, for a retargeting broadcast.
- The reason a broadcast failed, if it did.
- "Waiting for this number's WhatsApp messaging limit" with a count, while part of a WhatsApp audience is held for later days. They are sent automatically as the limit allows.
- "This broadcast is awaiting approval before it can be sent", with **Approve & schedule** if you may approve it.

## The numbers

| Number | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Sent** | Messages handed to the network. |
| **Delivered** | Messages the network reported as delivered. |
| **Read** | Messages the network reported as read. Depends on read receipts: people who turned them off never count as read. |
| **Replies** | Recipients who replied. |
| **Clicks** | Clicks on tracked links. Shown only when the broadcast tracked links. |
| **Failed** | Messages that could not be sent or delivered. |

The same totals, added up for every broadcast on the channel, appear at the top of the broadcasts list.

## Conversion funnel

**Conversion funnel** shows **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Read**, **Replies** and, with tracked links, **Clicks** as bars, each with its count and its share of the messages sent. It shows where people drop off.

## Engagement over time

**Engagement over time** charts the reads and direct replies recorded within 24 hours of each send. A zero means no receipt or reply was recorded. Older sends may lack timestamps.

## Delivery breakdown (Facebook and Instagram)

On Facebook and Instagram, **Delivery breakdown** shows how each person was reached:

- **Sent in the 24h window**: the person had written to you in the last 24 hours.
- **Delivered via opt-in**: reached through a notification opt-in.
- **Skipped — window closed**: neither applied, so the message couldn't be sent.

## Link clicks

When the broadcast tracked links, **Link clicks** lists each **Link** with **People** (how many different recipients clicked it) and **Clicks** (all clicks, including repeats).

## Message

**Message** shows what was sent: the text, or for WhatsApp the template's name with the value used for each variable.

## Failures and skips

**Failures and skips** groups the recipients who failed, were skipped or have no confirmed outcome, by reason, with a count for each. Common reasons:

| Reason shown | What it means and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| No WhatsApp opt-in is recorded for this contact, so marketing templates skip them. | See [audiences](/guide/broadcasts/audiences#contacts-without-a-whatsapp-opt-in). |
| Contact asked WhatsApp to stop marketing messages from this number. | Respect it; the contact can't receive marketing templates from this number. |
| Contact opted out of promotions. | Send a service update instead, if the message really is one. |
| Contact carries the Unsubscribe tag. / Contact opted out of private messages. / Contact is blocked. | Left out by design. |
| Messaging window closed. Use an approved WhatsApp template or an eligible opt-in. | The person hasn't written in the last 24 hours. |
| A template value was blank or missing. | Give every variable a value, or a fallback for per-contact values. |
| Template is not approved for this sending number. | Check the template's status on the templates page. |
| Provider paused or disabled the template. | WhatsApp paused the template, usually for low quality. Use another template. |
| Meta held this back to protect engagement: each person only gets so many marketing messages. | WhatsApp limits how many marketing messages one person receives. Nothing to fix on your side. |
| Provider rate limit reached. Retry later. | Too many messages at once; send to these people later. |
| Sending channel is unavailable. Reconnect it before retrying. | Reconnect the channel in **Accounts**. |
| Landline: this number cannot receive SMS, so it was skipped. | Use another channel for this contact. |
| The broadcast was paused, cancelled or held before this recipient. | The send stopped before reaching them. |
| The provider’s response was not confirmed. It was not retried. | Check the conversation before sending to this person again, so they aren't messaged twice. |
| The workspace plan no longer includes Broadcasts. | The plan changed during the send. See [Billing](/guide/billing/usage-and-limits). |

## Last test

If someone sent a test, **Last test** shows its status, the number it went to, the time and the result.

## Recipients and send times

The table at the bottom lists every recipient with their **Status** and the times their message was **Scheduled**, **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Read** and **Replied**. A failed or skipped recipient also shows the reason. A contact who was deleted since shows as **Deleted contact**.

Use the status menu to show **All recipients** or only one status; each status shows its count. Page through with **Previous** and **Next**. If the list doesn't load, click **Retry**.

<Tip>
To re-message part of a broadcast, for example everyone who read it but didn't reply, or everyone whose send failed, create a new broadcast and choose **Retarget a past broadcast**. See [Choose a broadcast audience](/guide/broadcasts/audiences).
</Tip>

## WhatsApp template performance

To compare templates rather than broadcasts, open the template's performance on the [templates page](/guide/broadcasts/message-templates#template-performance). It counts every send of that template, from broadcasts, flows and the inbox.
